# Player boundary

The parent page and the current table state supply the world. This skill
chooses what the player can receive through the current camera. It does not
invent a second world inside the spoken block.

Read this file when a scene contains hidden truth, automatic knowledge, a
character-specific view, or an unresolved player choice.

## Three information classes

Classify each relevant fact before you draft:

- **Perceivable.** A character can see, hear, smell, touch, or sense it now.
- **Knowable.** A character can recognize it through established expertise,
  culture, background, magic, or prior experience.
- **Hidden.** The fact is true but no character has a legitimate way to know it
  yet.

Most spoken blocks use perceivable facts. State knowable facts plainly when the
character would recognize them. Keep hidden facts in DM notes or a collapsed
`[!secret]-` callout. Do not turn hidden truth into certainty through mood.

Characters perceive. Players interpret. Show evidence, behavior, and physical
consequences. Do not state a conclusion that the evidence leaves for the
players. Do not hide obvious geometry, conspicuous danger, or normal expertise
to manufacture mystery. Mystery comes from incomplete circumstances.

## Player agency

Never write what a PC thinks, feels, wants, decides, intends, believes,
remembers incorrectly, concludes, or chooses to investigate. Do not narrate a
route, trust, surrender, victory, death, or other unresolved outcome. Show the
stimulus and leave the response to the player.

NPC and faction behavior can pursue established goals. The world can change
because of a resolved mechanic or an established pressure. The spoken block
stops at the next meaningful player decision.

If a description gives the table a false mental model, correct it directly.
Communication must not become a hidden challenge.

## Parent, canon, and stock

Read the owning parent before drafting. Established and locked facts remain the
authority. Preserve beliefs as beliefs. Do not promote a possibility to canon
because it makes a cleaner image.

If the parent lacks a signature, first-sight facts, and one usable property,
invoke `fleshing-out-content` before you write the spoken block. Select from the
facts that pass returns. If the parent kind is missing, invoke its owning craft
skill first. Do not add canon inside this prose pass.

The stock pass may add reversible texture. It may not decide hidden truth,
player action, or an unresolved consequence. Keep hidden truth in `[!secret]-`.
