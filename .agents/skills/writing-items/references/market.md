# Market

Three different numbers:

- **Rules value** — rarity baseline, mundane base cost, consumable treatment.
- **Asking price / sale offer** — what this counterparty will transact for.
- **Availability** — whether the item can be obtained here at all.

**Availability first, price second.** A high enough purse does not
make a Legendary item appear in an ordinary town.

Transaction terms come from:

- rarity and the local magical economy
- current demand and buyer specialization
- condition, charges, missing pieces, or uncertain provenance
- famous history or collectible value
- legality, faction control, religious taboo, or stolen status
- known or suspected curse
- urgency and bargaining leverage
- whether the seller wants coin, another item, information, service,
  access, or a quest completed

When no campaign economy has established a markup, report a plausible
range or a concrete offer with the reason.
