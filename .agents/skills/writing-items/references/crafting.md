# Crafting

Under the 2024 baseline for most magic items, the crafter and
assistants need Arcana proficiency and proficiency with the tool
appropriate to the item category. If the item casts spells, the
crafter must have those spells prepared on each crafting day. Each
crafting day is 8 hours. Qualified assistants can reduce total time
under the crafting rules.

Distinctive rare ingredients are an optional campaign overlay, not
hidden RAW. Use them when the component makes the fiction tangible or
turns acquisition into an adventure.

If a crafting project spans enough world time that a future query,
consequence, or due-check must refer to it, the occurrence is an
`event`. The item page stays timeless.
