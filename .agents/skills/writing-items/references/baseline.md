# 2024 baseline

Rules anchors, not a substitute for same-rarity comparison. Campaign
rulings and enabled house rules outrank this table.

| Rarity | Standard value | Crafting cost | Crafting time |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Common | 100 gp | 50 gp | 5 days |
| Uncommon | 400 gp | 200 gp | 10 days |
| Rare | 4,000 gp | 2,000 gp | 50 days |
| Very Rare | 40,000 gp | 20,000 gp | 125 days |
| Legendary | 200,000 gp | 100,000 gp | 250 days |
| Artifact | Priceless | — | — |

Add the full purchase cost of a mundane weapon, armor, or other priced
object incorporated into the magic item.

Consumables other than Spell Scrolls: halve standard value, crafting
cost, and crafting time. Spell Scrolls and Potions of Healing use
their specific rules.

Standard attunement capacity is three items. Requiring attunement
means the item is worth competing for one of those slots.

Rarity is a rough power measure. Benchmark the actual effect. As a
known 2024 guideline, a Rare item should generally not exceed a +2
bonus or a level-5 spell effect; retrieve current magic-item-creation
rules when another exact rarity ceiling matters.
