# Reward and loot

A bespoke reward should usually do at least two of these:

- commemorate something the character actually did
- reinforce an established identity or relationship
- enable a tactic the player already enjoys
- offer a new verb that broadens play
- turn a weakness into an interesting choice
- connect the character to a faction, place, mystery, enemy, or obligation
- seed a future transformation or decision

Review player wish lists when available. They are not purchase orders:
surprise, strange utility, and world-specific provenance are part of
the reward.

Track spotlight across the party over time. Equal excitement matters
more than identical gold-piece value in one scene.
