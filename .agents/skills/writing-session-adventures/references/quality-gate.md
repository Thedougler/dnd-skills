# Quality gate

Run every test. A miss is a patch on this guide or a handoff to the
owner skill, not a waiver.

## Scan

A DM who only has the outline pane plus the first five lines of a node
can answer: where we are, why it is live, what to speak, where play
goes next.

## Speak

Every `[!narration]` passes `theatre-of-the-mind`. Recap is past
tense. Everything else is present, third person. One breath per
sentence. Hidden information stayed in `[!secret]-`. Speech is
quoted in its own block.

## Theatre

Every named in-play place, person, creature, and notable object has
an Establish. Scene changes and crossings have narration. The table
can name a toy and an exit without a tactical map.

## Agency

The guide never writes what a PC decides, feels, or must do. An
earned CTA is "What do you do?" after beat narration. NPC Wants
predate the party. Possible Developments are pressures, not if/then
screenplays.

## Ownership

Durable facts are wikilinked or embedded. Session-local sentences are
marked by being on this Renderer. No second canon.

## Completeness

Every session-plan beat and situation is a node or an unused
possibility. Every node has **When** and **Move on** as states. Gaps
are listed, not silently filled.

## Run

Checks fail forward. Violence has a `[!danger]`. Speaking NPCs have
a `[!tip]` with Wants, a tell, and an opening line. Encounter math
is an embed of a `kind: encounter` page.

## Tables

Each random-table row used tonight is a situation in motion: what is
happening, what the table can perceive, what choice or cost it
creates. A speakable fragment when the result is visible. DCs stay
in the DM half.

## Renderer

`generated: true`. `audience: [dm]`. Night guides in
`wiki/sessions/<NNN>/`. Modules beside the owning situation in the vault.
Not a `kind`. Doctor is clean for any owner pages this run patched.
