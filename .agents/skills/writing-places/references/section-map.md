# Section map

Fill the schema's existing sections. Do not add a parallel outline.

`locations:` on other kinds still targets `kind: location`. Wikilink other spatial kinds in the body and in geography fields.

Required non-place sections: retrieve canon or write the thinnest provisional stub lint needs, then stop.

`kind: dungeon` is owned by `writing-dungeons` — use that skill's section map.

## `location`

**Write:** Arrival (spoken mental model via `theatre-of-the-mind`), Features, typical Inhabitants and spoor, If Left Alone as process, Secrets as hidden place truth.

**Senses:** short DM scan — one signature plus footing. Not a three-sense dump and not spoken.

**Each feature:**

- **Obvious** — seen at a glance
- **Reveals** — what interaction exposes or changes
- **Logic** — physical rules
- **Consequence** — what usefully follows

**Do not author:** tonight's roster, encounter staging, trap/trial pages.

## `settlement`

**Write:** Overview and Atmosphere narration, Districts as recursive mental models, civic features inside districts, Defenses as architecture.

**Power Structure:** link existing NPCs and factions, or the thinnest stub.

**Do not author:** a finance sheet, rumor tables (`kind: hook`), tonight's street scene.

## `region`

**Write:** Overview narration (mental model), Geography (terrain, `geo_anchor`s, processes), Landmarks.

**Peoples / Threats:** links to settlements, creatures, and factions already in canon.

**Do not author:** a Senses section, a three-object affordance checklist, a second Atmosphere dump under Geography.

## `route`

**Write:** Character, Passage, Wayfinding.

Hazards as place properties. Mint `kind: hazard` when the mechanism needs its own page.

**Do not author:** loops the road does not have.
