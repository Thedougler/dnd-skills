# Rot Navigation Table

## Table

**Roll:** `d8`

| Roll | Result |
|------|--------|
| 1 | **Spore hour bites early.** A black-edged fungal bloom bursts underfoot. Each unprotected creature makes DC 14 Constitution or gains 1 exhaustion. Good masks, magic, or a known safe grove negates it. |
| 2 | **Root-road reports.** A luminous root crosses the path. DC 14 Perception spots a safe crossing. Failure sends light pulsing toward [[region.aruhe-the-grove\|The Grove]] and draws the nearest major fauna. |
| 3 | **[[creature.corpsewood\|Corpsewood]] path.** A [[creature.corpsewood\|Corpsewood]] drags dead roots across the route, slow but relentless. Waiting 30 minutes avoids it. |
| 4 | **[[creature.terror-bird\|Terror-Bird]] groundshake.** A mossed [[creature.terror-bird\|Terror-Bird]] holds a quarter-mile claim. The ground tremor gives warning. If the party freezes or withdraws, it may not charge. |
| 5 | **Silence moth overlap.** [[creature.silence-moths\|Silence Moths]] load the canopy corridor. Inside the field, verbal spells and shouted warnings fail. Fire or a slow detour opens a route. |
| 6 | **[[item.rotheart\|Rotheart]] after the cost.** [[item.rotheart\|Rotheart]] grows where exhaustion has become visible. DC 15 Nature or Medicine identifies the cure. |
| 7 | **Cave mouth under roots.** A basalt opening descends under a split-bark gallery. It may bypass a surface circuit. Switch to [[random-table.aruhe-cave-navigation\|The Cave Navigation Table]] if the party descends. |
| 8 | **Clear Lake glimmer.** Pale sky shows through the black canopy. Mark one Navigation success toward [[location.aruhe-the-clear-lake\|The Clear Lake]] or [[region.aruhe-the-grove\|The Grove]], but the route crosses a root-road. |

> [!tip] Running This Table
> Use one 30-minute Exploration Turn for root-road crossings, Rot route choice, lakeward travel, cave-mouth searches, or Grove approach. Roll after a failed navigation check.

## Usage Notes

- **Navigation DC:** DC 15 Wisdom (Survival). Following a root-road removes the lost-route risk. [[region.aruhe-the-grove|The Grove]] knows someone is coming.
- **Progress:** Three successes before two failures reaches the next Rot objective: lake glimmer, Grove approach, safe grove, cave mouth, or withdrawal line.
- **Mapping:** Five total successes maps one Rot route, including a root-road choice and one air-safe rest point if found.
- **Failure:** On each failed 30-minute turn, roll this table. [[region.aruhe-the-rot|The Rot]] should pressure lungs, roots, and time before it demands a fight.

## Variants

- If the party follows a root-road by choice, roll twice and keep any result that reports them, points to [[region.aruhe-the-grove|The Grove]], or brings fauna.
