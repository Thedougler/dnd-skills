# Rotheart

> [!narration] Appearance
> A black-hearted fruit growing on the sickest trees, its pulp the colour of old blood, warm to the touch and faintly pulsing in the hand. Eating it feels like the plant's last defiance: it will not die, so neither will you.

## Properties

- **Type:** Fruit (consumable)
- **Rarity:** Very Rare
- **Attunement:** No
- Consuming takes an Action. **Remove all exhaustion, poison, and disease**, and **maximize Hit Dice healing for 24 hours** — the *potion of vitality* concept, grown rather than brewed.

## Biology

Extreme plant stress-response chemistry — the survival biochemistry a dying plant throws at the world, exaggerated to a full magical cure. The [[creature.blight|Blight]] removed the brakes on the plant's own refusal to die. Deep-interior specimens run Very Rare.

## Identification

DC 13 Nature or Medicine (1 minute): broad physiological function — a fruit that purges harm and restores. DC 15: exact effect. *Identify* spell: exact effect automatically.

## Placement

Found only after [[region.aruhe-the-rot|The Rot]] has begun inflicting exhaustion — the cure appears after the disease has bitten, deep in [[region.aruhe-the-rot|The Rot]]'s spore-heavy interior, where the corrupted air is the thing the fruit answers.

## Abundance

Very rare.
