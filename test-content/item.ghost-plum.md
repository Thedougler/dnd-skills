# Ghost Plum

> [!narration] Appearance
> A plum the pale blue-white of milk glass, coated in a bloom so thick it catches light the way fog does. In dim light the fruit seems to lose its outline against whatever is behind it.

## Properties

- **Type:** Fruit (consumable)
- **Rarity:** Rare
- **Attunement:** No
- Consuming takes an Action. Become **invisible for 1 hour**, ending early if you attack or cast a spell.

## Biology

Optical wax microstructures — the bloom that already makes plums pale and frosty — exaggerated into active camouflage. The [[creature.blight|Blight]] pushed the fruit's own light-scattering wax past ecology until the fruit hides in plain sight.

## Identification

DC 13 Nature or Medicine (1 minute): broad physiological function — a concealing fruit. DC 15: exact effect. *Identify* spell: exact effect automatically.

## Placement

Found before an exposed stealth crossing — the fruit that solves the next crossing sitting at its threshold. Uncommon in [[region.aruhe-the-quiet|The Quiet]], where the silent jungle is already the island's version of hiding.

## Abundance

Uncommon.
