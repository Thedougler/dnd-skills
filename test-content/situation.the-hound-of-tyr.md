# The Hound of Tyr

## Situation

**Session 09 fired the inspection; [[npc.aleksander-malone|Aleksander Malone]] is now hunting [[npc.shepherd-grigori|Grigori]] through [[region.midchain|Midchain]] courts on his own authority.**

- **Dramatic question:** Will [[npc.aleksander-malone|Malone]] kill [[npc.shepherd-grigori|Grigori]] before [[faction.dravosi-crown|the Crown]] confirms the heresy — and what happens to the crew caught in the crossfire?
- **If ignored:** [[npc.aleksander-malone|Malone]] closes the trail without the crew. He reaches [[npc.shepherd-grigori|Grigori]] and kills him the way he did at [[settlement.sarns-landing|Sarn's Landing]]. The crew hears after. [[faction.dravosi-crown|the Crown]]'s [[situation.answer-heresy|official heresy clock]] never triggers — [[npc.aleksander-malone|Malone]] acted first.

> [!secret]- What is actually happening
> [[npc.aleksander-malone|Malone]] is a heresy-hunter for [[faction.dravosi-crown|the Crown]] (or was — he acts on his own authority now). Session 09's [[vehicle.hcs-ordinance|Ordinance]] inspection established the port and ship class, but not the *[[vehicle.uncertainty|Uncertainty]]*'s crew. The crew transported [[npc.shepherd-grigori|Grigori]] and dropped him in [[settlement.calveno|Calveno]]. [[npc.aleksander-malone|Malone]] does not know. He hunts heretics, not thieves, and does not care about the *[[vehicle.uncertainty|Uncertainty]]* theft or [[npc.rupert-knighton|Rupert Knighton]]'s heir. His fast clock is 0/4 courts investigated.

## Gravity

- [[pc.delmar-fisk|Delmar]] — admiral on record; the *[[vehicle.uncertainty|Uncertainty]]* is the captured cutter; exposure is existential even though [[npc.aleksander-malone|Malone]] is not looking for thieves.
- [[pc.perrin-black-jaw|Perrin]] — dormant Crown thread: [[situation.answer-heresy|Answer the Next Confirmed Heresy]] waits on confirming his pact with [[npc.auralis|Auralis]].
- [[pc.crissdalynn-khinriss|Crissdalynn]], [[pc.jean-claude-tabarnack|Jean-Claude]], [[pc.catarina-davirelli|Catarina]] — sailed with [[npc.shepherd-grigori|Grigori]] and dropped him in [[settlement.calveno|Calveno]].

Decline is letting the hunt finish off-screen.

## Bridges

- **Courts:** each household narrows geography the crew sailed with [[npc.shepherd-grigori|Grigori]] aboard.
- **The Inspection:** fired Session 09. [[vehicle.hcs-ordinance|Ordinance]] under [[npc.corbin-knighton|Corbin Knighton]] boarded the *[[vehicle.uncertainty|Uncertainty]]*; [[npc.aleksander-malone|Malone]] asked after [[npc.shepherd-grigori|Grigori]], and the crew pointed him at [[settlement.calveno|Calveno]].
- **The Army/Not the Heir:** [[npc.shepherd-grigori|Grigori]]'s flock ([[situation.growing-flock|The Growing Flock]]) and army ([[situation.grigori-ascension|Grigori's Ascension]]) remain behind the hunt. If [[npc.aleksander-malone|Malone]] kills him, the master dies — but the bound heirs remain.

## Affordances

- The crew knows where [[npc.shepherd-grigori|Grigori]] is ([[settlement.calveno|Calveno]] → [[region.midchain|Midchain]]). [[npc.aleksander-malone|Malone]] does not.
- The *[[vehicle.hcs-ordinance|Ordinance]]* inspection gives the crew a chance to misdirect, delay, or warn.
- [[npc.shepherd-grigori|Grigori]]'s [[faction.khlysty|Khlysty]] threads can lead to [[faction.khlysty|The Flock]] or the Army; [[npc.aleksander-malone|Malone]] may follow them.
- [[npc.aleksander-malone|Malone]]'s leash is loose. The [[faction.dravosi-crown|Crown]] may pull him back, or decide the result is useful.

## Tide

- **Cause:** [[npc.aleksander-malone|Malone]] decided to act. [[npc.shepherd-grigori|Grigori]] is a heretic; [[faction.dravosi-crown|the Crown]]'s confirmation is a formality he skips.
- **What changes:** 0/4 segments. Each segment is one court or household investigated; the geography of the trail shrinks.
- **Visible evidence:** A heresy-hunter pronouncing what he knows aloud, bodies left at [[region.midchain|Midchain]] courts, and the *[[vehicle.hcs-ordinance|Ordinance]]* shadowing the route.
- **What opens or closes:** [[npc.shepherd-grigori|Grigori]]'s life, [[faction.dravosi-crown|the Crown]]'s official process, and the crew's deniability all close if the hunt finishes.

## Wake

If [[npc.aleksander-malone|Malone]] kills [[npc.shepherd-grigori|Grigori]], the Army/Flock loses its master. The bound heirs remain, but the [[faction.khlysty|Khlysty]] may fracture or a lieutenant may rise. The crew's delivery of [[npc.shepherd-grigori|Grigori]] to [[settlement.calveno|Calveno]] becomes the trail [[npc.aleksander-malone|Malone]] follows to them.
