# Glass Cricket

Pale cave crickets with elongated legs and antennae, found in [[region.aruhe-the-cave|The Cave]]. Warning system, not encounter filler.

## Overview

> [!narration] Player-facing description — read or adapt at the table.
Source is silent.

## Ecology

Cave crickets live among the root wells and cave fauna of [[region.aruhe-the-cave|The Cave]]'s imported-energy food web. Their jumping is one of the few signs of life at a Root Well.

## Behavior

They **go absolutely still when large vibrations enter the basalt** — including a [[creature.blackrail|Blackrail]] approaching. Another layer of [[region.aruhe-the-cave|The Cave]]'s fauna-based warning system the party can learn to read.

## Habitat

Root wells and damp chambers throughout [[region.aruhe-the-cave|The Cave]].
