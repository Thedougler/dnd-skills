# Redheart Berry

> [!narration] Appearance
> A berry the size of a child's fist, deep red to the point of black, so saturated the juice stains before it is broken. The flesh inside is a wet, reactive red — the color of a fresh wound and of the fruit at the same time.

## Properties

- **Type:** Fruit (consumable)
- **Rarity:** Rare
- **Attunement:** No
- Consuming takes an Action. Regain **8d4+8 hit points**.

## Biology

Hyper-concentrated anthocyanins, sugars, and reactive tissue. The [[creature.blight|Blight]] exaggerated the berry's own blood-red pigments and energy-dense sugars past ecological limits — a familiar fruit mechanism, not a new biology. The red is the plant's own chemistry pushed to the point of looking alive.

## Identification

DC 13 Nature or Medicine (1 minute): broad physiological function — something that restores. DC 15: exact effect. *Identify* spell: exact effect automatically. Environmental tell: a wounded animal eats a Redheart and visibly recovers.

## Placement

Found beside badly injured PCs or NPCs — the fruit the party most wants exactly when someone is hurt. Abundant on the terraces of [[region.aruhe-the-old-gardens|The Old Gardens]]: dozens or hundreds of healing berries in sight.

## Abundance

Abundant.
