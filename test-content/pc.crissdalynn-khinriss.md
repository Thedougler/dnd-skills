# Crissdalynn Khinriss

## Overview

![[assets/pcs/crissdalynn-khinriss-banner.webp]]

![[assets/pcs/crissdalynn-khinriss-banner-alt.webp]]

Fourteen. Blue-black iridescent feathers, geometric leather armor, chart satchel, talons and wings. Irreverent until the fight starts; first three seconds she maps friends, exits, weak points. [[creature.aarakocra|Aarakocra]] [[character-option.kensei|Kensei]] [[character-option.monk|Monk]]. Player: Courtney. Table constraint: no self-harm content. No darkvision — [[pc.jean-claude-tabarnack|Jean-Claude]]'s darkness blinds her. Trained with the [[faction.sentinels-of-the-eyrie|Sentinels of the Eyrie]]. Faith: Remnis first, Syranita and Aerdrie Faenya at the margins. Carries [[item.crissdalynns-dreidel|Crissdalynn's Dreidel]] ([[npc.master-kyzil|Kyzil]]'s gift; [[item.crissdalynns-dreidel|Fate Spinner]] name still contested) and [[item.nightmantle|Nightmantle]] (cursed cloak from [[npc.lavinia-sordi|Lavinia]], sourced to [[npc.osset|Osset]]).

## Gravity

- **Want:** Finish mapping [[region.drowned-maw|The Drowned Maw]]. [[npc.master-kyzil|Kyzil]]'s approval. Home.
- **Fear:** Losing [[pc.delmar-fisk|Delmar]]. The exile staying permanent because the map is incomplete.
- **Obligation:** The pilgrimage [[npc.master-kyzil|Kyzil]] set. Twenty-two months on the [[vehicle.red-lady|The Red Lady]] before it sank.
- **Line:** Does not abandon loyalty. Believes shiny things hold value.

## Relationships

- [[pc.delmar-fisk|Delmar Atticus Fisk]] — fixed point. Pulled him from the crow's nest through thirty feet of water.
- [[pc.perrin-black-jaw|Perrin Black-Jaw]], [[pc.jean-claude-tabarnack|Jean-Claude Tabarnack]], [[pc.catarina-davirelli|Catarina Da'Virelli]] — crew. [[pc.jean-claude-tabarnack|JC]]'s darkness vs her sight; he fears birds.
- [[npc.master-kyzil|Master Kyzil]] — mentor. Gave the dreidel. Confirmed surviving counts; has not asked about two missing Sentinels. No page.
- [[faction.sentinels-of-the-eyrie|Sentinels of the Eyrie]] — exiled her at 14 for careless craft, not malice.
- [[npc.osset|Osset]] — falcon-featured man [[npc.lavinia-sordi|Lavinia]] named as [[item.nightmantle|Nightmantle]] source. Name unknown at the Eyrie. No page.

## Backstory

Cast out at fourteen. Mapping [[region.drowned-maw|the Maw]] is the price of return. The wound is the exile, not the wreck. She knows the map is unfinished.

## Arc Notes

Pilgrimage not formally complete. [[npc.master-kyzil|Kyzil]] wants full records before the missing Sentinels question. Spotlight: open air, exposed casters, downed enemies. Pressure: wind, nets, low ceilings, ranged fire. Dramatic lever: choose her safety or [[pc.delmar-fisk|Delmar]]'s. Established [[character-option.grappler|Grappler]] ([[npc.master-kyzil|Kyzil]], Session 04, natural 1). Reach tattoo recharges at dawn.

Sheet capability snapshot (synced 2026-07-25): quarterstaff +7 / 1d8+4 bludgeoning, shortbow +7 / 1d8+4 piercing, talons +7 / 1d8+4 slashing. Unarmed strike +8 / 1d8+5 bludgeoning `[verify]` — a flat +1 over the [[character-option.kensei|Kensei]]-weapon math with no feature on the sheet explaining it. Passive Perception 16, Insight 13, Investigation 13; the sheet prints no skill-proficiency list, so which skills carry proficiency is unresolved, and languages are unprinted. [[character-option.kensei|Kensei]] Way of the Brush grants calligrapher's-or-painter's supplies (which one is unrecorded). Speeds 35 ft walk / 45 ft fly are as printed `[verify]` — walk sits 5 ft under the 40 expected from [[creature.aarakocra|aarakocra]] 30 plus Unarmored Movement, and the fly value does not match the species trait. The pc compiler reads the quarterstaff as Strength-based (Martial Arts' DEX substitution is unmodeled), so its projection understates her melee attack and damage.

Theoretical combat profile (combat-sim v6.3.0, seed 1, 2026-07-25): 5.70 sustained DPR vs AC 15, 4.88 vs AC 17; nova 7.84 vs AC 15 with an ally's Faerie Fire advantage — an upper bound, not re-run. Hard counters: AoE CHA/INT saves (both +0), enclosed space; no Evasion until level 7. Near-certain loss vs any adult dragon 1v1. Unmodeled: Read the Current, [[character-option.grappler|Grappler]]/[[character-option.tavern-brawler|Tavern Brawler]] riders, [[character-option.kensei|Kensei]]'s Shot, Wind Caller, seize-and-carry. ^[inferred]

Party compile (2026-08-10, closed-form `[calculated]`): 12.63 sustained DPR vs AC 15 (Quarterstaff +7, bonus Unarmed +8), nova 19.50 (Flurry of Blows, 1 Focus Point), AC 17 / EHP 70, save weakness CHA +0 / INT +0. The party's damage is back-loaded into her and [[pc.delmar-fisk|Delmar]]'s lanes and collapses when those two are controlled rather than damaged.

## Voice

Light until combat. Protective without announcing it. "My burden was to carry you out of that stupid water." Cartographer first, fighter second — she still insists it, and nobody at the table believes it anymore. "Friends. Exit. Weak point," every time, before anyone else has drawn a weapon.
