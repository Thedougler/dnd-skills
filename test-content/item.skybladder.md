# Skybladder

> [!narration] Appearance
> A fruit like a pale bladder the size of a helmet, so light it tugs at the vine in still air. Skin thin as a drum, and the flesh inside is dry, fibrous, and buoyant enough to lift a hand holding it.

## Properties

- **Type:** Fruit (consumable)
- **Rarity:** Very Rare
- **Attunement:** No
- Consuming takes an Action. Gain a **fly speed equal to your walking speed, with hover, for 1 hour**.

## Biology

Inflated, low-density seed-dispersal tissue — the dandelion trick and the bladder-fruit trick made whole-fruit. The [[creature.blight|Blight]] exaggerated the plant's own dispersal mechanism until the fruit itself is an airship. Deep-interior specimens run Very Rare.

## Identification

DC 13 Nature or Medicine (1 minute): broad physiological function — a fruit of flight or buoyancy. DC 15: exact effect. *Identify* spell: exact effect automatically.

## Placement

The general rule applies: place the fruit immediately before or beside the problem it solves — a canopy to cross, a fall to escape. Grows in the canopy of [[region.aruhe-the-rot|The Rot]], where the sealed air and the height make flight the answer the party will soon need.

## Abundance

Rare.
