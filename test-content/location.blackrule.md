# Blackrule

> [!narration] Arrival
> A black cut in volcanic terrace-rock, [[region.midchain-south|Southern Midchain]]. The chapterhouse rises from a landing of dark, worn stone, carved straight from the volcanic shelf. The rock itself has become walls and parapets. No flag marks it, and no banner hangs from the terraces that climb behind like the steps of some ancient altar. The only sign of occupation is the scuff-marks on the landing stones where boots have worn grooves through decades of pacing, and a thin thread of smoke rising from a chimney set deep in the cliff face. The air tastes of salt and cold iron. A figure moves on the highest terrace, still as stone until he shifts his weight. Sword practice in the morning light. Unhurried. Alone.

Chapterhouse [[npc.aleksander-malone|Aleksander Malone]] chose. Disdain for civilized excess — not exile, not punishment. Does not overlap [[location.ashglass|Ashglass]] (dead volcano, young red dragon, [[region.midchain-east|Eastern Midchain]]), [[location.the-hollow-choir|The Hollow Choir]] (reclusive singing order, same south), or [[location.corrigans-rest|Corrigan's Rest]] (retired pirates, [[region.midchain-north|Northern Midchain]]). [[location.ashglass|Ashglass]] and [[location.corrigans-rest|Corrigan's Rest]] stay unminted.

## Structure

**150 by 80 feet along the ridge, climbing 80 feet from a stone landing to the peak.**

- **Landing:** Room for one small boat, with iron rungs for the climb.
- **Stair:** Past terraces cut into the rock.
- **Sightlines:** North and east faces open to the sea, visible for miles.
- **Approach:** Landing and stair only; the cliffs elsewhere run sheer.
- One hall serves as chapel and barracks. [[deity.tyr|Tyr]]'s scales, cut into the altar stone. ^[ambiguous] (the lintel carries a shallower mark)
- A weapon rack stands oiled, waiting for a heresy case.
- The volcanic rock is its only furnishing.
- The nearest steps are eroding past repair.
- Islanders share his temperament, turning away outsiders the way Blackrule turns away its own.

## Senses

- **Signature:** Warm stone that smells of iron and old incense, not cooking.
- **Footing:** Cut treads, grit, drop-offs without rails. Wind across the terrace.

## Features

- **Chapel-mouth:**
  - **Obvious:** A low arch, [[deity.tyr|Tyr]]'s scales cut shallow in the lintel, no gilt.
  - **Reveals:** A single-room oratory: bench, basin, blade-rack. Nothing extra.
  - **Logic:** He lives as he hunts — judgment space, not a house.
  - **Consequence:** Anything left here is his or an offering. Theft is heresy in his mouth.
- **Terrace stair:**
  - **Obvious:** Switchbacks scored into the face, one way up from the landing.
  - **Reveals:** Sightlines down the lane; no second stair.
  - **Logic:** Volcanic rock holds an edge. Wet makes it slick.
  - **Consequence:** Approach is seen. Retreat is the same stair.
- **Rain-cistern:**
  - **Obvious:** A covered basin under the eave, chain and ladle.
  - **Reveals:** Water enough for one man. Ash film if the wind has been from the east.
  - **Logic:** No well. Terrace catch only.
  - **Consequence:** Poison or empty the cistern and he must leave or ration.

## Hazards

> [!danger] Active Hazards
> Unrailed drop from the terrace. A shove or failed footing is a fall onto lower benches (treat as a significant fall; exact height unset). Wet rock: Dexterity (Acrobatics) to keep the stair at speed, or go prone and slide toward the lip.

## Inhabitants

- [[npc.aleksander-malone|Aleksander Malone]] — Sole resident. Prayer at the chapel-mouth; departs when heresy word arrives; returns between hunts. No heresy case confirmed since [[settlement.sarns-landing|Sarn's Landing]]; he trains alone meanwhile.

No staff. No handler.

## Secrets

> [!secret]- Hidden Details
> He chose the site. Crown maps mark it as his chapterhouse, not a posting. Visitors expecting a garrison find one man and a long leash.

## Hooks

- **Moral.** Hard to say whether leaving him here, unused, is mercy or just delay. [[faction.dravosi-crown|Dravosi]] command has begun quietly weighing whether an unconfirmed case might be worth confirming anyway, just to use him before the wait turns him into something different and dangerous.
- **Personal.** This is where [[faction.dravosi-crown|the Crown]]'s answer to [[pc.perrin-black-jaw|Perrin Black-Jaw]]'s pact with the abyss will come from, whenever that day arrives.
- **Opportunistic.** The eroding terrace steps are the only real weakness in an otherwise unwelcoming, well-defended position. A way in that doesn't require [[npc.aleksander-malone|Malone]]'s cooperation.

## If Left Alone

The cut stays. Incense goes stale. When he is loosed, the chapel stays empty until he comes back or does not.
