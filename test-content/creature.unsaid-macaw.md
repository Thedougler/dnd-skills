## Overview

> [!narration] Player-facing description — read or adapt at the table.
A parrot sits in the orchard, magnificent and healthy — plumage too bright, eyes clear, nothing sick about it. It watches. Someone hesitates mid-sentence, catches themselves, goes on. Then the bird says, in that person's exact voice, the words they chose not to speak.

Non-hostile [[region.aruhe|Aruhe]] fauna: a macaw-like parrot that psychically perceives **surface thoughts** and mimics them aloud in the thinker's own voice. It is disturbing because it exposes things characters deliberately chose not to say. It creates social tension and roleplay, not combat danger. ^[inferred — encounter design intent from the source]

## Ecology

Follows [[region.aruhe|Aruhe]]'s rule: a familiar animal performing its natural behavior past healthy limits. Parrot traits pushed to extremes:

- vocal learning → perfect voice reproduction;
- social monitoring → awareness of nearby surface thoughts;
- contextual vocalization → repeats socially salient thoughts;
- curiosity → notices which thoughts provoke reactions.

It does not understand telepathy. It behaves as though thoughts are merely sounds other creatures somehow fail to hear. Like [[creature.aruhe-river-otter|Aruhe's River Otters]], it looks **magnificent and healthy**, not mutated or diseased.

Diet: fruit, nuts, seeds, insects, and shellfish.

**Flock learning.** Macaws learn phrases from each other. If one psychically hears "Please don't tell them" and vocalizes it, other birds can later learn the phrase normally. Later macaws may therefore say disturbing phrases that are: a current thought, previously overheard speech, or flock-learned mimicry. **Never clarify which unless circumstances make it obvious** — this prevents the mechanic from becoming perfectly readable.

## Behavior

Primary rule: **repeat thoughts according to social interest, not strategic importance.** The bird reacts to sudden hesitation, suppressed speech, embarrassment, fear, multiple creatures reacting to the same thought, or someone almost saying something and stopping. It does not understand secrets, murder investigations, or tactical information — it simply notices unusually salient internal "sounds."

**Signature moment:** a character nearly says something, stops — and the macaw says it instead.

**Lies.** No lie detection. Lying sometimes naturally produces revealing surface thoughts — an NPC who says "I have never seen him" while thinking *Except at the docks* gets answered by the macaw: "Except at the docks." Practiced liars may think only about presentation and reveal nothing useful.

**Emergent horror.** After players understand the species, simply placing one nearby alters conversations: self-censorship, hesitation, suspicion, deliberate mental distraction, [[vehicle.uncertainty|Uncertainty]] over whether private thoughts are safe. The creature generates tension without using any ability.

**Taming.** PCs may tame or adopt one — do not block this with arbitrary magic. The natural downside is enough: it exposes negotiation thoughts, repeats tactical worries, reveals attraction, distrust, greed, fear, ruins stealth with inappropriate mimicry, and may become socially bonded and follow its keeper persistently. Almost no combat advantage; enormous recurring roleplay value. ^[inferred — campaign function from the source]

## Habitat

Best habitats: [[region.aruhe-the-old-gardens|The Old Gardens]], river valleys / [[region.aruhe-the-grasslands|The Grasslands]] margins, and [[location.aruhe-the-clear-lake|The Clear Lake]] margins. Avoid making them common in [[region.aruhe-the-quiet|The Quiet]] — established [[region.aruhe|Aruhe]] canon relies on that region having essentially no birdsong or small-animal noise. Individual birds may briefly follow travellers into its edge.

## Lore

> [!secret]- What is actually known about this creature — collapsed by default.
> Sailors call it the "Confessor Macaw." It is not omniscient and not an automatic truth detector — only the foremost surface thought, only in a creature that knows at least one language, only what is socially salient. The modern fear metaphor is loss of informational privacy: always-on microphones, predictive algorithms, private drafts or searches exposed. ^[inferred — thematic reading from the source]

**Origin.** The Blight did not intentionally create psychic parrots. Evolution/corruption progression: increasingly sophisticated flock communication → better anticipation of flockmates' intentions → sensitivity to pre-vocal cognitive patterns → eventually anticipating speech and perceiving thought become biologically/magically indistinguishable. The Unsaid Macaw is a parrot so exceptionally good at listening that speech is no longer necessary.

## Tactics

> [!tip] Running this creature — see the linked statblock for the runnable loop.
Runnable loop: [[creature.unsaid-macaw]] (six-beat escalation: impossible mimicry → confirmation → social consequence → adaptation → Confess → indifference). Ecology that is not mechanical: **never let the DM invent a PC's secret thoughts** — when the bird reads a PC, ask that player what their character is thinking right now that they are choosing not to say; the player authors the thought, the bird provides the supernatural exposure.
