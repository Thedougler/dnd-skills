# The Cave

The buried underside of [[region.aruhe|Aruhe]]: a vast collection of extinct lava tubes left from the island's volcanic construction. Not a sixth band — an under-region, in the same sense that [[region.aruhe-the-river|The River]] is an artery rather than another concentric layer. The surface jungle hides where you are. The Cave hides whether there is still a way out.

**Tonal thesis.** The Cave is the fear of being trapped in a tunnel after the lights go out. Not abstract fear of darkness, not buried tombs: a subway tunnel, utility tunnel, collapsed mine, underground parking structure — you don't know where the exit is, your phone would have no signal, the air might be bad, [[faction.the-passage|The Passage]] behind you might no longer exist — and then you feel something coming down the tunnel. A modern fear as recognizable beneath the surface as the [[creature.deer-stalker|Deer-Stalker]] or [[creature.wolfrabbit|Wolfrabbit]], without putting modern technology into the setting. ^[inferred — design-intent synthesis from the source's tonal statement]

## Overview

> [!narration] Overview
> The hole beneath the roots is not a burrow.
>
> Black stone curves down beneath them in a tube wide enough to walk three abreast. The walls are smooth in long places and savagely folded in others, frozen in the shape of something that once flowed. Roots have punched through the ceiling in pale curtains. Water ticks from their tips.
>
> Thirty feet in, daylight is already only a grey disc behind you.
>
> Fifty feet in, it is gone.
>
> The floor continues downhill.
>
> There are footprints in the mud.
>
> Yours are not the largest.
>
> Somewhere much farther ahead, something strikes the stone once.
>
> Then again.
>
> Then again.
>
> The second impact reaches your feet before it reaches your ears.
>
> Stop when they extinguish the light, mark the wall, continue downhill, or turn around.

Some tubes are walking-wide. Others are cathedral chambers. Others [[vehicle.the-narrow|Narrow]] until armor scrapes both walls. They braid, fork, collapse, reconnect, and occasionally open beneath the jungle through skylights whose mouths have been hidden completely beneath centuries of growth.

## Geography

**Not a sixth band.** The Cave is an under-region: a second geography cutting through several bands at once, like [[region.aruhe-the-river|The River]] is an artery rather than a concentric layer. Entrances occur beneath:

- **[[region.aruhe-the-old-gardens|The Old Gardens]]** — terrace collapses break into shallow tubes. [[location.aruhe-the-old-mouth|The Old Mouth]] is the one huge collapsed entrance near the lower terraces.
- **[[region.aruhe-the-quiet|The Quiet]]** — root wells and old skylights are swallowed by vegetation. [[location.aruhe-the-daylight-hole|The Daylight Hole]] is a collapsed skylight here.
- **[[region.aruhe-the-rot|The Rot]]** — the deepest root systems have penetrated the basalt and formed entire subterranean ecosystems.
- **Near [[location.aruhe-the-clear-lake|The Clear Lake]]** — flooded and collapsed tubes exist beneath the old volcanic highlands, but there is **no secret Cave entrance to [[region.aruhe-the-grove|The Grove]]**. [[region.aruhe-the-grove|The Grove]] remains the last room.

That last point matters: the Cave creates alternate routes and dangerous shortcuts without invalidating [[region.aruhe|Aruhe]]'s established geography.

**The Flow.** The oldest major tunnels generally slope away from the island's old volcanic highlands. An experienced character can eventually deduce: **down-tube generally means coastward; up-tube generally means inland.** Not always, but often enough to matter. A **DC 14 Intelligence (Nature) or Wisdom (Survival)** check after examining several flow patterns reveals the principle. Competence actually works — the caves are not arbitrary maze rolls.

**Scale.** The caves are not one continuous dungeon beneath all 500 miles of [[region.aruhe|Aruhe]]. There are dozens or hundreds of old flow systems, some only a few hundred feet long, some extending for miles, with later collapses occasionally connecting different generations of tunnel. The known Cave is the interconnected western/central system the party can actually enter.

Large branches commonly range: 10–30 feet wide, 6–20 feet high, with occasional chambers 100+ feet across, crawlways narrowing below 3 feet, and vertical collapse shafts connecting two or three tube levels. Real wild lava tubes are frequently rough, dark, rubble-filled environments requiring scrambling and route-finding rather than convenient underground hallways. ^[inferred — grounding from the source's cited National Park Service references]

## Route Logic

The Cave is a **volcanic under-level**: a discontinuous second geography beneath several of [[region.aruhe|Aruhe]]'s surface bands, not a single hallway beneath the whole island. Separate lava-flow systems meet where later collapses, flooded tubes, root wells, and vertical shafts have opened a connection. The party can learn that two mouths belong to the same route without assuming that every dark opening connects to every other one.

The island-scale pointcrawl, Expedition Turns, and route-state procedure remain deferred to [[dungeon.aruhe-wilderness-megadungeon|Aruhe Wilderness Mega-Dungeon]]. This region owns the Cave's durable spatial logic and the consequences of learning, opening, losing, or avoiding an underground connection.

| [[region.aruhe-the-quiet\|The Quiet]] ⇄ [[region.aruhe-the-rot\|The Rot]] | Possible, provisional link | A source-proposed cross-connection could skip a long surface approach. No exact tube or exit is fixed here; the party must establish it by exploration. |
| [[region.aruhe-the-river\|The River]] ⇄ [[location.aruhe-the-clear-lake\|The Clear Lake]] shelf | Possible, provisional link | Flooded or collapsed tubes beneath the old highlands may provide a deep shortcut. The water route is not a confirmed second entrance, and the exact emergence remains unknown. |
| A collapse returning toward [[region.aruhe-the-old-gardens\|The Old Gardens]] | Possible, provisional link | A learned shaft could return a deep expedition to the Gardens, but [[location.aruhe-the-old-mouth\|The Old Mouth]] remains the only named Gardens entrance. |
| [[region.aruhe-the-grove\|The Grove]] | Excluded by canon | No Cave route reaches [[region.aruhe-the-grove\|The Grove]]. It remains [[region.aruhe\|Aruhe]]'s last room; a Cave shortcut cannot replace [[region.aruhe-the-rot\|The Rot]] approach or create a second door. |

The possible links are **skip-level shortcuts**, not guaranteed map edges. A party that finds one can bypass surface surveillance, a known [[creature.bear-elk|Bear-Elk]] circuit, or a stretch of Rot, but it may emerge in darkness, water, a collapse pocket, or a predator's feeding system. A connection may be one-way until the return route is learned; the party should be able to retreat to a known mouth or reroute when a branch proves impassable rather than having the whole expedition depend on one unmarked tunnel.

### Learning the under-level

The Cave rewards route knowledge. Marked walls, flow direction, root wells, sound behavior, air quality, safe squeeze points, and the location of stable shelves turn an unknown descent into a usable connection. Treat the durable access states as:

| Route state | Cave consequence |
| --- | --- |
| **Unknown** | Full darkness, navigation, and collapse risk; a branch may end in water or a dead fall. |
| **Observed** | A mouth and a destination are recognized, but the party cannot yet rely on the route or its return. |
| **Mapped** | The party can choose known forks, mark hazards, and retreat or reroute without repeating the first search. |
| **Mastered** | A stable line, refuge, and exit are known; the shortcut compresses travel while retaining its environmental danger. |
| **Shortcut discovered** | One or more surface levels can be bypassed, subject to whether the connection remains open. |

Route mastery changes the party's options, not the Cave's ecology. A collapsed tube can erase a shortcut, a flood pulse can make a low passage temporary, and the [[creature.blackrail|Blackrail]] can learn the noise of a frequently used route. Retreat, a second exit, and a newly learned side passage are legitimate outcomes; pressing deeper is never the only way to make exploration meaningful.

## Climate

The Cave has no horizon and almost no landmarks. Darkness is ordinary, not supernatural: a torch creates 20 feet of bright light and then dim light beyond it; everything outside that radius still exists. The party should come to understand the difference between "I cannot see anything" and "I can see perfectly clearly for forty feet."

**Sound lies.** Unlike [[region.aruhe-the-quiet|The Quiet]] — which suppresses warning sound and makes predators unnaturally close — the Cave does almost the opposite. Every noise carries: a whispered conversation appears thirty feet away; a dropped buckle cracks through three chambers; the [[creature.blackrail|Blackrail]]'s legs may sound as though they're ahead when they're actually moving in a parallel tube behind the wall. A **DC 14 Wisdom (Perception)** check can determine the approximate direction of a major sound; on a failure you know its apparent direction — but echoes may have lied about which connecting passage produced it. Not "you hear nothing." You hear too much.

**Air.** The air cannot be trusted. Nothing looks different when breathable air becomes bad (see [[hazard.aruhe-dead-air|Dead Air]]).

## Peoples

Source is silent. The design note has the [[creature.blackrail|Blackrail]]'s name coming from sailors "much later" — no current inhabitants of the Cave are described.

## Threats

> [!danger] Active Threats
> Fewer encounters than the surface, but worse individual ones — and the possibility of separation. [[creature.blackrail|The Blackrail]] dominates the interconnected main tubes; the region's other dangers are environmental. When something comes, there is nowhere to stand aside.

- **[[creature.blackrail|The Blackrail]]** — the apex predator, a forty-foot cave centipede that treats every wall, ceiling, and floor as equally usable. Its approach gives plenty of warning: faint rhythmic vibrations, then dust, then the leg-rattle. The horror is not "gotcha, monster attacks" — it is *it is definitely coming, what do you do with the thirty seconds you have?*
- **[[hazard.aruhe-dead-air|Dead Air]]** — low chambers that collect unbreathable gas. Flame burns poorly, headache, deliberately deliberate breathing, dead cave insects below a particular elevation.
- **[[hazard.aruhe-drumstone|Drumstone]]** — thin roofs of older tubes beneath the current one. Footsteps sound slightly hollow; then the crust collapses and the party is vertically separated — and everyone heard it. So did everything else.
- **[[hazard.aruhe-the-squeeze|The Squeeze]]** — routes that [[vehicle.the-narrow|Narrow]] to spaces barely large enough for a humanoid. Standard 5e squeezing mechanics. The regional trick is choice: a large tunnel is easy movement and the [[creature.blackrail|Blackrail]] can follow; a crawlway is miserable movement and the [[creature.blackrail|Blackrail]] cannot. **Claustrophobia becomes shelter.**
- **[[hazard.aruhe-flood-pulse|Flood Pulse]]** — surface rain entering through skylights and cracks far uphill; violent pulses traveling through the lowest channels.

**Root Wells.** Where a surface tree has broken through the roof, hundreds of pale roots hang into the Cave. Among the only places that feel alive: water drips, blind insects feed, cave crickets jump, bats roost above, and there may be a faint green-grey glow from luminous Rot sap in the deepest systems. They are sources of water, vertical navigation clues, rich concentrations of prey — and **[[creature.blackrail|Blackrail]] feeding grounds**. A **DC 13 Wisdom (Survival)** check at a Root Well determines whether the recent damage is insect feeding, or whether the roots were stripped clean by something forty feet long rubbing against them.

**The Cave's fauna.** The cave food web begins with surface energy imported underground: roots → root-feeding insects → predatory arthropods; bats → guano → decomposers → cave fauna; fallen animals → scavengers → predators. [[region.aruhe|Aruhe]]'s impossible productivity pushes that system until it can sustain the [[creature.blackrail|Blackrail]]. [[creature.cave-bat|Cave Bats]], [[creature.root-roach|Root Roaches]], and [[creature.glass-cricket|Glass Crickets]] are ecosystem, not encounter filler — and all three are warning systems the party can learn to read.

## Landmarks

- **[[location.aruhe-the-great-bore|The Great Bore]]** — a nearly straight tube three miles long, 25–40 feet across. The Cave's best road: a few hours to cover what takes a day or more above. The Cave equivalent of [[region.aruhe-the-river|The River]] — and something else already owns it.
- **[[location.aruhe-the-daylight-hole|The Daylight Hole]]** — collapsed skylight beneath [[region.aruhe-the-quiet|The Quiet]], seventy feet up to a circle of green canopy. An emergency exit from the Cave — into another set of problems.
- **[[location.aruhe-the-drowned-tube|The Drowned Tube]]** — a major tunnel filled wall to wall with still black water, the route continuing underwater. Not mandatory. It exists to make the players voluntarily say *No.*
- **[[location.aruhe-the-old-mouth|The Old Mouth]]** — one huge collapsed entrance near the lower [[region.aruhe-the-old-gardens|Old Gardens]]; possibly how the party first discovers the region. [[creature.wolfrabbit|Wolfrabbit]] warrens stop at the point where daylight disappears.
- **Root Wells** — see Threats.

## Secrets

> [!secret]- Hidden Details
> The Cave is the buried underside of the island, reachable through scattered collapses in [[region.aruhe-the-old-gardens|The Old Gardens]], Quiet, and Rot — without replacing the existing route to [[region.aruhe-the-grove|The Grove]]. The [[creature.blight|Blight]] asks the same question it always asks: *what if something real did not have to remain a reasonable size?* Lava tubes really branch, collapse, create skylights, collect water, contain darkness, require scrambling, grow unusual ecosystems, and carry roots down from the forest above; centipedes really climb cave walls, attack vertebrates, use venom, grip prey with many legs, and hunt bats in caves. The Cave only removes the size limit. ^[inferred — core-transformation statement from the source]
>
> The modern fear underneath it all: **You are underground. There is only one tunnel. Something is coming down it. And you have just realized the little recess you passed sixty feet ago was not inconvenient terrain. It was the emergency exit.**

## Campaign Role

The Cave is an **optional high-risk bypass**:

| | Surface | Cave |
|---|---|---|
| Route | slower navigation, with trails and visible sky | faster when a tube is known; disorienting when it is not |
| Notice | silent birds, surface surveillance, and learned predator circuits | darkness and concealment from the surface, but every sound can travel and echo |
| Ecology | many creatures, usually readable territories | fewer encounters, but specialized cave food webs and a worse apex predator |
| Pressure | navigation, exposure, and predators | claustrophobia, dead air, flood pulse, collapse, and separation |
| Forage | more temptation | almost none; Root Wells are useful but also feeding grounds |
| Exits | sunlight eventually returns if the party can navigate back | useful entrances and emergency exits exist, but the next one is uncertain |

Players can potentially bypass a known [[creature.bear-elk|Bear-Elk]] route by finding a lava tube beneath it. That does not make the Cave "better" — it means they get to choose: cross the road patrolled by the enormous animal whose schedule they understand, or climb into the hole where the [[creature.blackrail|Blackrail]], the stone, the water, and the air each impose a different [[vehicle.uncertainty|Uncertainty]]. A large tube is easier to move through but lets the apex predator follow; [[hazard.aruhe-the-squeeze|The Squeeze]] is miserable but may be too [[vehicle.the-narrow|Narrow]] for it. The party can choose the exposure it prefers, withdraw when the cost changes, or return later with a learned connection and better tools.
