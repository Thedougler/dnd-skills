## Overview

> [!narration] Player-facing description — read or adapt at the table.
> A boar's body under a porcupine's armament, swollen past anything either animal could carry. Bone quills the length of javelins bristle from the shoulders, spine, and haunches. The tusks have grown into the skull and out again. It roots through the jungle floor head-down, shoving aside deadfall and stone alike, and when it charges the quills flatten against its body like a ram's crest.

## Ecology

The [[creature.blight|Blight]]'s corruption fused boar aggression with a porcupine's defensive arsenal and removed the off switch. Thornbacks are territorial and solitary. Two in the same stretch of jungle fight each other before they fight anything else. The quills grow back within hours of falling out, so the jungle floor around a thornback's territory carries spent spines everywhere, each one long enough to use as a stake.
