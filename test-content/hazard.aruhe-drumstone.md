# Drumstone

Sometimes the "floor" of [[region.aruhe-the-cave|The Cave]] is actually the **thin roof of an older tube beneath the current one**. The first indication is that footsteps sound slightly hollow. This is grounded directly in the way lava-tube ceilings collapse and create skylights. ^[inferred — grounding from the source's cited US Geological Survey reference]

## Description

> [!narration] Player-facing description — read or adapt at the table.
> The footing here sounds slightly hollow underfoot. A drum over empty space. The floor continues — and under it, something older waits.

## Trigger

A **Medium or larger creature crosses the crust normally**. Significant weight over the thin roof of the lower tube.

## Effect

> [!danger] What happens when the hazard triggers.

The crust collapses. A creature crossing normally makes a **DC 14 Dexterity saving throw**. On failure: the crust collapses and the creature falls **20–40 feet**, taking standard falling damage, and lands **prone amid rubble in a lower passage**. On success: it reaches stable rock at the edge.

More importantly: **the party has now been vertically separated.** And everyone heard the collapse. So did everything else.

## Detection

Footsteps sound slightly hollow. A **DC 15 Wisdom (Perception)** check notices this before putting significant weight on the crust.

## Counterplay

> [!tip] Running this hazard — how the party can mitigate, avoid, or neutralize it.

The hollow sound is the tell. A party that listens for it can route around the thin crust or cross at the edge. On success, stable rock at the edge holds.

## Escalation

> [!secret]- What happens if the hazard is ignored or interacted with repeatedly — collapsed by default.

The collapse vertically separates the party — and the sound carries. The noise announces the party's position to everything in [[region.aruhe-the-cave|The Cave]], including the [[creature.blackrail|Blackrail]].
