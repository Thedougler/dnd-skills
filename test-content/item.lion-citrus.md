# Lion Citrus

> [!narration] Appearance
> A citrus the size of a fist, rind the colour of a lion's pelt, heavy with oil that brightens the air around it. The peel comes away in a single warm coil. The flesh is impossibly sweet and electric.

## Properties

- **Type:** Fruit (consumable)
- **Rarity:** Rare
- **Attunement:** No
- Consuming takes an Action. Gain **10 temporary hit points and the benefits of *bless* for 1 hour**, with no concentration required.

## Biology

Extreme stimulant, sugar, and terpene chemistry. The citrus's own oils and stimulants are concentrated past what any fruit should carry — a recognizable plant mechanism (rind oils, sugars, mild stimulants) pushed to magical extremes by the [[creature.blight|Blight]].

## Identification

DC 13 Nature or Medicine (1 minute): broad physiological function — a fortifying or rousing fruit. DC 15: exact effect. *Identify* spell: exact effect automatically.

## Placement

A terrace fruit of [[region.aruhe-the-old-gardens|The Old Gardens]] — the abundant courage-and-luck fruit that makes a party's first terrace climb tempting. The general rule applies: place fruit immediately before or beside the problem it solves.

## Abundance

Abundant.
