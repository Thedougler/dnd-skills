# Stonepear

> [!narration] Appearance
> A pear whose flesh has gone hard as pumice, grey-green and so heavy it thuds when set down. Bite through the grit and the inside is sweet, cold, and briefly mineral.

## Properties

- **Type:** Fruit (consumable)
- **Rarity:** Rare
- **Attunement:** No
- Consuming takes an Action. Gain **resistance to all damage for 1 minute**.

## Biology

Pear sclereids — the stone cells that give pears their grit — exaggerated into magical reinforcement. The [[creature.blight|Blight]] pushed a familiar fruit mechanism (lignified stone cells) until the fruit's own structure reads as armor.

## Identification

DC 13 Nature or Medicine (1 minute): broad physiological function — a hardening or protective fruit. DC 15: exact effect. *Identify* spell: exact effect automatically. Environmental tell: an animal consumes a Stonepear before fighting.

## Placement

Found before [[creature.terror-bird|Terror-Bird]] territory — the fruit that solves the next problem sitting in the path to it. Common in [[region.aruhe-the-quiet|The Quiet]], where the mute jungle gives it no warning chorus.

## Abundance

Common.
