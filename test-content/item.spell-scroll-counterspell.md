# Spell Scroll (Counterspell)

> [!narration] Appearance
> Brittle-edged parchment sealed with unmarked grey wax. Unrolled, the single line of script shifts when looked at straight on and only settles in peripheral vision. The roll is colder than the room. Held near the ear it hums like wind in rigging. Air above the ink prickles as if a match is about to catch.

## Properties

- **Type:** Scroll
- **Rarity:** Uncommon
- **Attunement:** No
- **Value:** 170 gp
- Single casting of Counterspell. Scroll save DC **15**, attack bonus **+7**.
- Stocked at the [[business.shelf-instrument-dealer|Shelf Instrument Dealer]], [[business.cabinet-of-morsani|Cabinet of Morsani]], and [[location.vask-reclaimed-goods|Vask's Reclaimed Goods]]. Shops unminted.

## History

Pulled from a wreck's strongbox with no claimant. Passed through Vask's salvage with the rest of that hold. Origin as unrecorded as the ship.

Along contested water a faction meeting is rarely settled with steel alone. This scroll buys silence instead of a duel.

## Mechanics

SRD spell-scroll rules ([[rule.magic-items]]). Cipher is unintelligible if Counterspell is not on the reader's spell list. No material components. Normal casting time. The scroll crumbles on a successful cast. Interrupted casting does not consume it.

If the spell is on the list but above the reader's available level: spellcasting-ability check DC 10 + spell level (DC 13). Failure: the spell vanishes with no effect.

Counterspell remains a Reaction when a creature within 60 feet casts with Verbal, Somatic, or Material components. The scroll does not grant a free Reaction or put the spell on an off-list class.

## Quirks

Unmarked wax. Cold. Thin dry hum.

> [!secret]- Secrets
> No house mark is the tell. Whoever commissioned it did not want a name on the seal.
