## Overview

> [!narration] Player-facing description — read or adapt at the table.
**Enormous black predatory bird towering over a horse**, wingspan filling the canopy even folded. The beak is massive, serrated inside with visible teeth. Moss and green vegetation grow directly from its feathers and wings, the [[creature.blight|Blight's]] corruption made physical, [[region.aruhe|Aruhe]] claiming even its apex predators. Yellow piercing eye. Clawed feet built like a hunting bird's, each talon the length of a forearm. A corrupted prehistoric predator.

![[assets/creatures/terror-bird.jpg]]

A mossed tree that sprints. The charge arrives through the ground first.

- Taller than a horse. Folded wings fill the gap between trunks.
- Black plumage. Living moss grows from the feathers, thickest along the wing-bones.
- One yellow eye. The beak is serrated on the inner edge. Teeth show when it gapes.
- Each talon is as long as a forearm. It sprints on two legs. It does not fly.
- Any creature that touches the ground feels the approach within 30 feet.

## Ecology

Axebeaks are nuisance mounts. The [[creature.blight|Blight]] scaled them past every limit that kept them small. Mass and hunger grew without a brake. Range grew to match. A terror-bird outgrows every other animal on [[region.aruhe|Aruhe]] except the [[creature.bear-elk|Bear-Elk]]. The two avoid each other by instinct.

The moss is not a coat. It is island tissue. It grows from the feathers the way it reclaims the terraces. The bird does not notice. The moss matches the canopy, so a still bird reads as a trunk with a yellow knot.

Each bird holds one quarter-mile claim. It will not share that claim with another of its kind. It hunts from stillness. It stands in shade until prey enters. Then it charges. The ground announces the rush before the eye finds the bird. The beak claps once at the start. Until then the claim is quiet.

It kills large prey with beak and talon. It swallows what fits. After a swallow it returns to a digest roost. The roost is a pressed circle of moss at a trunk. Bone, hoof, and a sour bolus sit in the same green as its back. It does not fight over scraps. It does not chase prey that leaves the first rush.

Hunger is the pressure. A hungry bird holds the rim for longer. A fed bird is a mossed silhouette and a tremor that does not close.

Traces of a live claim:

- Crushed corridors through grass that point at the shaded rim.
- Droppings with whole bone.
- Flattened patches that later host [[hazard.razer-grass|Razer-Grass]].
- Empty bird-sound. Other fauna have already left.

A smaller bird is last year's bird, not a second kind. Burn scars around the muzzle mark a young one that tried a [[creature.grubnade|Grubnade]] flower. No one counts the adults. A count requires a view of the bird before the bird has a view of you.

## Behavior

The bird inhabits the claim before anyone arrives. It stands. It watches the light. It leaves only to digest or to drive out a rival.

**Still-hunt.** Wings stay half-spread as camouflage and balance. Moss and leaf sit on the same plane. From the open, it is a dark wall until the yellow eye moves.

**One rush.** The first charge is the commitment. Prey that leaves melee after that rush can end the encounter. Prey that stays in beak reach still faces rake and swallow. The bird does not hunt scraps and does not run a second sprint into cover it refused on the first.

Contact:

| Party act | Bird |
|---|---|
| Freeze, or leave the quarter-mile | It may hold still. The claim is enough. |
| Run in the light | It charges. The floor shakes first. |
| Reach eight-foot grass, water, or [[hazard.razer-grass|Razer-Grass]] | The charge ends. |
| Throw food or leave a carcass | It ignores the scrap. |
| Wound it, then leave melee | It breaks off. |
| Stay in reach after a failed rush | It rakes and tries to swallow. |
| Enter the Grove flower ring | It stands as a mourner. It does not hunt. |

A rival terror-bird in the same quarter-mile is a fight, not a hunt. The tremor is also a warning to its own kind.

At the black-flower ring of [[region.aruhe-the-grove|The Grove]], the bird gathers with other fauna and does not cross. Do not run it as an ambush there. The Grove page owns that state. A take or a strike on the memorial can break it.

## Habitat

Home zones: [[region.aruhe-the-rot|The Rot]] to [[region.aruhe-the-grove|The Grove]], and the shaded rims of [[region.aruhe-the-grasslands|The Grasslands]] in [[region.aruhe-the-quiet|The Quiet]]. Deep-interior predator.

A river valley is the only long sightline in the jungle. A bird that holds the surrounding quarter-mile takes the canopy rim as a hunt post. It waits in shade until prey is in the light, then charges. Open sky is bait.

It does not enter the eight-foot grass. It does not enter water. It will not charge through [[hazard.razer-grass|Razer-Grass]]. A white stand ends the rush that defines it.

Rim posts include the grassland bank at [[location.aruhe-the-long-reach|The Long Reach]]. They also include the cliff rim of [[location.aruhe-the-clear-lake|The Clear Lake]] when a bird holds that quarter-mile. The party also meets them in the inner jungle. On the last approach to the Grove, the mourner line can replace the hunt.

## Lore

> [!secret]- What is actually known about this creature — collapsed by default.
> Not a new species. The [[creature.blight|Blight]] took an axebeak and removed the brakes on size, hunger, and range. The moss is living island tissue, not a parasite the bird can shed. At [[region.aruhe-the-grove|The Grove]] the same bird stands as a mourner because the memorial holds the island's rules, not because the bird chose peace.

Castaways learn one rule first: if the floor shakes, do not run toward the light. Sailors who only hear the stories call it a roc that lost the sky. The tremor is the true tell. The moss is the second. A yellow eye in a trunk is the last.

[[item.stonepear|Stonepear]] sits on the path before a claim. The fruit is not bait from the bird. It is the island's usual offer: a tool for the next problem, if anyone takes it.

## Variants

None. A smaller bird is a younger bird. A Grove mourner is a state, not a subspecies.

## Relationships

Blight-corrupted fauna of [[region.aruhe|Aruhe]].

- **[[creature.blight|Blight]]** — the pressure that scaled the axebeak and grows moss from its feathers.
- **[[creature.bear-elk|Bear-Elk]]** — the one land animal it will not contest. Avoidance is instinct, not a learned scar.
- **[[creature.aruhe-crown-squid|Crown Squid]]** — dangerous prey, and the opposite mind. The terror-bird's first charge is its commitment. The squid likes a chase. Escape from canopy into a Grassland cut trades one for the other.
- **[[creature.snakewood|Snakewood]]** — the grab it should not make. Terror-birds are too large for normal prey. A young snakewood that grabs one is ripped out of the canopy.
- **[[creature.grubnade|Grubnade]]** — avoided. They do not hunt flowers that hold them. Muzzle scars teach the young ones.
- **[[creature.deer-stalker|Deer-Stalker]]** — yields carcasses and will not be seen by this bird. Light is how the terror-bird notices it.
- **[[creature.aruhe-river-otter|Aruhe River Otter]]** — avoids the bird on land. In water the otters dominate, and the bird does not swim.
- **[[hazard.razer-grass|Razer-Grass]]** — not crossed. A charge also flattens ground that later hosts a white stand.
- **[[item.stonepear|Stonepear]]** — placed before a claim. The fruit solves the next problem if anyone takes it.
- **[[region.aruhe-the-grove|The Grove]]** — mourner line on the last approach. Hunt stops at the flower ring unless the memorial is violated.
