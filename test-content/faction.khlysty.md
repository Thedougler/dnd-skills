# Khlysty

Ecstatic doctrine: flesh is not the enemy of spirit; excess is devotion. It left [[region.otel-karn|Otel Karn]] generations ago and became this cult. Standalone `_raw/khlysty.md` is now archived into this page. Publicly they are a nameless healer pattern; internally the Flock. Outsiders (Crown) say Khlysty.

## Overview

Publicly: a heresy the [[faction.dravosi-crown|The Dravosi Crown]] reserves [[npc.aleksander-malone|Aleksander Malone]] to hunt once infiltration is confirmed. Internally: an office (Korabl) carried furthest by [[npc.shepherd-grigori|Shepherd Grigori]], who believes he invented the survival trick.

## Goals

- **Stated goal:** Transcendence through the flesh, not against it.
- **True goal:** Keep [[creature.hierarch|Hierarch]] threads alive across generations. [[npc.shepherd-grigori|Grigori]]'s read: outgrow "blood thins, coherence fails."
- **Immediate objective:** Stay below the threshold where anyone looks. Two confirmed binds ([[faction.tessarine-concordat|Tessarine]] heir in [[settlement.calveno|Calveno]]; [[faction.dravosi-crown|Dravosi]] merchant-lord's daughter). Refresh by courtesy, not spectacle. Clock 2/6 toward using threads to steer votes and marriages.

**Front ([[situation.grigori-ascension|The Army, Not the Heir]]):** bind enough dispersed noble hosts across [[faction.dravosi-crown|Dravosi]], [[faction.tessarine-concordat|Tessarine]], and the [[faction.chain-council|Chain Council]] that a [[creature.hierarch|Hierarch]]'s blood-thins-and-fails weakness becomes obsolete. Off-screen if unopposed: another court, another dying heir. Mid-term: [[npc.shepherd-grigori|Grigori]] learns of the [[item.pearl-of-souls|Pearl of Souls]] through [[faction.tessarine-concordat|Tessarine]] channels.

## Methods

Quiet healing. Blood left in another body. Dormant threads in noble houses — [[faction.dravosi-crown|Dravosi]], [[faction.tessarine-concordat|Tessarine]], [[faction.chain-council|Chain Council]]. No single heir to attack.

> [!tip] Running This Faction
> Not a street cult with banners. A shepherd who saved your crewmate. The word *Khlysty* is Crown language. Islanders on [[region.otel-karn|Otel Karn]] do not use it for what they live.
