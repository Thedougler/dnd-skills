# Giant's Guava

> [!narration] Appearance
> A guava the size of a skull, skin flushed pink and gold, so ripe it gives under a thumb. The smell is deep and glandular — fruit, yes, and something animal underneath.

## Properties

- **Type:** Fruit (consumable)
- **Rarity:** Rare
- **Attunement:** No
- Consuming takes an Action. Your **Strength score becomes 25 for 1 hour**.

## Biology

Growth hormones and signalling compounds pushed across the plant–animal divide. The [[creature.blight|Blight]] exaggerated the guava's own growth chemistry until it speaks directly to animal muscle — a recognizable mechanism (plant hormones that shape plant growth) forced past its boundary.

## Identification

DC 13 Nature or Medicine (1 minute): broad physiological function — a strengthening fruit. DC 15: exact effect. *Identify* spell: exact effect automatically.

## Placement

Found near evidence of a [[creature.bear-elk|Bear-Elk]] crossing — the fruit that lets someone match the island's strong things, placed where the party will soon need exactly that. Ranges across [[region.aruhe-the-old-gardens|The Old Gardens]] into [[region.aruhe-the-quiet|The Quiet]].

## Abundance

Common.
