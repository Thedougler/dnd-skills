# Aruhe Beach

The charted mouth of [[region.aruhe|Aruhe]] — one reef gap, one short shingle landing. Coral at low tide still stands jagged. The name is the sand, not the reef.

This is the island's only approach for anything larger than a canoe. The hyper-nature starts in the water — coral too sharp, crabs too large, shells that used to be skulls — but it has not yet closed the beach. Safest ground on [[region.aruhe|Aruhe]]. Past the first riser the garden begins to want.

For an Outer [[region.aruhe|Aruhe]] expedition, this is a safe entrance and extraction point: a crew can land, listen, regroup, and choose whether to go inland. The retreat over the same gap and back to open water remains visible from the shingle, so leaving is a real and legible choice while tide, vessel, and shelter permit. The broader route system belongs to the deferred [[dungeon.aruhe-wilderness-megadungeon|Aruhe Wilderness Mega-Dungeon]], not this landing page.

## Overview

> [!narration] Overview
> Jagged reef a half-mile out. At low water the coral stands up like a mouthful of teeth. One gap. Then a short shingle beach you can walk end to end in minutes. A broken [[vehicle.vethka|Vethka]] hull sits half-buried above the tideline. The swell never quite stops talking. Past the sand, dark terrace stone starts to climb. Stop when they choose the gap, the wreck, or the climb.

On the shingle the worst a visitor meets is a shark in the gap or a crab wearing a skull. Birds watch, crabs forage, and other fauna continue their natural behavior. The tide, footing, and territorial hazards remain real.

## Geography

A reef sits roughly half a mile offshore along this western face. One gap is the only charted approach for anything bigger than a canoe. The beach is shingle, short, and the sole landing; the rest of [[region.aruhe|Aruhe]]'s coast is cliff, locked ribbon, or unbroken tooth-line. Tidal shallows expose at low water and drown at high. [[region.karath|Karath]]'s cliffs stand across half a mile of channel on a clear day. From here the island behind the terraces does not end.

The land is still working. Swell breaks the ring and feeds the gap. Low tide lays [[region.verdant-teeth|The Teeth]] bare and uncovers every buried shell. High tide hides the skulls and makes the gap a different crossing. Wreckage collects here for generations — hull-plank, bone, and the dark [[vehicle.vethka|Vethka]] above the tideline — and the local crabs have outgrown every natural shell on the reef.

Inland, the first dark risers of [[region.aruhe-the-old-gardens|The Old Gardens]] start at the vegetation line. That climb is the only way off the sand. Off the beach into the ring is swim or wreck. Survivor testimony and footprints give the same direction: warnings from the wreck and a trail that leaves the hull toward the tideline before stopping at the first terrace point toward the Gardens. The active rescue staging and which castaways can be reached remain owned by [[situation.aruhes-hungry-isle|Aruhe's Hungry Isle]].

The reef crossing is a durable reward when it is learned. A crew that observes and charts the gap can treat reef navigation as mastered on later expeditions: future landings are automatic or advantaged as the DM judges, so a return need not replay the first approach. The mastery is knowledge and access, not salvage from the reef.

## Climate

Tide and swell run the hazard more than weather. Morning mist sits on the shingle and burns off by midday, then returns with evening. Offshore wind. Wrong tide turns the gap itself into the crossing.

## Peoples

No one holds it. [[creature.grung|Grung]] patrol the surrounding reefs and refuse to land, watching the gap without using it. Survivors of [[event.aruhe-wreck|The Aruhe Wreck]] — [[npc.sandro|Sandro]] and [[npc.nino|Nino]] — shelter in the hull while that aftermath is live.

## Threats

> [!danger] Active Threats
> Giant sharks work the gap when something struggles. At low tide, [[creature.reef-skull|Reef Skulls]] bury in shingle — dog-sized hermit crabs in [[creature.human|Human]] skulls or hull-chunks — and erupt under feet. [[creature.vine-lash|Vine Lash]] hangs at the vegetation line. Giant crabs work the shallows. Blood hawks dive on whatever the tide leaves exposed.

**Reef gap (vessel).** Threading the gap: DC 12 Dexterity (water vehicles). Failure scrapes coral for 11 (2d10) bludgeoning to the vessel; each creature on deck DC 12 Dexterity or prone. Failure by 5 or more grounds the vessel 1d4 hours; giant sharks arrive in 1d6 rounds.

**Skull shingle (low tide).** DC 14 Wisdom (Perception) while crossing. Failure: 1d4+1 [[creature.reef-skull|Reef Skulls]] erupt within 5 feet as a surprise round.

## Landmarks

- **The gap** — only inbound channel for anything larger than a canoe. Visible as a break in the tooth-line at low water.
- **The wrecked hull** — half-buried [[vehicle.vethka|Vethka]] above the tideline. Windbreak, survivor shelter, dark shape from the reef.
- **Tideline moss** — the line survivors treat as the start of the island's claim. Watch is free. Taking starts here.
- **First risers** — dark cut stone at the vegetation line. Handoff into [[region.aruhe-the-old-gardens|The Old Gardens]].
