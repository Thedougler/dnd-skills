## Overview

> [!narration] Player-facing description — read or adapt at the table.
A cloud of pale moths the size of a ship's sail, drifting through the canopy in absolute silence. The jungle's ambient noise dies a full thirty feet ahead of the swarm, so the first sign of approach is the absence of everything else. Individual moths are thumb-sized and colorless. Together they blot out the canopy light and fill the air thick enough to choke on.

## Ecology

Moths do not swarm like this. [[creature.blight|Druid Lich]]'s corruption bound thousands of individuals into a single hunting body, coordinated without sound, steering by vibration alone. The silence is the hunting method. A party inside the aura cannot coordinate, cannot cast verbal spells, and cannot call for help when the cloud settles over them.

## Behavior

Drift dusk and dawn. Fire drives them off. They do not pursue prey that leaves tree cover. First tell is silence, not sight.

## Habitat

Home zones: [[region.aruhe-the-rot|The Rot]] into [[region.aruhe-the-grove|The Grove]]. The swarms drift the deep interior at dusk and dawn, following the same corridors through the canopy. Fire drives them off. They do not pursue prey that leaves the tree cover.
