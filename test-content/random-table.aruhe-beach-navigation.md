# [[region.aruhe-the-beach|Aruhe Beach]] Navigation Table

## Table

**Roll:** `d8`

| Roll | Result |
|------|--------|
| 1 | **Gap current shifts.** Speak: "The swell in the throat changes. White water that was a seam is a wall." Wait 30 minutes, or DC 12 Dexterity (water vehicles) now. Failure scrapes coral, 11 (2d10) bludgeoning to the vessel. |
| 2 | **Skull shingle wakes.** Speak: "Low water shows shells that are not shells. They click when the next foot would land." DC 14 Wisdom (Perception) spots [[creature.reef-skull\|Reef Skulls]] first. Failure starts the skull-shingle hazard. |
| 3 | **Blood hawks mark a take.** Speak: "Wings stoop at the smell of blood or bait. The birds want the meat, not the crew." Dive at exposed food. |
| 4 | **[[creature.grung\|Grung]] boats hold the line.** Speak: "Patrol hulls slide closer to the gap and stop. Spears stay aboard." They will not land. A visible parley costs 30 minutes and can confirm they fear the sand more than the party. |
| 5 | **Vine line at first risers.** Speak: "Where beach becomes stone, a clean patch of ground sits under hanging green." A [[creature.vine-lash\|Vine Lash]] waits there. DC 13 Wisdom (Survival) reads the patch. Failure puts the first climber under the lash. |
| 6 | **Mist hides the safe seam.** Speak: "Mist takes the reef marks. The throat is a rumor." Next Navigation success counts only if a second PC succeeds on DC 13 Wisdom (Perception), Intelligence (Investigation), or water vehicles. |
| 7 | **The wreck shade changes.** Speak: "The broken [[vehicle.vethka\|Vethka]] creaks and drops a plank. Something dry shows in the new dark." Search: a refuge, a fresh footprint, or a hidden water jar, and mark one Beach route success without a Survival roll. |
| 8 | **Coral mouth closes.** Speak: "The gap starts to close on the tide. The way out is a clock." Leave now with advantage on the next gap check, or stay and take disadvantage until the next tide window. |

> [!tip] Running This Table
> Use one 30-minute Exploration Turn when the party lands, crosses the shingle, watches the reef, searches the wreck, or tries to leave through the gap. Roll only after a failed navigation check or when time matters.

## Usage Notes

- **Navigation DC:** DC 12 Wisdom (Survival) on foot, or DC 12 Dexterity with water vehicles for the gap.
- **Progress:** Three successes before two failures finds the intended local objective: gap, wreck, first risers, or extraction line.
- **Mapping:** Five total successes maps the Beach route for later returns.
- **Failure:** On each failed 30-minute turn, roll this table. The result costs time, changes position, reveals a hazard, or triggers a local encounter.

## Variants

- At high tide, replace result 2 with giant crabs in the shallows.
- At low tide, roll twice and keep the result that involves reef, shingle, or gap.
