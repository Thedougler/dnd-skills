# Nino

## First Impression

> [!narration] Narration
> He stays in the surviving shade of a broken [[vehicle.vethka|Vethka]] hull. Fresh prints run from the wreck to the tideline and stop. He does not raise his voice. He does not step onto the first terrace. When anyone looks inland, he puts his boot on the moss line and leaves it there.

## Wants

- **Right now:** Leave [[region.aruhe|Aruhe]] without crossing the garden line.
- **Long term:** Get off the island alive.

> [!tip] Playing Tips
> Silence first. Repeated caution. Do not make him the explainer if [[npc.sandro|Sandro]] is present — Nino shows the line; [[npc.sandro|Sandro]] states it. If pressed inland: [[npc.nino|Nino]]: "Not that way."

## What They Know

- The shoreline is different from the jungle.
- Fruit is not food here.
- Tomo is gone. He does not have [[npc.sandro|Sandro]]'s full account.

## Relationships

- [[npc.sandro|Sandro]] — fellow [[settlement.calveno|Calveno]] captive. She has not explained Tomo.
- Tomo — dead the first night. No page.
- [[vehicle.vethka|Vethka]] — remaining shelter.

## Levers

- A sail that is not [[creature.grung|Grung]].
- A route that never crosses the garden line.
- Asking him to go inland shuts him down.

> [!secret]- Secrets
> Source is silent on a private motive beyond leaving. Do not invent one.

> [!danger] If Violence Starts
> He does not fight the island. He goes back into the hull.

## If Left Alone

He keeps to [[npc.sandro|Sandro]]'s fires and the hull shade until the wreck fails. Then he joins the island's count. See [[situation.aruhes-hungry-isle|Aruhe's Hungry Isle]] and [[situation.the-aruhe-rescue|The Aruhe Rescue]].

## Mechanics

No statblock. Exhausted commoner. He will not be bait.

## Continuity

Still using the wreck's shade as of the wreck's aftermath. Page minted from the [[region.aruhe|Hungry Isle]] draft; no dedicated Nino source yet.
