> [!narration] Overview
> Gun decks on a grey hull. Fifteen ports a side, all closed. Brass. A white Admiralty stripe wider than any other Crown ship in [[region.verdant-scatter|The Scatter]]. Pennants on every mast. Figurehead holds a crown up — a claim, not a gift. The ship moves slow. It does not need to be fast.

First-rate, 215 feet, six years at [[region.harwick|Harwick]] Naval Yard. Largest hull afloat in these waters. Copper-sheathed. 104 mounted guns plus eight chasers. Face of the figurehead left unfinished on purpose.

Does not patrol. Does not escort. Deploys when [[faction.dravosi-crown|the Crown]] must show full force. Carries the Admiral of [[region.shattered-sea|The Shattered Sea]] (source also points at a [[pc.delmar-fisk|Delmar]] interview page — do not collapse that to PC [[pc.delmar-fisk|Delmar]] without a later source). ^[ambiguous] Word of its coming runs weeks ahead.

Does not chase. Presents guns and waits. One full lower-deck broadside ends most fights. Cannot turn in shallows. Fears fire ships.

**Grand Magazine.** Specialist shot once per Bastion Turn. Crits at peak. Nat 1 misfires, does not explode.

**Admiral's War Room.** Fleet orders or tactical assessment once per Bastion Turn. While in a region, Crown assets treat Captain as filled. Hostile captains in visual range: DC 14 Wis or shaken.

**Battle Surgeon's Deck.** Surgery / potions / disease. Short rest: PCs roll Hit Dice twice, take higher. Long rest after combat: +1 Hit Die recovered.

No named captain in source. Roles: master gunner, flag lieutenant, surgeon, surgeon's mate (cook).
