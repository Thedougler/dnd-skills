# The Clear Lake

Highland lake in inner [[region.aruhe-the-rot|The Rot]]. Source of the rivers that open [[region.aruhe-the-grasslands|The Grasslands]]. Not a second heart. [[region.aruhe-the-grove|The Grove]] stays the last room, on a shelf above one shore.

Warm mineral water that looks glacial: gin-clear over pale stone, turquoise only in the deep, still enough to hold the sky twice. The bowl is miles across. The far shore is weather. The floor does not resolve.

At deep [[region.aruhe|Aruhe]]'s center this is a hub, not a finish line. [[region.aruhe-the-river|The River]] offers an upstream channel into [[location.calveno-la-vasca|The Basin]], while downstream cuts leave it; Rot trails and the exposed root-road converge around the pale stone; cave exits can emerge beneath the old highlands; and [[region.aruhe-the-grove|The Grove]] hangs on a shelf above one shore. The full island-scale route procedure remains deferred to [[dungeon.aruhe-wilderness-megadungeon|Aruhe Wilderness Mega-Dungeon]]. The lake's open sky, white lip, and double reflection make it a recognizable strategic center from any approach, while the shelf remains the last room rather than a second door.

![[assets/places/aruhe-the-clear-lake.jpeg]]

> [!narration] Arrival
> The jungle breaks on pale stone. A wide lake sits in the bowl, still enough that the sky lies on it twice. The water is gin-clear over white shelves. The far shore is a pale line in the haze. Deeper in it goes turquoise, then dark where branches tangle and will not resolve. Something in that tangle might be a shoulder. Fish tick the shallows. Prints and crushed grass mark the lip. A green shelf hangs above one shore, fruit-sweet. Streams leave the stone and cut downhill. [[region.aruhe-the-river|The River]] that led here is one of them. Stop when they drink, stare down, climb the shelf, or keep to [[region.aruhe-the-rot|The Rot]].

## Senses

- **Signature:** Stillness after river noise. No lap. Warm water on the hand. The look is ice; the touch is not.
- **Footing:** Pale stone, slick where wet, undercut in places. Soft print-mud at the main drink lane.

## Features

- **Deep-island hub:**
  - **Obvious:** Several kinds of trace meet at the bowl: an upstream river channel, Rot game trails, luminous root-road scars, and cave mouths or collapse exits below the highland stone. The green Grove shelf overlooks one shore.
  - **Reveals:** These are distinct approaches, not one prescribed corridor. A party can arrive by water, trail, root-road, or cave and still recognize the same pale lip.
  - **Logic:** The lake is a basin where [[region.aruhe-the-river|The River]] source, the Blight's root network, and discontinuous volcanic tubes meet. [[region.aruhe-the-grove|The Grove]] shelf is land above the water, not a submerged entrance.
  - **Consequence:** The lake is a strategic center for choosing the next approach. Arriving here does not force a climb to [[region.aruhe-the-grove|The Grove]], make a cave exit reliable, or turn the open water into a safe route.

- **Open-sky reset:**
  - **Obvious:** After the sealed, spore-heavy [[region.aruhe-the-rot|The Rot]], canopy gives way to miles of sky and a horizon broken only by pale water and weather. Sound and sight travel differently here.
  - **Reveals:** The party can pause to read shore traffic, river headings, root movement, cave signs, and the shelf in one shared view. The lake changes the expedition's pacing from enclosed attrition to exposed assessment.
  - **Logic:** The bowl interrupts the canopy; open water carries the sky while the pale lip leaves little cover. The break in pressure is physical, not a promise of safety.
  - **Consequence:** Give the table a moment to compare approaches before committing. Watering-hole predators and the lake's depth and egress hazards still apply.

- **Mastered approaches:**
  - **Obvious:** Repeated traffic leaves different marks: landing wear and current-scoured stone at [[region.aruhe-the-river|The River]], boot and claw sign on Rot trails, luminous pulses along roots, and scraped or water-marked cave exits.
  - **Reveals:** Reading and recording those marks turns a first arrival into durable access. A mapped or mastered approach remembers its safe landing, transition, or refuge instead of making a later party rediscover the whole crossing.
  - **Logic:** The lake basin's landmarks do not move, but its routes have different costs and failure modes. Route knowledge changes which transitions can be trusted, not the ecology of the water, roots, Rot, or caves.
  - **Consequence:** Later expeditions can combine approaches — for example, follow [[region.aruhe-the-river|The River]] upstream, change to a Rot trail, and use a known root-road or cave exit — resolving only the new transition. Mastery compresses travel without making a shortcut mandatory or the [[region.aruhe-the-grove|Grove]] bypassable.

- **The pale lip:**
  - **Obvious:** Open stone around a quarry-eye of water miles across. Far shore is haze, not a shout. Prints, droppings, crushed grass.
  - **Reveals:** This is the island's watering hole. Traffic uses the main shore. Something large has used this stone on a clock.
  - **Logic:** One sky the interior fauna must break cover for. Hunters watch the same lane.
  - **Consequence:** Standing here to look down is the same skyline as drinking. Cover is the tree line, not the postcard.

- **False shelves:**
  - **Obvious:** White stone under gin-clear water. The first shelf looks close enough to wade.
  - **Reveals:** Nature or a long look: the next pale band is much farther than it reads. Fish stay in the top water.
  - **Logic:** Clarity flattens depth. The water is warm. The lip is slick and undercut.
  - **Consequence:** A sip at the lip is easy. A step past the first shelf is a swim. Climbing out is the hard part.

- **The snag layer:**
  - **Obvious:** Below the turquoise, a tea-dark / olive tangle of branches and logs. Something in it is vaguely [[creature.human|Human]]. The true bottom does not appear.
  - **Reveals:** From the lip the shape will not resolve. Reach the snags and it is later dead, not the pair on the shelf.
  - **Logic:** Wood and bodies catch where the bowl goes dark. Fish do not cross into [[faction.the-tangle|The Tangle]].
  - **Consequence:** Looking is free. Going down is how the lake keeps people.

- **Grove shelf:**
  - **Obvious:** A small green room above one shore. Fruit-sweet air. Too-green grass. The last clearing.
  - **Reveals:** [[region.aruhe-the-grove|The Grove]]: tree, two unmarked graves, black flowers. The lake is visible from the tree line. The still water, snag layer, and memorial shelf point toward grief being kept on land, not inside the lake. Out of [[region.aruhe-the-grove|The Grove]] is still back through [[region.aruhe-the-rot|The Rot]], not a swim.
  - **Logic:** Memorial on land. The ocean channel beyond the western landing was the drowning place, not this freshwater bowl and not a second door.
  - **Consequence:** The evidence points toward [[region.aruhe-the-grove|The Grove]] without dictating the route. River-follow finds the lake, then the shelf; Rot trails, root-road, or a known cave exit can bring the party to the hub first, and a party may stop at the water instead.

- **Outlet streams:**
  - **Obvious:** Water leaving the far stone, cutting downhill into Rot, then Quiet.
  - **Reveals:** These are the rivers that tear [[region.aruhe-the-grasslands|The Grasslands]] open. Follow a cut uphill and this bowl is the source.
  - **Logic:** The heading that does not lie, run backward.
  - **Consequence:** Obvious approach, not a gate. Bushwhack still works.

## Hazards

> [!danger] Active Hazards
> The lip is a watering hole. [[creature.deer-stalker|Deer-Stalkers]] wait in pale rock and grass for whatever leaves the cluster. A [[creature.terror-bird|Terror-Bird]] that holds this quarter-mile uses the cliff rim and does not swim. The [[creature.bear-elk|Bear-Elk]] circuit includes this shore.

**Depth lie.** DC 14 Intelligence (Nature) or Wisdom (Survival) to read the actual drop before wading. Failure: the first shelf is treated as three feet and is closer to thirty. Entering past the lip is swimming.

**Egress.** Climbing out at the undercut lip is a DC 15 Strength (Athletics) check. Failure: the creature starts its next turn in the snag water, 20 feet down, and is restrained by branches (DC 14 Athletics or Acrobatics to end). The branches are terrain, not a creature.

**Hold breath.** Standard drowning once a creature cannot breathe. The water is warm. Cold is not the kill.

**Skyline.** A creature on the open lip has no cover from the rim or the drink-lane rocks. After travel under sealed canopy, Perception into the shade is at disadvantage until one minute in the light (same glare as [[region.aruhe-the-grasslands|The Grasslands]]).

**[[creature.bear-elk|Bear-Elk]] window.** Same 4-hour local circuit as a lake-facing Quiet/Rot approach. This shore is on those inland loops. DC 15 Wisdom (Survival) to time the gap from prints and wet stone.

## Inhabitants

No people. [[faction.grung-clans|Grung Clans]] never reached this bowl.

- [[creature.wolfrabbit|Wolfrabbits]] and [[creature.thornback|Thornbacks]] — daily drinkers on the main lip. Prints, quills, crushed grass.
- [[creature.deer-stalker|Deer-Stalkers]] — still in pale rock and shore grass. They take whatever walks off the lip alone.
- [[creature.terror-bird|Terror-Bird]] — rim post when one holds the quarter-mile. Does not enter the water.
- [[creature.bear-elk|Bear-Elk]] — drinks here when the circuit says so. The stupid lane.
- [[creature.aruhe-crown-squid|Crown Squid]] — forested margins only; the open shoreline is refuge from it.
- [[creature.unsaid-macaw|Unsaid Macaws]] — forested margins only; a perched bird may echo a drinker's unsaid thought in their own voice. Non-hostile; not common.
- Small fish and pale weeds in the top water. Insects at dusk. Nothing in the water hunts.

## Secrets

> [!secret]- Hidden Details
> The Blight's grief does not live in the water as a second phylactery. Destroying the Bloom still ends her. The lake keeps running either way.

## If Left Alone

Animals keep coming to drink. Hunters keep taking the opportunity. Rivers keep cutting [[region.aruhe-the-grasslands|The Grasslands]]. The snag layer keeps what it has. No named imminent change unless the Bloom falls: then the watering hole remains.
