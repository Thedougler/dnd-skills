# The Squeeze

Some routes of [[region.aruhe-the-cave|The Cave]] [[vehicle.the-narrow|Narrow]] to spaces **barely large enough for a humanoid**. Use the normal 5e **squeezing** mechanics rather than inventing a subsystem.

The important regional trick is **choices**:

> Large tunnel: easy movement. [[creature.blackrail|Blackrail]] can follow.
>
> Crawlway: miserable movement. [[creature.blackrail|Blackrail]] cannot.

[[region.aruhe-the-cave|The Cave]] continually asks: **How much comfort are you willing to exchange for safety?**

## Description

> [!narration] Player-facing description — read or adapt at the table.
> The tunnel narrows. Not a dead end — a squeeze: stone on both sides, barely room for a body, a way through if you want it badly enough. A character with a pack may have to push it ahead. A shield may need to be unstrapped. Nobody loses equipment automatically. The pressure is enough.

## Trigger

Choosing to pass through a route that narrows below comfortable width. Passive, route-level — it activates on use, not on proximity.

## Effect

> [!danger] What happens when the hazard triggers.

Standard 5e squeezing mechanics: movement through a space [[vehicle.the-narrow|Narrow]] enough for one creature is difficult terrain, and a creature can't squeeze through a space built for a creature one size smaller or less. A character wearing a pack may have to push it ahead; a shield may need to be unstrapped. **Nobody loses equipment automatically** — the pressure is enough.

The real effect is choice: the crawlway is shelter. The [[creature.blackrail|Blackrail]] cannot follow.

## Detection

Visible on approach — [[faction.the-passage|The Passage]] simply narrows. No roll required.

## Counterplay

> [!tip] Running this hazard — how the party can mitigate, avoid, or neutralize it.

The Squeeze is counterplay, not a tax: the tiny holes the party hated fifteen minutes ago are suddenly safety when the [[creature.blackrail|Blackrail]] commits to a major tube. Claustrophobia becomes shelter.

## Escalation

> [!secret]- What happens if the hazard is ignored or interacted with repeatedly — collapsed by default.

Source is silent. Design intent: the pressure is enough; nobody loses equipment automatically.
