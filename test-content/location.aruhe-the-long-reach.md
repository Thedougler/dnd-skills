# The Long Reach

Where [[region.aruhe-the-river|The River]] comes out of the inner jungle and runs beside one of the great Grassland cuts. Two to three miles of broad, slow, astonishingly clear water. 150 to 400 feet across depending on season. Pale limestone shows beneath ten or fifteen feet of green-blue water. Long ribbons of submerged grass stream downstream. Fallen trees lie visible on the bottom. Sunlight flashes off schools of little silver fish. The water is cool enough that after days in [[region.aruhe|Aruhe]] it feels almost obscenely good.

On one bank: eight-foot grasses. On the other: the hundred-foot jungle comes right down to the water and hangs over it in curtains of roots.

There is no visual sign that anything is wrong. No bones. No blood. No mist. No ominous dead trees.

This is among the prettiest places in the entire [[region.shattered-sea|Shattered Sea]].

The Long Reach is a dangerous river node, not a scheduled encounter or a tonight-specific roster. Its broader route network belongs to the deferred [[dungeon.aruhe-wilderness-megadungeon|Aruhe Wilderness Mega-Dungeon]]; this page owns the local bank-and-water choices, the otter contact, and what [[creature.aruhe-river-otter|The Family]] remembers when the party returns.

## Overview

> [!narration] Arrival
> The canopy tears open and [[region.aruhe-the-river|The River]] widens. White stone shines through green-blue water fifteen feet deep. Grass ribbons stream downstream like hair. Silver fish flash. The air is cooler. The water is loud but not violent — a steady, pleasant rush. Eight-foot grass walls one bank. Hundred-foot roots curtain the other. It looks like the first honest rest since the beach. Stop when they step in, drink, fill skins, or watch the far bank.

## Senses

- **Signature:** Cool spray on the face after days of humid silence. Water loud enough to cover a whisper at ten feet. The look is tropical; the touch is mountain-stream cold.
- **Footing:** Grass bank — difficult terrain, crushed corridors point at what walked them. Jungle bank — root mat, slick stone, vertical drops. Water — smooth limestone, deceptively slippery, current reads slower than it pulls.

## Features

### The pale bottom

- **Obvious:** White limestone through fifteen feet of green-blue. Fallen trees. Fish. Ribbons of weed.
- **Reveals:** Clarity flattens depth. The first shelf reads three feet; it is closer to thirty. Fish stay in the top water.
- **Logic:** This clarity is maintained. Grazers are culled. Shade-trees are felled. [[creature.aruhe-river-otter|The Family]] patrols the margins.
- **Consequence:** A sip at the lip is easy. A step past the first shelf is a swim. Climbing out is the hard part.

### The grass margin

- **Obvious:** Eight-foot blades right to the water's edge. Crushed corridors. Droppings. The grass leans toward the current.
- **Reveals:** [[region.aruhe-the-grasslands|The Grasslands]] start here. [[creature.terror-bird|Terror-Birds]] wait in the rim shade. [[creature.deer-stalker|Deer-Stalkers]] stand in the blades and do not lean with the wind.
- **Logic:** [[region.aruhe-the-river|The River]] is the only reliable heading through the grass. The grass is the only cover from the rim.
- **Consequence:** Walking the grass bank is easy travel and exposure. The water covers sound — including yours.

### The jungle curtain

- **Obvious:** Hundred-foot trunks at the water's edge. Root curtains hang into the current. Vines thick as a wrist.
- **Reveals:** [[region.aruhe-the-quiet|The Quiet]] resumes here. Things hang in the overhang and drop on what passes below — [[creature.aruhe-crown-squid|Crown Squids]] are one of the things that sentence warns about. The canopy rim is a hunting post.
- **Logic:** The jungle never stopped at the water. It leans over it.
- **Consequence:** The jungle bank is cover and difficult terrain. Vertical threat. Things drop from roots.

### [[creature.aruhe-river-otter|The Family]]'s stage

- **Obvious:** Otters. Four to six visible at any time. Twelve feet nose to [[region.the-tail|Tail]]. Dark chocolate-black, tremendously muscular. Pale throat markings individually distinctive. They hold hands while drifting. They groom. They whistle. They roll. Pups wrestle on the bank.
- **Reveals:** They are not feeding. They have already eaten. They are *playing*. The first otter surfaces thirty feet away. Looks. Chirrups. Another head surfaces beside it. One rolls onto its back. One dives. Something tugs the trailing end of somebody's rope.
- **Logic:** Anything entering the water is participating. Anything taken from [[region.aruhe-the-river|The River]] belongs to [[creature.aruhe-river-otter|The Family]]. Anything that responds to play teaches them a new game.
- **Consequence:** If someone goes in to retrieve the rope, the game becomes more interesting. The entire family becomes *excited*. Not enraged. **Excited.**

Treat the contact as a behavior/social-predator puzzle before it becomes combat. Let [[creature.aruhe-river-otter|The Family]] show the sequence — **watch → gear interaction → reaction testing → targeted grapple → hunt switch** — as observable feedback rather than opening with damage. Consistent posture and gestures, calm communication, releasing or recovering gear without a tug-of-war, withdrawing to the bank, and redirecting the game toward a nonessential rope or oar can create a way through; learning [[creature.aruhe-river-otter|The Family]]'s rules is a better answer than trying to defeat it. The targeted grapple is the last non-lethal warning in the play state. [[creature.aruhe-river-otter|The Family]] remembers what works, so a later visit may begin with a different gear test or with less patience.

## Hazards

> [!danger] Active Hazards
> **[[creature.aruhe-river-otter|River Otter Family]]** — the signature threat. Run the first contact as a social-predator puzzle, not an automatic fight; see the creature page for full behavior. Escalation: watch → equipment interaction → reaction testing → targeted grapple → hunt switch (harm one family member = instant lethal coordination). Telegraph the catastrophic transition: whistles shorten and synchronize, pups disappear into the roots, adults stop rolling and fix on a target; then all sound and bodies vanish for ten seconds before the first strike from below. A party that reads the warning can still withdraw or redirect before the switch. Once it fires, use [[creature.aruhe-river-otter|The Family]] unit's hunt mode rather than a normal social check.
>
> **Grassland rim** — [[creature.terror-bird|Terror-Bird]] charge from canopy shade. [[creature.deer-stalker|Deer-Stalker]] still-hunts in blades.
>
> **Jungle rim** — drop attacks from root curtains. Vine-lash analogues.
>
> **Current** — reads slower than it pulls. DC 13 Strength (Athletics) to hold position mid-stream. Failure: moved 20 ft downstream per round.
>
> **Depth lie** — DC 14 Intelligence (Nature) or Wisdom (Survival) to read actual drop before wading. First shelf reads 3 ft, is ~30 ft.

## Inhabitants

- **[[creature.aruhe-river-otter|River Otter Family]]** — 4–6 adults, 2–4 pups. [[creature.aruhe-river-otter|The Family]] that owns this stretch. Dens in the jungle curtain root caverns.
- **Silver fish schools** — abundant, thumb-length, harmless. The otters' primary caloric base alongside larger prey.
- **[[creature.deer-stalker|Deer-Stalkers]]** — visit the grass margin to drink. The otters kill them and play with the heads.
- **[[creature.terror-bird|Terror-Bird]]** — holds the grassland rim quarter-mile. Does not enter water.
- **[[creature.wolfrabbit|Wolfrabbits]]** — hunt the jungle margin at dawn/dusk. Pack launches from terrace walls.

## Secrets

> [!secret]- Hidden Details
> The Long Reach is [[creature.aruhe-river-otter|The Family]]'s *hunting range*, not their den. Their core territory is upriver toward [[location.aruhe-the-clear-lake|The Clear Lake]] — dens in root caverns, cached food, the matriarch.
>
> The otters *know* what the [[creature.deer-stalker|Deer-Stalker]] means to intruders. They have watched parties react to [[creature.deer-stalker|Deer-Stalker]] encounters. They perform the head-toss *for* an audience: a gift, a threat, a demonstration. *"We killed the thing you fear. What are you?"*
>
> The Blight marker here is readable: **unnatural clarity, concentrated biomass, insect silence.** A DC 15 Wisdom (Nature) or Intelligence (Arcana) check reveals the water is *tended* — grazers culled, shade-trees felled, margins patrolled. This is a garden.
>
> Shooting an otter is the fast switch. Staying in the water > 10 minutes is the slow switch (boredom → drowning games). Touching a pup is instant hunt. Casting a spell in the water = curiosity → test → hunt if repeated.
>
> The party can *pass* as "not prey" by learning [[creature.aruhe-river-otter|The Family]]'s specific games. "Not family" ≠ "auto-attack." It means "you are enrichment until proven threat." Survival by playing along — but never safe.

## If Left Alone

[[creature.aruhe-river-otter|The Family]] plays. Pups wrestle. Adults drift holding hands. Fish flash. The water stays gin-clear. The grass stays eight feet tall. The jungle stays hung over the bank. [[region.aruhe-the-river|The River]] remains the best road through [[region.aruhe|Aruhe]] — and the otters remain why that isn't good news. Nothing changes unless the Bloom falls: then the garden may stop being tended, the water may muddy, [[creature.aruhe-river-otter|The Family]] may scatter or hunt differently. [[region.aruhe-the-river|The River]] keeps running either way.
