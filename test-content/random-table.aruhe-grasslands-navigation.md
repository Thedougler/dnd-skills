# Grasslands Navigation Table

## Table

**Roll:** `d8`

| Roll | Result |
|------|--------|
| 1 | **Rim sees first.** Speak: "Hard light hits before the eyes finish. The tree-line is a black wall." For one minute, Perception into shade has disadvantage. A [[creature.terror-bird\|Terror-Bird]] at the rim may already be in position. |
| 2 | **Grass corridor remains.** Speak: "Crushed eight-foot blades point back to the jungle like a road." Mark a return line. Any follower has advantage to track them for the next hour. |
| 3 | **White patch ahead.** Speak: "A flood-scoured hollow is white with blades. No game trail crosses it." [[hazard.razer-grass\|Razer-Grass]]. DC 13 Survival routes around. Failure: 30 minutes or 1d4 slashing per 5 feet. |
| 4 | **River pull.** Speak: "The water is loud and straight through the grass." Following it: one automatic Navigation success toward [[region.aruhe-the-river\|The River]], both banks exposed. |
| 5 | **Still blades.** Speak: "One patch of grass does not lean with the wind." A [[creature.deer-stalker\|Deer-Stalker]] waits there. Approached alone, it lets them pass and follows after dusk. |
| 6 | **Quickeye temptation.** Speak: "[[item.quickeye-berry\|Quickeye Berries]] hang beside a pursuit route." DC 15 Nature or Medicine names the *haste* effect. |
| 7 | **Macaw at the wall.** Speak: an [[creature.unsaid-macaw\|Unsaid Macaw]] repeats one character's unspoken fear from the grass edge. The echo can reveal a disagreement, a planned retreat, or a kept secret. |
| 8 | **Canopy gap saves and sells.** Speak: "Open sky. Nothing can drop from above. The rim has a clear charge lane." Mark one success, then river, grass, or shade. |

> [!tip] Running This Table
> Use one 30-minute Exploration Turn when the party crosses a valley, follows a crushed path, seeks [[region.aruhe-the-river|The River]], or stays under the rim shade. Roll after a failed navigation check.

## Usage Notes

- **Navigation DC:** DC 13 Wisdom (Survival). Use DC 15 when the party leaves [[region.aruhe-the-river|The River]] line inside the grass.
- **Progress:** Three successes before two failures reaches the opposite rim, a river access, or a known grass corridor.
- **Mapping:** Five total successes maps a valley route and one safe Razer-Grass bypass.
- **Failure:** On each failed 30-minute turn, roll this table. Results should use exposure, glare, and grass walls before combat.

## Variants

- In Rot-adjacent valleys, replace result 7 with yellow grass and faint sap-glow at the rim.
