The apex predator of [[region.aruhe-the-cave|The Cave]]: a giant cave centipede, roughly 30–45 feet long, that treats every wall, ceiling, and floor as equally usable. Its polished back looks like parallel strips of wet black iron, and once it commits to a major tube it follows that corridor with horrifying directness. The name can come from sailors much later — not because anyone on [[region.aruhe|Aruhe]] knows what a railway is. It has not become intelligent. That makes it worse.

The thing that makes the metaphor click: [[region.aruhe-the-cave|The Cave]] is the fear of being trapped in a tunnel after the lights go out, and the Blackrail is the oncoming train.

## Ecology

**Size.** Huge monstrosity, roughly 30–45 feet long, enough muscle to move a body several tons in a tunnel.

**Territory.** Solitary. One adult may dominate several miles of interconnected main tube.

**Diet.** Enormous [[creature.cave-bat|Cave Bats]], surface animals fallen through skylights, cave arthropods, anything descending to drink, and occasionally other Blackrails.

**Metabolism.** Comparatively slow when resting. It may go days after a major kill without actively hunting — the ecology works because it does not need a tyrannosaur's daily caloric intake under the island.

**Real basis.** Large *Scolopendra* centipedes are venomous predators capable of taking vertebrates; *Scolopendra gigantea* has been observed climbing cave ceilings to attack bats and hanging from the ceiling while restraining prey heavier than itself using multiple pairs of legs. The Blight simply removes the brakes. ^[inferred — design grounding cited in the source]

Normal centipede: many independently gripping legs, rapid acceleration, flattened body for entering crevices, venomous forcipules, antenna-driven sensing, ability to climb, powerful manipulation of prey with multiple legs, opportunistic predation.

[[region.aruhe|Aruhe]]: **forty feet long**; venom intended for prey the size of deer; every wall, ceiling, and floor equally usable; antennae read air movement; body and legs read vibration through basalt; can hold several struggling creatures simultaneously.

## Behavior

It has **three behaviors**, not random encounter-table aggression:

1. **Listen.** A resting Blackrail presses much of its body against stone and detects walking through the tunnel. Normal movement is noticeable. Running is obvious. Hammering a piton into basalt might as well be ringing a dinner bell.
2. **Approach.** It does not creep when it knows prey cannot escape. The party first feels faint rhythmic vibrations, then dust falls, then comes the leg-rattle. **It gives plenty of warning** — the horror is not "gotcha, monster attacks," it is *it is definitely coming; what do you do with the thirty seconds you have?*
3. **Pin.** Its preferred attack is not biting somebody in half. It rushes the group, curls around a victim using several body segments, pins them to the wall, and applies its forcipules. The venom does the killing. Then it carries the immobilized prey somewhere quiet.

**Senses.** Antennae read air movement; body and legs read vibration through basalt. Its actual eyesight is poor — flight, silence, water, or breaking contact with the rock can meaningfully interfere with it. That gives clever players counterplay.

## Habitat

The interconnected main tubes of [[region.aruhe-the-cave|The Cave]], [[region.aruhe|Aruhe]]. One adult per several miles of main tube.

## Tactics

> [!tip] Running this creature — see the linked statblock for the runnable loop.
Runnable loop: [[creature.blackrail]]. Ecology that is not mechanical:

- **It is an oncoming train encounter.** It cannot turn sharply at full speed. It cannot fit everywhere. The tiny holes the party hated fifteen minutes ago are suddenly safety — [[region.aruhe-the-cave|The Cave]]'s design language: **claustrophobia becomes shelter.**
- It moves at 60 feet per round when committed; the party feels it coming for roughly thirty seconds first.
- It can hold several struggling creatures simultaneously and carries immobilized prey away.

## Variants

**Juvenile Blackrails.** One to three feet long. Dangerous vermin, potentially modeled from the existing **Giant Centipede** stat block with improved HP. The useful ecology detail: **if there are juveniles here, the adult is not** — adult Blackrails are violently cannibalistic toward unrelated young. Players can learn this and make decisions with it.

## Relationships

- **[[region.aruhe-the-cave|The Cave]]** — its entire territory. The region is designed around it: warning cadence, tunnel geometry, crawlway safety.
- **[[region.aruhe-the-river|The River]]** — structural parallel. [[region.aruhe|Aruhe]]'s existing River uses the same trick: the safest, easiest route is also the territory of the thing that dominates that environment. The Blackrail owns [[region.aruhe-the-cave|The Cave]]'s best road ([[location.aruhe-the-great-bore|The Great Bore]]).
- **[[creature.cave-bat|Cave Bats]]** — primary diet. Colonies take flight when something disturbed them farther inside.
- **[[creature.root-roach|Root Roaches]]** / **[[creature.glass-cricket|Glass Crickets]]** — prey and early-warning system: they sense its vibrations before the party does.
- **[[creature.blight|The Blight]]** — the force that removed the size limit: *what if centipedes did not have to remain a reasonable size?* ^[inferred — the source frames the creature as the Blight's "removed the brakes" transformation]
