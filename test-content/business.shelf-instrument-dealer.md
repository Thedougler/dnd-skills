# The Shelf Instrument Dealer

A shop on [[settlement.tidefall|Port Tidefall]]'s Shelf. Captains can buy sextants and spyglasses here. Most other [[region.crown-isles|Crown Islands]] shops order from [[settlement.calveno|Calveno]].

> [!narration] Arrival
> Glass cases hold sextants, compasses, and chronometers. One chronometer has a crack and costs less. Charts and almanacs fill the back. Used items sit on felt by the counter. Just west of the [[location.harbourmasters-office|Harbourmaster's Office]].

## Shopkeeper

**[[npc.oswald-crench|Oswald Crench]]** — Dwarf. Years of peering through lenses made him squint. He calibrates instruments for Crown patrols. He won't chat or haggle.

## Inventory

The dealer keeps practical navigation instruments in stock: spyglasses, sextants, compasses, chronometers, charts, and almanacs. One cracked chronometer and one second-hand calibrated spyglass are discounted; ask Crench about their provenance.

## Geography

The dealer sits just west of the [[location.harbourmasters-office|Harbourmaster's Office]].
