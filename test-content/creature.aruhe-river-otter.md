# Aruhe River Otter

## Overview

> [!narration] Player-facing description — read or adapt at the table.
> A sleek dark shape surfaces thirty feet out. Twelve feet from nose to [[region.the-tail|Tail]]. Heavy-bodied, slick, dark chocolate-black, tremendously muscular through the shoulders. A pale throat marking — unique, like a fingerprint. Clear eyes. Perfect teeth. It looks at you. Chirrups. Another head surfaces beside it. Then another farther downstream. One rolls onto its back. One dives. A few seconds later something tugs the trailing end of your rope.

![[assets/creatures/aruhe-river-otter.png]]

Not a mutant. Not diseased. **Magnificent.** Glossy coat. Fat pups. Strong adults. The Blight didn't ruin them. She perfected them according to a worldview in which nothing outside the family deserves consideration.

## Ecology

Real giant river otters (*Pteronura brasiliensis*) live in cohesive family groups, defend territories, hunt socially, communicate extensively, and organize prey against shorelines. They harass caiman in behavior that appears remarkably game-like. Families collectively attack large caiman that threaten them.

The Blight took everything that makes an otter an otter and removed the ecological brakes:

- **Social cohesion** → xenophobia
- **Curiosity** → experimentation
- **Territoriality** → ownership
- **Intelligence** → cruelty without morality
- **Play** → predation

This family is an especially pure expression of that pressure: devotion becomes xenophobia, play becomes predation, and territoriality becomes ownership.

**Size.** Large. 12 feet nose to [[region.the-tail|Tail]] on a large adult. ~1,200–1,500 lbs. Heavy-bodied, muscular through shoulders and [[region.the-tail|Tail]].

**Family unit.** 4–6 adults, 2–4 pups. [[location.aruhe-the-long-reach|The Long Reach]] family patrols ~3 miles of river. Core territory upriver toward [[location.aruhe-the-clear-lake|The Clear Lake]] (dens in root caverns, cached food, matriarch).

**Diet.** Silver fish schools (abundant, thumb-length), larger fish, capybara-analogues, waterfowl, [[creature.deer-stalker|Deer-Stalkers]] that come to drink. The otters are *keystone engineers* — they cull grazers that would muddy the water, fell shade-trees, patrol margins. [[location.aruhe-the-long-reach|The Long Reach]]'s clarity *is* their garden.

**Biomass.** [[region.aruhe-the-river|The River]] sustains them through engineered productivity. The Blight's garden holds nature at the extreme and never lets it back.

## Behavior

### The Family Boundary

Absolute. Blood kin only. The Blight perfected *otter-ness*; otters don't adopt non-otters. The party can *pass* as "not prey" by learning the family's specific games — but "not family" ≠ "safe." It means **"you are enrichment until proven threat."**

### The Three Rules

1. **Anything entering the water is participating.**
2. **Anything taken from [[region.aruhe-the-river|The River]] belongs to the family.**
3. **Anything that responds to play teaches them a new game.**

These are learnable. The DM runs them as a feedback engine.

### Escalation Ladder (Single Session)

| Stage | Behavior | Party Experience |
|-------|----------|------------------|
| **1. Watch** | Surface 30 ft out. Chirrup. Roll. Dive. | "They're curious." |
| **2. Equipment** | Tug rope. Steal backpack. Pass oar between them. Return it. Take it again. | "They're playing with our gear." |
| **3. Reaction test** | Brush ankle underwater. Rise beside canoe, peer over gunwale. Mimic spell component rattle. | "They're testing us." |
| **4. Targeted grapple** | Pull one PC underwater. Release. Another catches ankle. Surface coughing — otters chirping, rolling, darting. | "They're trying to kill me." → *Realization: they aren't trying to kill me **yet**.* |
| **5. Hunt switch** | Harm one family member → whistles stop, splashing stops, all vanish. 10 sec silence. One PC hit from below. Rest see only wakes. Lethal coordination. | "Oh." |

### The Hunt Switch

**Triggers (any):**
- Deal damage to a family member (fast switch)
- Touch a pup (instant)
- Stay in water > 10 minutes (slow switch — boredom → drowning games)
- Cast spell in/on water → curiosity → test → hunt if repeated
- Enter a den / cross invisible territorial line

**Effect:** Play ends. Military coordination begins. The family operates as a single tactical unit — shared intelligence, flanking, drag-underwater, silence. Five minutes after perceived threat is killed/driven off: pups wrestle again.

### Learning & Memory

The family remembers. Lightweight reputation tags on campaign state (not PC sheets — the *otters* hold the memory):

- `otter:long-reach:knows-ropes` — ropes tied to gear no longer work
- `otter:long-reach:fears-fire` — fire spells cause dive + reposition
- `otter:long-reach:targets-[PC-name]` — specific PC identified as "makes group enter water"
- `otter:long-reach:mimics-[spell-sound]` — they mimic a verbal component

Recognition = scent + behavioral pattern. They remember "the one who casts fire," not "Gorwin the Wizard."

### The [[creature.deer-stalker|Deer-Stalker]] Head

Before the party ever fights them: four otters playing in shallows around something. One dives, catches it, tosses a branching object into the air.

**The antlered head and torn cervical spine of a [[creature.deer-stalker|Deer-Stalker]].**

Not feeding. Already eaten. Playing tug-of-war.

This establishes the food chain using a creature the island has already taught the party to fear. The [[creature.deer-stalker|Deer-Stalker]] avoids larger fauna. **These are what [[region.aruhe-the-river|The River]] monsters are afraid of.**

Performed *for* an audience: a gift, a threat, a demonstration. *"We killed the thing you fear. What are you?"*

## Habitat

**Primary:** [[location.aruhe-the-long-reach|The Long Reach]] — broad, slow, gin-clear stretch over white limestone. 150–400 ft wide, 10–15 ft deep.

**Range:** ~3 miles of river. Core territory upriver toward [[location.aruhe-the-clear-lake|The Clear Lake]] (dens, pups, matriarch, cached food).

**Banks:** Grassland side (exposure, [[creature.terror-bird|Terror-Bird]], [[creature.deer-stalker|Deer-Stalker]]). Jungle side (cover, vertical drops, root-curtain drops). The family does not haul prey onto banks where [[hazard.razer-grass|Razer-Grass]] grows — a kill left there would cut the ones coming back for it.

**Water:** Fastest travel. Cool. Clear. **Otter territory.**

## Tactics

> [!tip] Running this creature — see the linked statblock for the runnable loop.
> **Runnable loop:**  (family unit as single combat entity: shared HP pool, distributed multiattack, coordinated grapple/drag). Ecology that is not mechanical: watch → equipment → reaction test → targeted grapple → hunt switch (harm one = instant lethal coordination) → 5 min later pups wrestle.

### Running the Escalation

**Stage 1–3 (Play):** One initiative for the family. Actions distributed: *Observe* (Perception), *Interact* (Sleight of Hand / Athletics to tug/steal), *Test* (Insight / contested check). No damage. Goal: learn party reactions.

**Stage 4 (Grapple):** *Coordinated Grapple* — two otters grapple one target, third drags underwater, fourth positions for ankle-catch on release. Non-lethal. Goal: see what the party *does* when someone goes under.

**Stage 5 (Hunt):** Family becomes a **single tactical entity**. Shared initiative. *Silent Coordination* — flanking, drag-underwater, hold-breath tracking, lethal bites. Target priority: whoever harmed family → whoever enters water → isolated targets.

**After hunt:** 5 minutes → pups wrestle. The switch is *clean*. No rage. No mourning. The family resumes.

## Variants

**Long Reach Family** (detailed above) — 4–6 adults, 2–4 pups. Throat markings: matriarch = trident, others = unique.

**Lake Core Family** — larger (6–8 adults), more pups, matriarch is Huge (15 ft). Dens in root caverns at [[location.aruhe-the-clear-lake|The Clear Lake]]. More cautious; they *are* the territory.

**Downriver Families** — smaller (2–3 adults), more skittish, less coordinated. [[location.aruhe-the-long-reach|The Long Reach]] family dominates [[region.aruhe-the-river|The River]] hierarchy.

## Relationships

- **[[creature.blight|Druid Lich]]** — the corruption that perfected the otter.
- **[[creature.deer-stalker|Deer-Stalker]]** — prey. Plaything. The otters kill them and toss the heads.
- **[[creature.terror-bird|Terror-Bird]]** / **[[creature.bear-elk|Bear-Elk]]** — things the otters avoid on land. In water, the otters dominate.
- **[[creature.aruhe-crown-squid|Crown Squid]]** — ecological boundary. A grabber trailed into occupied water meets six enormous otters pulling the other direction; mature squids treat open water as unsafe.
- **[[creature.grubnade|Grubnade]]** — the one thing the family sniffs, recoils from, and leaves alone. Food-web intelligence: they recognize and respond to the island's creatures rather than eating everything.
- **[[hazard.razer-grass|Razer-Grass]]** — avoided as a haul-out. They cross water, not the white stands.
- **[[region.aruhe-the-river|The River]], [[location.aruhe-the-long-reach|The Long Reach]], [[location.aruhe-the-clear-lake|The Clear Lake]]** — engineered garden, hunting range, core territory.
