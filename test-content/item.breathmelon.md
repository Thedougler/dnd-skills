# Breathmelon

> [!narration] Appearance
> A pale green melon so light it floats in the shallows, rind dimpled like a lung. Thumped, it rings hollow. Cut open, the flesh is a lattice of air-cells that squeak when compressed.

## Properties

- **Type:** Fruit (consumable)
- **Rarity:** Rare
- **Attunement:** No
- Consuming takes an Action. For 1 hour you can **breathe air and water**, and your **swim speed equals your walking speed**.

## Biology

Gas-filled aerenchyma — the air-tissue that lets wetland plants breathe underwater — exaggerated into whole-fruit buoyancy and gas transport. A floating, breathing fruit grown from a plant's own wetland mechanism. The [[creature.blight|Blight]] removed the brakes, not the design.

## Identification

DC 13 Nature or Medicine (1 minute): broad physiological function — a fruit that lets you breathe where you cannot. DC 15: exact effect. *Identify* spell: exact effect automatically. Environmental tell: a floating Breathmelon visibly releases trapped gas underwater.

## Placement

Found immediately before dangerous river travel — and before [[creature.aruhe-river-otter|River Otter]] territory. [[region.aruhe-the-river|The River]] is already [[region.aruhe-the-river|Aruhe]]'s best route, and the otters drag prey underwater. The fruit that answers the next problem sits at its threshold.

## Abundance

Abundant near suitable wetlands — [[region.aruhe-the-river|The River]] margins where the island's own water-road tempts travellers to take the one thing that makes it survivable.
