# River Navigation Table

## Table

**Roll:** `d8`

| Roll | Result |
|------|--------|
| 1 | **Otters hold hands.** Speak: "A family of [[creature.aruhe-river-otter\|Aruhe River Otters]] drifts thirty feet out and watches gear, hands, and ropes." If the party plays back, the otters learn a new game. |
| 2 | **Rope tug.** Speak: "Something below takes a trailing rope, paddle, or strap and pulls." Release it, win DC 14 Athletics, or enter the water. Harm to an otter turns play into the hunt. |
| 3 | **[[item.breathmelon\|Breathmelon]] at the exact wrong place.** Speak: "[[item.breathmelon\|Breathmelon]] floats at the crossing you would rather not stop in." DC 13 Nature or Medicine names the water-breathing benefit. |
| 4 | **Cataract voice.** Speak: "[[region.aruhe-the-river|The River]] sound deepens behind a bend. The water is telling the truth." DC 14 Survival names a required portage before boat or swimmers commit. Failure: 30 minutes and risk of separation in rapids. |
| 5 | **Too-clear pool.** Speak: "The water drops into a deep pool where sound dies." Wait, detour along a bank, or cross with disadvantage on hearing-based checks this turn. |
| 6 | **Jungle bank drops a shadow.** Speak: "Root curtains hang over the water. Something waits in them." A [[creature.aruhe-crown-squid\|Crown Squid]] or other vertical hunter. It will not extend far over deep otter water unless starved. |
| 7 | **Grass bank shakes.** Speak: "River noise covers the first steps. The grass on the bank moves against the current." [[creature.terror-bird\|Terror-Bird]] charge. DC 14 Perception by the Watch spots it. |
| 8 | **Tended reach evidence.** Speak: "Cut shade trees. Fish packed in one hole. No grazers." [[region.aruhe-the-river\|The River]] is gardened. Mark one Navigation success and one lore clue about [[creature.aruhe-river-otter\|The Family]]. |

> [!tip] Running This Table
> Use one 30-minute Exploration Turn for swimming, boating, portage, bank travel, or route study on [[region.aruhe-the-river|The River]]. Roll after a failed navigation check or social mistake with the otters.

## Usage Notes

- **Navigation DC:** DC 13 Wisdom (Survival) from the bank, DC 14 Dexterity with water vehicles in current, or DC 14 Athletics for swimmers.
- **Progress:** Three successes before two failures finds the next safe landing, portage, or channel seam.
- **Mapping:** Five total successes maps a reach, including one safe landing and one hazard to avoid.
- **Failure:** On each failed 30-minute turn, roll this table. [[region.aruhe-the-river|The River]] should stay beautiful and useful even when it becomes dangerous.

## Variants

- In [[location.aruhe-the-long-reach|The Long Reach]], treat results 1 and 2 as the default first contact.
