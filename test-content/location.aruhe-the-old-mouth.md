# The Old Mouth

One huge collapsed entrance near the lower [[region.aruhe-the-old-gardens|Old Gardens]]. This may be how the party first discovers [[region.aruhe-the-cave|The Cave]]. The terraces have partly fallen into it.

> [!narration] Arrival
> The terrace wall has fallen into the hillside — a mouth of black stone big enough to swallow a house, with the cut terraces tilting down into it. Daylight reaches only so far, and then the black begins. Along one wall, rabbit warrens run up to the light line and stop.

## Senses

- **Signature:** The terraces tilting into darkness. [[creature.wolfrabbit|Wolfrabbit]] warrens ending in a straight line.
- **Footing:** Collapsed terrace rubble descending into the tube.

## Features

- **The collapsed terraces:**
  - **Obvious:** A huge collapsed entrance; the terraces have partly fallen into it.
  - **Reveals:** An entrance to [[region.aruhe-the-cave|The Cave]] beneath [[region.aruhe-the-old-gardens|The Old Gardens]] — possibly the party's first discovery of the region.
  - **Logic:** Terrace collapses break into shallow tubes beneath the gardens.
  - **Consequence:** The largest known entrance; the natural way in.

- **[[location.the-warren|The Warren]] line:**
  - **Obvious:** [[creature.wolfrabbit|Wolfrabbit]] warrens stop abruptly along one wall at the point where daylight disappears.
  - **Reveals:** To experienced [[region.aruhe|Aruhe]] players, something before they have seen anything underground: **whatever lives down there outranks the thing that hunts up here.**
  - **Logic:** [[creature.wolfrabbit|Wolfrabbits]] refuse to den below the point where daylight disappears.
  - **Consequence:** A free piece of information about the [[creature.blackrail|Blackrail]] — delivered before the party has seen the creature.

## Hazards

> [!danger] Active Hazards
> Source is silent on mechanical hazards at the mouth itself. Below the light line is [[region.aruhe-the-cave|The Cave]] with all its hazards.

## Inhabitants

- [[creature.wolfrabbit|Wolfrabbits]] — warren the terraces above the light line; their dens stop at the dark.
- [[creature.blackrail|The Blackrail]] — outranks the [[creature.wolfrabbit|Wolfrabbits]]; the reason for [[location.the-warren|The Warren]] line.

## Secrets

> [!secret]- Hidden Details
> [[location.the-warren|The Warren]] line is [[region.aruhe-the-cave|The Cave]]'s first lesson: observation is free. The party does not need to descend to learn that something worse than a [[creature.wolfrabbit|Wolfrabbit]] lives down there.

## If Left Alone

The terraces keep slumping into the mouth; [[creature.wolfrabbit|Wolfrabbits]] keep their dens above the light line. No named imminent change.
