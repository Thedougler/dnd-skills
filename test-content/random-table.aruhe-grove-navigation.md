# Grove Navigation Table

## Table

**Roll:** `d8`

| Roll | Result |
|------|--------|
| 1 | **Mourner line watches.** [[creature.terror-bird\|Terror-Birds]], [[creature.corpsewood\|Corpsewood]], and canopy predators stand at the boundary and do not cross. Any violent move may make the line answer. |
| 2 | **Black flowers ring the grass.** The ring is the mourner line. DC 14 Nature or Religion reads it as a threshold, not a trap. |
| 3 | **Two graves refuse roots.** A careful search finds that no root crosses either grave. DC 15 Nature or Medicine reveals that the refusal is deliberate and biologically wrong. Mark one lore success. |
| 4 | **Fruit over the graves.** Perfect fruit hangs where it should age. It is not stronger than other [[region.aruhe\|Aruhe]] fruit. |
| 5 | **Lakeward shelf tempts exit.** The [[location.aruhe-the-clear-lake\|Clear Lake]] shows through the trees. Climbing down leaves the last room and returns to lake and Rot pressure. It is not a second door into [[region.aruhe-the-grove\|The Grove]]. |
| 6 | **The ground knows names.** A root pulse stops under one character's feet, then withdraws. Recognition, not a combat cue. |
| 7 | **The Blight does not enter yet.** [[creature.blight\|Hinewai]] remains offstage unless the tree is struck, a grave is violated, or the party calls to her. Let silence and the memorial answer first. |
| 8 | **Stillness gives time.** Nothing attacks. The party gets 30 minutes to rest, study, argue, sketch, pray, or leave. Mark one success toward understanding [[region.aruhe-the-grove\|The Grove]]. |

> [!tip] Running This Table
> Use one 30-minute Exploration Turn only when the party searches, rests, tests the boundary, studies the graves, or considers the fruit in [[region.aruhe-the-grove|The Grove]]. Do not use it as a wandering-monster table.

## Usage Notes

- **Navigation DC:** DC 13 Wisdom (Survival) to keep a Rotward exit line. Use Intelligence (Investigation), Nature, Medicine, or Religion for Grove understanding.
- **Progress:** Three successes before two failures finds the safe exit, reads the memorial, or identifies the next meaningful choice.
- **Mapping:** Five total successes maps [[region.aruhe-the-grove|The Grove]] boundary and one return line to [[region.aruhe-the-rot|The Rot]].
- **Failure:** On each failed 30-minute turn, roll this table. Most failures should reveal sharper meaning or raise tension, not start combat.

## Variants

- If the Bloom is Wounded or Failing, replace result 8 with a visible fracture in the memorial state.
