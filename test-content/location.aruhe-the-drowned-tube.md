# The Drowned Tube

A major tunnel intersected by groundwater. Its black surface fills [[faction.the-passage|The Passage]] **from wall to wall**. No current. No visible far shore. The roof continues **four feet above the water and slowly descends**. The route definitely continues underwater.

This should **not** be a mandatory path. It exists to make the players voluntarily say: **No.**

That is valuable in horror.

> [!narration] Arrival
> The tunnel ahead is full of water — wall to wall, black, still. No current. No far shore visible. The roof continues four feet above the surface and slowly descends until it meets it. Whatever the route does next, it does underwater. The choice is yours.

## Senses

- **Signature:** Absolute stillness. Black water that gives no hint of depth or what it holds.
- **Footing:** The tube floor ends at the waterline; the way forward is swimming.

## Features

- **The water:**
  - **Obvious:** Black water fills [[faction.the-passage|The Passage]] wall to wall. No current. No visible far shore.
  - **Reveals:** The route continues underwater — the roof descends to meet it.
  - **Logic:** Groundwater intersected a major tube.
  - **Consequence:** Passing means committing to an underwater continuation with no visible end, no known length, and no guarantee of air.

- **The descending roof:**
  - **Obvious:** Four feet of clearance at the waterline, slowly dropping.
  - **Reveals:** The airspace is going away.
  - **Logic:** The roof continues down toward the water's surface.
  - **Consequence:** The decision point is explicit: turn back, or commit to the submerged route.
