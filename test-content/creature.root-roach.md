# Root Roach

Hand-sized pale cockroaches clustered around the Root Wells of [[region.aruhe-the-cave|The Cave]]. Mostly harmless. They are valuable **environmental information**.

## Overview

> [!narration] Player-facing description — read or adapt at the table.
Source is silent.

## Ecology

They cluster around root wells, where roots penetrate from the surface and cave arthropods feed. Part of the imported-energy food web: roots → root-feeding insects → predatory arthropods.

## Behavior

Mostly harmless — but they **vanish into cracks seconds before a [[creature.blackrail|Blackrail]] arrives**, because they sense vibration before the party does. That makes them a free early-warning system for anyone who notices them go.

## Habitat

Root wells throughout [[region.aruhe-the-cave|The Cave]].
