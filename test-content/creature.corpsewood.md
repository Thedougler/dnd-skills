## Overview

> [!narration] Player-facing description — read or adapt at the table.
> A dead tree walking. Forty feet tall, bark blackened and split, every branch bare. The roots have pulled free of the ground and drag behind it like a skirt of cables. It moves at a walk, never faster, and the ground shakes with each step. The wood died years ago. The thing moving it did not.

## Ecology

The Blight's corruption does not need living tissue. A corpsewood is a dead tree reanimated through the root network, powered by the same corruption that feeds the Death Bloom. It walks because the Blight walks it, patrolling the deep interior on a route that changes with the season. Fire stops its regeneration. Nothing else does.

## Behavior

It moves at a walk, never faster. Patrol route changes with the season. It does not leave the tree line.

## Habitat

Home zones: [[region.aruhe-the-rot|The Rot]] into [[region.aruhe-the-grove|The Grove]] on [[region.aruhe|Aruhe]]. The deep interior only. The party encounters them on the approach to the central grove, where the corruption is thickest and the ground itself resists passage.
