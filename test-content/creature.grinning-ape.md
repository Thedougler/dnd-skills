## Overview

> [!narration] Player-facing description — read or adapt at the table.
Great apes the size of a gorilla, dark-furred, moving through the terrace canopy on knuckles and feet. The Blight's corruption locked every facial muscle into a permanent rictus grin. Lips pulled back over teeth. Eyes wide and fixed. The expression never changes. They sit in the canopy in groups of three to five, grinning down at whatever moves below.

## Ecology

The corruption took a social primate and stripped out everything social. Grinning apes no longer groom or vocalize. They sit in the canopy until something moves below them. Then they drop as a group. The rictus carries no aggression. The corruption locked the muscles, and the face has nowhere left to go. The grin means nothing, which makes it worse.

## Behavior

Groups of three to five. They throw stones from above before closing. They do not groom or vocalize.

## Habitat

Home zones: [[region.aruhe-the-old-gardens|The Old Gardens]] into [[region.aruhe-the-quiet|The Quiet]] on [[region.aruhe|Aruhe]]. The terrace canopy gives them elevation and cover.
