# Old Gardens Navigation Table

## Table

**Roll:** `d8`

| Roll | Result |
|------|--------|
| 1 | **Terrace crust gives way.** Speak: "The stone underfoot sounds hollow, then the riser drops." DC 13 Dexterity avoids a 10-foot fall, 3 (1d6) bludgeoning, and prone. A dark warren mouth opens. [[creature.wolfrabbit\|Wolfrabbit]] work. |
| 2 | **Fruit solves the problem.** Speak: "[[item.redheart-berry\|Redheart Berries]] or [[item.lion-citrus\|Lion Citrus]] hang exactly beside the block in the path." DC 13 Nature or Medicine names the benefit. |
| 3 | **Warren breath.** Speak: "Warm air moves from a collapsed wall, animal-close." Following it bypasses one exposed riser and enters cramped inhabited stone. Small scout: advantage on the next Navigation check. Medium armored scout: disadvantage. |
| 4 | **Vine waits over the only handhold.** Speak: "The only climb is a rope of green. Clean bones lie under it." A [[creature.vine-lash\|Vine Lash]] among ordinary vines. DC 14 Wisdom (Perception) spots the bones. Failure gives it the first grab. |
| 5 | **Unsaid witness.** Speak: an [[creature.unsaid-macaw\|Unsaid Macaw]] repeats a character's unspoken worry in that character's voice. It is not hostile. Let the exposed thought change the next choice. |
| 6 | **Hot bloom.** Speak: "One huge flower ticks. Predators walk around it." Three mature [[creature.grubnade\|Grubnades]] feed inside. DC 13 Wisdom (Survival) reads the detour. Failure disturbs the flower. |
| 7 | **Grinning watchers.** Speak: "Three to five apes sit above and copy the last hand sign." If ignored, they throw fruit and stones to test reactions. If attacked, they drop as a group. |
| 8 | **The Broken Terrace opens.** Speak: "The upper junction shows through the trees. The three paths rejoin." Mark one Navigation success. Watch 30 minutes: also learn one safe rejoin for a later map. |

> [!tip] Running This Table
> Use one 30-minute Exploration Turn for a climb, ditch route, warren route, or upper-terrace search. Roll after a failed navigation check, or when the party stops to test the fruit.

## Usage Notes

- **Navigation DC:** DC 13 Wisdom (Survival). Use DC 14 for the flooded ditch in bad light or after rain.
- **Progress:** Three successes before two failures reaches the Broken Terrace, the tree line, or a known return line.
- **Mapping:** Five total successes maps one Gardens route: terrace stair, flooded ditch, or collapsed warrens.
- **Failure:** On each failed 30-minute turn, roll this table. A result can still reveal a useful route clue.
