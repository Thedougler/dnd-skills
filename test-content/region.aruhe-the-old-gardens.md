# The Old Gardens

Dark cut-stone terraces climb the slope above [[region.aruhe|Aruhe]]'s beach, collapsed and swallowed by growth that has gone past garden and into appetite. No chart remembers who cut the risers or channeled the ditches. [[creature.grung|Grung]] who named the island never landed to look closer; the name is what a passing eye can tell from the water: terraces once tended, now wrong.

This is where [[region.aruhe|Aruhe]]'s theme first reads at arm's length. Everything that still grows here is a hyper form of itself — larger, hungrier, slightly off. Citrus hangs too heavy. Yam vines are rope-thick. Ordinary terrace plants have the posture of things that want.

![[assets/places/aruhe-the-old-gardens.jpeg]]

## Overview

> [!narration] Overview
> Dark wet stone climbs the landward face in broken steps, from the shingle below to a tree line already eating the upper walls. Flooded ditches sit between the levels, still and green. Yam vines thick as a wrist and citrus the size of a fist hang in every gap, too ripe, too many. The air tastes of fruit that has gone sweet toward rot. The only climb is through the collapsed risers. Stop when they choose a riser, a ditch, or the tree line above.

No one holds this ground. The food is real.

## Geography

The terrace country sits on [[region.aruhe|Aruhe]]'s western landward face behind [[region.aruhe-the-beach|The Beach]]: several miles of broken elevation to the tree line of [[region.aruhe-the-quiet|The Quiet]], and tens of miles along that face. A day's climb in most places. The risers were cut true once. Roots found every seam and pulled the work apart. What remains is dark wet stone, vine-choked steps, and gaps in the walls that double as the only inland stair from this landing. Upland, the same freshwater opens [[region.aruhe-the-grasslands|The Grasslands]] before it reaches these ditches.

Flooded irrigation ditches run between levels. The water stands, thick with algae and drowned blossom. Some stretches are easier to wade than to walk the broken stone. Others hide how far the next drop is.

The land is still working. Fruit sets and drops faster than anything should eat it. Vines take another riser every season. [[creature.wolfrabbit|Wolfrabbit]] warrens hollow the walls from the inside, so a terrace that looks solid can be a crust over a den. Growth here is not recovery. It is excess: color too saturated, leaves too large, fruit hanging as if it leaned toward the path.

Lateral travel follows a single level. Climbing uses the collapsed risers. The tree line above is the handoff into closed canopy.

## Route logic

These local routes are the Old Gardens slice of the deferred [[dungeon.aruhe-wilderness-megadungeon|Aruhe Wilderness Mega-Dungeon]]. This page owns the terrain, loop, and access consequences; the dungeon page can later compose them with [[region.aruhe|Aruhe]]'s island-scale route network.

### Lower Gardens loop

The Lower Gardens are a temptation area, not a corridor. The beach-side terraces and the upper junction are connected by three routes that rejoin around **The Broken Terrace**. The loop makes the party choose what kind of exposure it accepts, and lets a later expedition use a different answer without replaying the first climb.

| Route | Advantage | Cost |
|---|---|---|
| **Terrace stair** | Fast, obvious ascent with the clearest sight of the next riser | Exposed to watchers and the [[creature.wolfrabbit\|Wolfrabbit]] and [[creature.vine-lash\|Vine Lash]] territories |
| **Flooded irrigation ditch** | Concealed lateral movement and cover below the retaining walls | Water, slick footing, and drops hidden under algae and drowned blossom |
| **Collapsed warrens** | Shortest line through the broken wall and a bypass of the most exposed risers | Cramped, unstable, and inhabited; a solid-looking span may be a den roof |

The three lines are meaningful alternatives rather than three entrances to separate scenes. A party can change routes at the lower terraces or where they rejoin near the Broken Terrace; discovering a safe mouth, firm crossing, or overlooked riser is a durable access gain. None of the routes becomes safe merely because it is known.

### Upper Gardens

The Upper Gardens [[vehicle.the-narrow|Narrow]] against the tree line. Fallen fruit remains where it dropped. This is terrain owned by the Gardens, not a fixed rescue scene. [[situation.aruhes-hungry-isle|Aruhe's Hungry Isle]] decides whether a castaway, testimony, or another live pressure is present.

### The Broken Terrace

The Broken Terrace is the upper route junction and a decision surface: open stone, broken retaining walls, and several lines of sight make waiting, watching, withdrawing, or changing routes viable choices. It is also a territorial predator site. [[creature.wolfrabbit|Wolfrabbit]] signs and den mouths mark the local claim, but this page does not place a tonight-specific roster or prescribe an encounter.

The territory responds to intrusion and behavior. The party can observe the boundary, skirt it by ditch or warren, retreat, or defend itself if a predator attacks. A fight is one possible player choice, not the required resolution; any specific cast, stakes, and encounter procedure belong to the situation or encounter owner, while this region owns the terrain, territory, and route consequences.

### Route mastery

Garden routes retain the island's durable access states. Mastery removes repeated navigation friction, not the Gardens' living choices:

| Route state | Access consequence in the Old Gardens |
|---|---|
| **Unknown** | The party pays the full climb cost and must read the terrace, ditch, or warren as it goes. |
| **Observed** | The route mouth and destination are known; the party can choose among the three lines deliberately. |
| **Mapped** | Navigation failure is largely removed, and the party can switch at a known rejoin point. |
| **Mastered** | The climb compresses to a known circuit with a reliable refuge or camp; returning does not replay the first discovery. |
| **Shortcut discovered** | A mapped ditch crossing or warren mouth bypasses an exposed riser or terrace section. |

Route knowledge does not tame a territorial predator or waive collapse or footing hazards. It gives the party informed access and the power to spend time and exposure on the route it prefers.

## Climate

Humid slope. Stone stays slick after [[region.aruhe-the-beach|The Beach]] mist burns off. Shade thickens with every rise; the upper terraces sit in green gloom while the lowest still take sun. Standing water holds the heat and the smell. By afternoon the fruit-sweet air is close enough to taste. Wind dies as the jungle takes the top.

## Peoples

None. Cultivation ended when the terraces collapsed. [[creature.grung|Grung]] patrol the reefs and will not set foot on the first riser.

## Threats

> [!danger] Active Threats
> The garden hunts. `[[creature.wolfrabbit]]` warrens honeycomb every wall and launch from elevation. [[creature.vine-lash|Vine Lash]] hangs from canopy anchors, shoulder-thick and leafless, indistinguishable from the other rope-vines until something walks under. [[creature.grinning-ape|Grinning Apes]] sit in the terrace canopy in groups of three to five and drop as a group. Giant spiders web the brush between levels. [[creature.grubnade|Grubnades]] feed in the absurd flowers, citrus, and yams — the exact food supply they want; a flower holding three or more mature ones is a hot bloom. The Broken Terrace is a territorial site, not a guaranteed fight; the party's route and response determine whether the territory becomes a confrontation.

**Terrace collapse.** Warrens have hollowed the stonework. When a creature heavier than 100 pounds crosses an undermined riser, DC 13 Dexterity or fall 10 feet: 3 (1d6) bludgeoning, prone, rubble in a 10-foot radius of difficult terrain. The collapse opens a warren; 1d4 [[creature.wolfrabbit|wolfrabbits]] emerge the following round.

**The fruit.** Feral yams and citrus on every level, oversized and unnervingly perfect. Eating heals 1d4 hit points per piece. The ordinary produce is not poisoned, cursed, or secretly weakened, and the miracle fruits work exactly as their item pages state. Ordinary 1d4-healing terrace produce is the baseline: [[item.redheart-berry|Redheart Berries]] (8d4+8 HP) and [[item.lion-citrus|Lion Citrus]] (temp HP + *bless*) grow abundant here.

**Upper terraces.** Mature [[creature.aruhe-crown-squid|Crown Squids]] are usually absent here — the trees are too interrupted — but juveniles occasionally hunt the upper terraces.

**[[creature.snakewood|Snakewood]] juveniles.** Younger [[creature.snakewood|Snakewoods]] can hunt the terraces; the sixty-foot adults stay farther inland so [[creature.snakewood|Snakewood]] never replaces [[creature.vine-lash|Vine Lash]] here.

**[[creature.unsaid-macaw|Unsaid Macaws]].** Non-hostile residents of the feral orchards — the primary habitat for the species. A macaw reads the foremost surface thought of a visible, language-knowing creature within 30 ft. and repeats it aloud in the thinker's exact voice. The first echo reads like another garden threat; then it returns to preening.

## Landmarks

- **Terrace stair of broken risers** — the only climbable way inland from [[region.aruhe-the-beach|The Beach]]. Gaps, not a kept stair. Same dark stone visible from the landing.
- **Flooded ditches** — old irrigation, now standing water between levels. Cover, bad footing, and a quieter lateral path.
- **Lower Gardens loop** — the terrace stair, flooded irrigation ditch, and collapsed warrens connect the lower landing to the upper junction by different costs and exposures.
- **Upper Gardens** — narrowed, greener terraces against the tree line.
- **The Broken Terrace** — an open, collapsed junction where the three routes rejoin and a territorial predator claim must be read or avoided.
- **Feral orchards** — yam and citrus still fruiting in the vine, too heavy and too bright.
- **Warren mouths** — burrows in every terrace wall, larger than a rabbit's den ought to be. Dark, packed, used.
- **[[location.aruhe-the-old-mouth|The Old Mouth]]** — one huge collapsed cave entrance near the lower terraces. Terrace collapses break into shallow tubes of [[region.aruhe-the-cave|The Cave]]; this may be how the party first discovers [[region.aruhe-the-cave|The Under-Region]].
- **Tree line** — where canopy closes and [[region.aruhe-the-quiet|The Quiet]] begins. Sight dies. Sound is already thinning.

> [!secret]- Secrets
> The terraces were tended by someone; the notes do not name them. The hyper-growth is the [[creature.blight|Druid Lich]]'s garden still running without a gardener — nature held at the extreme, not wilderness returning to balance.
