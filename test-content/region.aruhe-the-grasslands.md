# The Grasslands

River-cut valleys in [[region.aruhe|Aruhe]]'s jungle, not a sixth concentric band. Freshwater forces the hundred-foot canopy apart. Light reaches the floor for miles along the water. Grass takes the hole.

This is the island's first sky after the beach, and it is bait. The mute dark of [[region.aruhe-the-quiet|The Quiet]] breaks. Heat and glare arrive together. Eight-foot blades close the floor again. Something already stands in the shade at the rim.

![[assets/places/aruhe-the-grasslands.jpeg]]

## Overview

> [!narration] Overview
> The canopy tears open. A river has cut a valley of hard noon light out of the hundred-foot trees, and the sky is suddenly there. Eight-foot grass fills the floor, gold-green, thick as a wall, running as far as the water runs. After the mute jungle the water is loud. The grass nearest [[region.aruhe-the-river|The River]] leans. Further in, some of it does not. Stop when they step into the light, follow the water, or stay under the trees.

## Geography

Not a prairie and not a single meadow. Wherever a river has enough fetch to keep the canopy from closing, a valley opens — a few hundred yards to a mile or more across, miles long along the water. Older charts mark freshwater on [[region.aruhe|Aruhe]]. These openings are that water, seen from inside.

The process is simple. Rivers drain [[location.aruhe-the-clear-lake|The Clear Lake]] toward the coast. Trunks cannot close over moving water. Light hits soil that the closed jungle never feeds, and grass answers at the island's usual excess: blades eight feet tall, thick enough to hide a standing person, packed so tight that a body moving through them leaves a crushed corridor. [[region.aruhe-the-river|The River]] is the only reliable heading and the only clear sightline. Leave the water and the field is a second jungle that happens to be gold.

Most valleys open in [[region.aruhe-the-quiet|The Quiet]]. A few persist into [[region.aruhe-the-rot|The Rot]], where the grass yellows and the rim sap-glows. Downstream the cuts thin toward [[region.aruhe-the-old-gardens|The Old Gardens]]; they do not become terrace ditches.

Occasionally a valley holds a low, waist-high island of strange whiteness: [[hazard.razer-grass|Razer-Grass]] colonies, grown where animals have repeatedly disturbed the ground — crossings, old wallows, river bends, flood-scoured clearings, ground flattened by a [[creature.terror-bird|Terror-Bird]] charge. Every trail bends around them. ^[inferred — habitat preference proposed by the design, not yet observed in play]

[[hazard.stillbloom|Stillbloom]] does not grow through the main valleys. That conceptual space belongs to [[hazard.razer-grass|Razer-Grass]]: the beautiful field cuts you, while Stillbloom's claim — the field is perfectly safe, just don't move — belongs to the darker Quiet/Rot transition. ^[inferred — design intent from the Stillbloom draft]

The land is still working. The work is a trap that looks like relief. Sky, heat, and running water after days of mute shade. Then the grass, and the dark wall of trunks that never stopped watching the hole.

## Play Logic

The Grasslands are river-created openings, not a sixth concentric band. Each valley is a giant exposed chamber in the jungle's route network: it connects enclosed approaches along the water, then reverses the concealment that made [[region.aruhe-the-quiet|The Quiet]] dangerous. In the jungle, the party cannot see what is near; here, everything at the shaded perimeter can see entrants in the open. The broad travel and dungeon topology remain deferred to [[dungeon.aruhe-wilderness-megadungeon|Aruhe Wilderness Mega-Dungeon]]; this page owns the valley's local spatial logic.

Grass is still a wall rather than an open floor: it is difficult terrain and heavily obscures beyond 10 feet. Movement makes a legible crushed trail, so concealment leaves evidence. The shaded canopy rim is where predators wait, and the glare makes the dark-to-open transition dangerous until eyes adjust. [[hazard.razer-grass|Razer-Grass]] is not a blanket biome; it appears as discrete, telegraphed patches that can be recognized and routed around before anyone treats the beautiful field as safe.

## Climate

Sudden glare after closed canopy. Heat sits in the valley at midday and does not move. [[region.aruhe-the-river|The River]] is loud enough to cover a step, a breath, a charge until it is close. After rain the blades slick and the valleys flood at the banks. Night does not empty the grass. It only takes the sky back.

## Peoples

None live here. Cultivation never reached these cuts. [[faction.grung-clans|Grung Clans]] named the mute jungle from the terrace edge and turned back; they have no name for [[region.aruhe-the-river|The River]] valleys. The nearest settlement is still [[region.karath|Karath]], a country away across the interior and a half-mile channel.

## Threats

> [!danger] Active Threats
> The light is the lie. [[creature.deer-stalker|Deer-Stalkers]] stand in the grass and do not lean with the wind, then close on whoever walks off alone after dark. [[creature.terror-bird|Terror-Birds]] wait in the shaded rim, mossed and still, and charge anything that breaks cover in the open. [[region.aruhe-the-river|The River]] covers sound. [[creature.grubnade|Grubnades]] occur occasionally, especially inside large flowering plants along [[region.aruhe-the-river|The River]] cuts — never in huge numbers.

**Glare.** After travel under sealed canopy, Perception checks into the tree-line shade are at disadvantage until a creature has spent one minute in the valley. The rim sees first.

**Grass walls.** Eight-foot blades. Difficult terrain. While in the grass, the area is heavily obscured beyond 10 feet. A creature already in the grass has advantage on Dexterity (Stealth) checks. Crushed corridors last hours and point at whatever walked them.

**[[hazard.razer-grass|Razer-Grass]]** — waist-high stands of white, glass-armored grass. The blades cut (1d4 per 5 ft moved); breaking a stand shatters loudly and blooms glass dust (DC 14). Soaked stands do not bloom; fire detonates them. No game trail crosses a stand. The fauna know it; the party will learn.

**Rim charge.** Each valley's canopy edge is a [[creature.terror-bird|Terror-Bird]] hunting post when one holds the surrounding quarter-mile. The bird does not enter the grass. It waits in shade until prey is in the light, then the ground shakes.

**[[creature.unsaid-macaw|Unsaid Macaws]].** River-valley margins, a secondary habitat for the species. A macaw perches on the grass wall and echoes the unsaid surface thoughts of whoever is nearby, in their own voice. Not a predator — the exposure is the hazard.

**Canopy gap.** [[creature.aruhe-crown-squid|Crown Squids]] refuse to cross broad openings — no anchor points — except under extreme hunger. Escaping the canopy means entering the grass, which is exactly what the rim [[creature.terror-bird|Terror-Bird]] waits for: open sky is bait.

**Forbidden fruit.** [[item.quickeye-berry|Quickeye Berries]] (Very Rare, *haste*) grow in the valleys — the fruit of impossible speed, placed for pursuit or patrol-timing challenges. It works exactly as advertised.

## Landmarks

- **River valleys** — miles of hole in the hundred-foot canopy. First sky inland of the beach. Visible as a brighter seam from the last few paces of jungle.
- **[[region.aruhe-the-river|The River]]** — only clear line through a valley. Loud. The heading that does not lie. Follow it uphill for days and it ends at [[location.aruhe-the-clear-lake|The Clear Lake]].
- **Grass walls** — eight-foot blades. A second jungle. Corridors of crushed stems are the only map.
- **Canopy rim** — the dark wall of trunks around each valley. Where the charge starts. Where the hope ends.

> [!secret]- Secrets
> The openings are not safe rooms. The [[creature.blight|Druid Lich]]'s garden uses sky the same way the terraces use fruit. Water and light after the mute dark are how people linger in the open long enough to be seen.
