# Quickeye Berry

> [!narration] Appearance
> A small glossy berry, almost black, that leaves a bitter bite on the tongue and a wide-eyed, trembling alertness behind the eyes. The cluster it grows in looks half-alive, like a handful of watchful pupils.

## Properties

- **Type:** Fruit (consumable)
- **Rarity:** Very Rare
- **Attunement:** No
- Consuming takes an Action. Gain the effects of *haste* for 1 minute, with no concentration required.

## Biology

A guarana-like stimulant fruit — real-world caffeine chemistry pushed to magical extremes. The [[creature.blight|Blight]] exaggerated the berry's own stimulant compounds until one fruit is a full magical alacrity. Deep-interior specimens run Very Rare.

## Identification

DC 13 Nature or Medicine (1 minute): broad physiological function — a fruit of impossible speed or alertness. DC 15: exact effect. *Identify* spell: exact effect automatically. Environmental tell: a creature eats a Quickeye and bolts at impossible speed.

## Placement

Found before a pursuit or patrol-timing challenge — the fruit that solves the next chase sitting in its path. Rare in [[region.aruhe-the-grasslands|The Grasslands]], where the open valleys make speed the difference between sight and charge.

## Abundance

Rare.
