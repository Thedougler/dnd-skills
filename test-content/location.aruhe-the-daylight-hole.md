# The Daylight Hole

A collapsed skylight beneath [[region.aruhe-the-quiet|The Quiet]]. From below, the party can see a perfect circle of green canopy **seventy feet overhead**. Rain falls through it. Roots descend. Birds never do.

This becomes an emergency exit from [[region.aruhe-the-cave|The Cave]] — but climbing into [[region.aruhe-the-quiet|The Quiet]] simply exchanges one set of problems for another.

> [!narration] Arrival
> The tunnel roof has fallen away. Seventy feet up, a perfect circle of green canopy holds the sky — rain falls through it, roots hang down in pale curtains. No birds ever cross the circle. The climb is possible. [[region.aruhe-the-quiet|The Quiet]] waiting up there is its own price.

## Senses

- **Signature:** The first true daylight [[region.aruhe-the-cave|The Cave]] has offered — a bright disc of green overhead, rain ticking on the tube floor.
- **Footing:** Collapse rubble under the hole; root strands offer the handholds.

## Features

- **The root climb:**
  - **Obvious:** Roots descend from the canopy circle seventy feet overhead.
  - **Reveals:** A way up — and out.
  - **Logic:** Climbing is possible: **DC 14 Strength (Athletics)** with the roots.
  - **Consequence:** Failure by less than 5: no progress. Failure by 5 or more: **fall from current height unless secured.**

- **The circle:**
  - **Obvious:** A perfect circle of green canopy seventy feet up.
  - **Reveals:** Rain falls through it; roots descend; birds never do — the fauna of [[region.aruhe-the-quiet|The Quiet]] avoids the mouth. ^[inferred — the birds never entering is stated; why is not]
  - **Logic:** A skylight mouth hidden completely beneath centuries of growth.
  - **Consequence:** Emergency exit from [[region.aruhe-the-cave|The Cave]] into the mute quiet of the surface jungle.

## Hazards

> [!danger] Active Hazards
> The fall. Unsecured climbers who fail by 5+ fall from current height. What waits above is [[region.aruhe-the-quiet|The Quiet]] — where predators close without sound.

Source is silent. Birds do not cross the circle; the source does not say what does.
