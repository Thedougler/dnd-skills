# Tessarine Concordat

Amnian-model merchant consortium. Regional base: [[settlement.calveno|Calveno]]. Cares about credit, not heritage. Runs [[settlement.calveno|Calveno]] and [[region.verdant-scatter|The Scatter]]'s credit economy. Debt is its politics. Holds its deepest roots in the east by credit and debt.

> [!tip] Using this source
> Campaign overview: The Tessarine Concordat runs [[settlement.calveno|Calveno]] and [[region.verdant-scatter|The Scatter]]'s credit economy. Debt is its politics. No active Front clock stated yet.

## Overview

Began as an Amnian house consortium, chartered the way Amn once held Port Nyanzaru. True wealth is not left exposed in [[settlement.calveno|Calveno]]. Oral tradition puts a hidden depository — [[location.vault-of-the-first-factor|Vault of the First Factor]] — on an uncharted [[region.midchain|Midchain]] limestone spire.

## Goals

- **Stated goal:** Notes paid. Collateral held. Trade moving.
- **True goal:** Contain [[npc.aldric-drave|Aldric Drave]]. [[npc.cosimo-verantio|Cosimo Verantio]], the Concordat's true architect, is a Magus Dragon who accumulates obligation instead of gold; he hid as the [[faction.chain-council|Chain Council]]'s "Principal" to commission the Pearl heist against Aldric's bloodline limits, not [[deity.umberlee|Umberlee]]. The commissioned fleet was [[faction.fisks-fleet|Fisk's Fleet]].
- **Immediate:** [[settlement.calveno|Calveno]] counting houses. Salvage interest in [[faction.antheri|Antheri]] [[region.shelfworks|Shelfworks]] (name and upper layout, not the deep city). Silence anyone asking about [[npc.detto-kalash|Detto Kalash]]'s mail.

## Methods

Credit, notes, confiscated collateral. Does not care who you are if the ledger balances. The Concordat rarely says no; it says yes with terms. Common instruments: letters of credit, route underwriting, debt-recovery holds, salvage contracts, and Council obligations tied to private debt. The Seven Houses Council sits at a four-to-three Tessarine-aligned deadlock — four Houses owe enough, benefit enough, or fear enough disruption that Tessarine proposals pass unless the three holdouts can make refusal profitable.

> [!tip] Running This Faction
> Heritage is noise. The question is always: can you pay, and what did you put up. The dragon works through the factor network — his Circle — who never know what they serves. Destroying Tessarine gold would not wound him; invalidating debt, freeing councils from obligation, or making Tessarine paper unreliable would.

## Resources

[[settlement.calveno|Calveno]]. Notes. Rumored vault. Salvage buyers. [[faction.the-passage|The Passage]] uses Tessarine paper and commercial channels as cover — no formal arrangement between the two.

## Leadership

Source is silent on the sitting Envoy. The vault predates the modern structure. No official will confirm it exists. The hidden hand is [[npc.cosimo-verantio|Cosimo Verantio]], operating through factors who never know he is their Principal. [[npc.shepherd-grigori|Shepherd Grigori]] wears a Tessarine shirt under his [[faction.dravosi-crown|Dravosi]] naval coat.

## Front: [[situation.magus-dragons-hoard|The Magus Dragon's Hoard]] (dormant)

Lifecycle: dormant. Aim: accumulate obligation — leverage over councils, trade routes, institutions, and inherited debt across [[settlement.calveno|Calveno]] and [[faction.dravosi-crown|the Crown]] Islands — in place of gold. Off-screen if unopposed: PC-adjacent councils and factions accumulate Concordat debt without knowing who ultimately holds it.

Clock: 8 segments ([[item.slow-burn|Slow Burn]]), 0 filled. Still dormant. [[npc.iacopo-fieschi|Fieschi]]'s public confession cracked local concealment, but no wiki fact shows the hoard shrinking or the deadlock shifting.

Consequence at fill: the Concordat calls in simultaneous debts, shattering the 4-3 deadlock and forcing unanimous ratification of a Tessarine charter subordinating [[settlement.calveno|Calveno]]'s governance. The same leverage extends to [[faction.dravosi-crown|the Crown]] Islands. Centuries of accumulated obligation convert to direct control the dragon no longer hides.

Triggers: the crew investigates the Concordat's debt-holding structure past a single factor's public face (pursuing The [[location.vault-of-the-first-factor|Vault of the First Factor]], confronting [[npc.cosimo-verantio|Cosimo]] directly); or a second named debtor repeats [[npc.iacopo-fieschi|Fieschi]]'s public confession.

**Three-Clue Trail** — conclusion: [[npc.cosimo-verantio|Cosimo Verantio]] is a Magus Dragon and the Concordat's factor network is his unwitting Circle, accumulating obligation instead of gold as its true hoard.

- Clue 1: [[settlement.calveno|Calveno]] factor building. Salvage manifests and crew lists for [[region.shelfworks|Shelfworks]] [[faction.antheri|Antheri]] orders show appetite and reach disproportionate to any merchant house; a pressed factor lets slip that "[[npc.cosimo-verantio|the Principal]]" personally reviews every [[faction.antheri|Antheri]] lot.
- Clue 2: the Seven Houses Council's 4-3 deadlock. A holdout House member names debts that outlive the debtor and pass to heirs and successor firms — a pattern no ordinary moneylender sustains across generations.
- Clue 3: [[location.vault-of-the-first-factor|The Vault of the First Factor]], nearest physical match to a hoard, whose accounting tracks obligation compounding across centuries, tied to no single mortal lifespan. Reaching its records may require breaching wards or guardians.
