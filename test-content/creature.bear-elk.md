## Overview

> [!narration] Player-facing description — read or adapt at the table.
Elk antlers on a bear's frame, bigger than a draft horse, built lower and wider. Reddish-dark fur over a chest and shoulders thick enough to turn steel. Full-spread antlers drip with a luminous, viscous sap that glows faintly in the dark. Pale eyes with no visible pupil. Curved fangs and tusks that belong to neither animal. It covers fifty feet at a dead run, the ground shaking under it, faster than anything its weight suggests.

![[assets/creatures/bear-elk.jpg]]

## Ecology

The Blight's corruption fused the two largest animals on [[region.aruhe|Aruhe]] and didn't stop pushing. The frame is a bear's, broad through the chest, low to the ground, built for power over distance. The rack is an elk's, full-spread antlers that would [[item.the-snap|Snap]] a normal animal's neck, dripping with a luminous sap that has no source in the animal's own biology. The sap comes from the Blight. It seeps from the antler velvet the way resin seeps from a wounded tree. It glows faintly in the dark, which is how most creatures on [[region.aruhe|Aruhe]] know to get out of the way.

The bear-elk doesn't need to eat often. Its metabolism runs on corruption the same way the island's plants run on sunlight. It patrols because the Blight needs it to patrol, and it has been doing it long enough that every tree along the route carries claw-scored bark at chest height.

## Behavior

It patrols a fixed route and timing every day. The challenge is that the route covers every approach between [[region.aruhe-the-quiet|The Quiet]] and [[region.aruhe-the-rot|The Rot]]. A party that wants to pass must time the patrol or find an alternative.

## Habitat

Territory runs the boundary between [[region.aruhe-the-quiet|The Quiet]] and [[region.aruhe-the-rot|The Rot]] on [[region.aruhe|Aruhe]].
