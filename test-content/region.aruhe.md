# Aruhe

Deserted [[region.midchain-east|Eastern Midchain]] island, a country of mute jungle east of [[region.verdant-teeth|The Verdant Teeth]]. From [[region.karath|Karath]]'s channel the landing still reads as one beach and old terraces; the mass behind them is a green nation. Nothing on it is doing the job a chart would expect. [[creature.grung|Grung]] patrol boats work the western reefs and will not land.

[[region.sorn|Sorn]] traders who speak [[creature.grung|Grung]] say the name means *hungry isle* and will not say more.

![[assets/places/aruhe-2.jpeg]]

## Overview

> [!narration] Overview
> Broken reef a half-mile out. The coral stands too sharp, too close. One gap. One short shingle beach. Terrace stone climbs behind the sand into fruit hanging too heavy and vines thick as a wrist. Above that the jungle is mute and does not stop — ridge behind ridge of sealed canopy, a green country, no birdsong. [[region.karath|Karath]]'s cliffs stand across half a mile of channel. Stop when they choose the gap, [[region.aruhe-the-beach|The Beach]], or stay off.

A [[creature.grung|Grung]] raiding fleet wrecked in part on these reefs; survivors still shelter in the hull. Full account: [[event.aruhe-wreck|The Aruhe Wreck]]. Captive-trail pressure: [[situation.the-aruhe-rescue|The Aruhe Rescue]]. Island expedition: [[situation.aruhes-hungry-isle|Aruhe's Hungry Isle]]. Full playable topology: [[dungeon.aruhe-wilderness-megadungeon|Aruhe Wilderness Mega-Dungeon]].

## Geography

**Extent.** Roughly 500 miles along the long axis, 150 miles at the waist — South Island scale. Crossing the interior is weeks. The charted landing is one reef gap on the western face.

**Playable scale.** Aruhe's geographic scale remains real, but the island is explored as a nested pointcrawl: broad regions are levels, landmarks are nodes, and rivers, game trails, ridges, root-roads, and caves are routes between them. The full playable topology belongs to [[dungeon.aruhe-wilderness-megadungeon|Aruhe Wilderness Mega-Dungeon]]; this region owns the island-scale frame rather than its room index or local encounter procedures.

**Position.** Western approaches sit directly east of [[region.karath|Karath]] across half a mile of open channel, close enough to swim. The rest of Aruhe falls away from that strait for hundreds of miles. [[region.karath|Karath]] is a neighbor to the landing, not to the heart.

**Topography.** Coastal reef, one charted gap, one short landing beach. Other beaches exist as reef-locked ribbons and cliff coves; they are not approaches. Terraces climb in cut steps behind the landing face, stonework collapsed and swallowed. Above the terrace line, tree-cover closes sight to a few paces and then keeps going. Rivers drain from [[location.aruhe-the-clear-lake|The Clear Lake]] in inner [[region.aruhe-the-rot|The Rot]] and tear valley meadows through that canopy; those openings are [[region.aruhe-the-grasslands|The Grasslands]].

The land is still working. The work is appetite. Every familiar thing on Aruhe has been pushed past its job: larger, hungrier, slightly wrong. A rabbit hunts in a pack. A deer stands upright and waits. Coral grows like teeth. Fruit heals and the garden minds who takes it. That first becomes obvious on the terrace climb. [[region.aruhe-the-beach|The Beach]] still looks landable. The interior is the same garden, sick and composed.

Bands, beach to heart:

| Band | Role |
|---|---|
| [[region.aruhe-the-beach\|Aruhe Beach]] | Reef, shingle, sole landing |
| [[region.aruhe-the-old-gardens\|The Old Gardens]] | Collapsed terrace stonework, feral crops |
| [[region.aruhe-the-quiet\|The Quiet]] | Outer jungle where ambient sound dies |
| [[region.aruhe-the-rot\|The Rot]] | Deep interior, spores and blackened leaves |
| [[region.aruhe-the-grove\|The Grove]] | Named central grove around the Death Bloom |

**[[region.aruhe-the-river|The River]]** drains [[location.aruhe-the-clear-lake|The Clear Lake]] eastward through [[region.aruhe-the-rot|The Rot]] and [[region.aruhe-the-quiet|The Quiet]], opening [[region.aruhe-the-grasslands|The Grasslands]] wherever the current prevents canopy closure. It is not a band — it is the artery. The water is the best road through Aruhe. Something else already owns it.

[[region.aruhe-the-grasslands|The Grasslands]] are not a sixth band. They interrupt [[region.aruhe-the-quiet|The Quiet]] (and a few cuts in [[region.aruhe-the-rot|The Rot]]) wherever freshwater from [[location.aruhe-the-clear-lake|The Clear Lake]] forces the hundred-foot trees apart into river valleys. The lake is a highland basin inside [[region.aruhe-the-rot|The Rot]], not a sixth band and not a second heart. [[region.aruhe-the-grove|The Grove]] remains the last room, on a shelf above one shore.

[[region.aruhe-the-cave|The Cave]] is the buried underside of the island: extinct lava tubes from Aruhe's volcanic construction, an under-region cutting through several bands at once — the same sense in which [[region.aruhe-the-river|The River]] is an artery rather than another concentric layer. Entrances occur through scattered collapses beneath [[region.aruhe-the-old-gardens|The Old Gardens]], [[region.aruhe-the-quiet|The Quiet]], and [[region.aruhe-the-rot|The Rot]], and flooded tubes exist near [[location.aruhe-the-clear-lake|The Clear Lake]] — but there is no secret cave entrance to [[region.aruhe-the-grove|The Grove]]. [[region.aruhe-the-cave|The Cave]] creates alternate routes and dangerous shortcuts without invalidating the established geography.

**Penetration bands.** These are soft access bands, not level requirements or hard gates. Territory, visible consequence, and learned routes communicate when an approach is beyond the party's current tolerance.

| Band | Island-scale frame |
|---|---|
| **Outer Aruhe** | [[region.aruhe-the-beach\|The Beach]], [[region.aruhe-the-old-gardens\|The Old Gardens]], and the [[region.aruhe-the-quiet\|The Quiet]] edge — the landing and first routes. |
| **Middle Aruhe** | The [[region.aruhe-the-quiet\|Quiet]], [[region.aruhe-the-grasslands\|Grasslands]], [[region.aruhe-the-river\|River]], [[location.aruhe-the-long-reach\|Long Reach]], and approaches through [[region.aruhe-the-cave\|The Cave]] — the route network and its ecology. |
| **Heart Aruhe** | [[region.aruhe-the-rot\|The Rot]], deep cave routes, [[location.aruhe-the-clear-lake\|The Clear Lake]], and [[region.aruhe-the-grove\|The Grove]] — the inner island and Death Bloom. |

**Expedition travel.** One Expedition Turn is roughly 4 hours; two turns make a normal 8-hour travel day. Each turn is route, role choices, one resolution, and consequence. Roll only when failure has a meaningful consequence. A consequence can be time, route change, discovery, exposure, lost resources, attracting fauna, or an encounter — not automatically combat. This preserves the island's scale without rolling every mile or hour; local hazards and procedures remain on their owning pages.

**Route knowledge.** Route states are durable expedition access and knowledge, so later returns do not replay the first crossing:

| Route state | Durable access |
|---|---|
| **Unknown** | Full travel cost and navigation risk. |
| **Observed** | Destination is known. |
| **Mapped** | Navigation failure is largely removed. |
| **Mastered** | Travel compresses significantly, with a known camp or refuge. |
| **Shortcut discovered** | One or more nodes can be bypassed. |

## Climate

Coastal [[region.midchain|Midchain]]: reef swell, morning mist on [[region.aruhe-the-beach|The Beach]] that burns off by midday and returns with evening, then offshore wind. Dawn and dusk are when packs move and moths load the canopy. After dark the interior stops waiting. Things that stood still all day start to close. ^[inferred]

## Peoples

No settlement on the island. The close land is [[region.karath|Karath]], and [[faction.grung-clans|Grung Clans]] will not set foot on this sand. The ports that talk about beach smoke are [[settlement.sparhold|Sparhold]] and [[settlement.kalowe|Kalowe]] across [[region.midchain|Midchain]] water.

[[creature.grung|Grung]] treat any taking of fruit, river catch, forage, or trapped game as a death sentence. Their whole account is one survivor's telling from generations ago: a landing party hunted down by fauna that turned on [[creature.grung|Grung]] scent.

The origin of her vow is established on [[creature.blight|Druid Lich]]: a captive archdruid escaped across the ocean channel, lost her companion during the crossing, carried him inland to [[region.aruhe-the-grove|The Grove]], and bound herself into the living ground. Archived draft fiction retains earlier variants as evidence, not competing current history.

## Threats

**Island-wide identity.** Aruhe is a beautiful living system whose ecological brakes have failed: growth, predation, surveillance, and territorial enforcement work too successfully and in slightly wrong forms. Its horror is coherent rather than a set of unrelated monsters. Detailed topology and encounter procedures belong to [[dungeon.aruhe-wilderness-megadungeon|Aruhe Wilderness Mega-Dungeon]] and the linked owners.

> [!danger] Active Threats
> Danger rises with elevation. [[region.aruhe-the-beach|The Beach]] is landable. The terraces hunt. The interior is the same hunger, sealed. Passing ships see no sign.

**The pattern.** Do not run generic beasts. Run a familiar shape doing the wrong job.

- [[creature.wolfrabbit|Wolfrabbit]] — rabbit silhouette, cat musculature, pack launches from terrace walls. Dawn and dusk. They do not chase far. The island provides.
- [[creature.deer-stalker|Deer-Stalker]] — deer frame, wasting-still, crashes off a carcass then comes back after dark with a voice stolen from the fire.
- [[creature.reef-skull|Reef Skull]] — hermit crabs past any shell on the reef, buried in shingle in skulls and hull-chunks.
- [[creature.grinning-ape|Grinning Ape]] / [[creature.vine-lash|Vine Lash]] — terrace canopy and rope-vines that wait over the only climb.
- [[creature.thornback|Thornback]] / [[creature.strangler-fig|Strangler Fig]] / [[creature.bear-elk|Bear-Elk]] — Quiet floor, trail funnels, and the scored circuits that block known Quiet-into-Rot approaches.
- [[creature.silence-moths|Silence Moths]] / [[creature.corpsewood|Corpsewood]] / [[creature.terror-bird|Terror-Bird]] — Rot air, walking deadwood, mossed charge on the last approach.
- [[creature.grubnade|Grubnade]] — hand-sized caterpillar that detonates when disturbed; the explosion is reproduction. The few insects the island cannot eat.
- [[hazard.razer-grass|Razer-Grass]] — the plant version: grass armored with glass. The blades cut; breaking them is worse. Fire makes it detonate; water makes it safe and harder to see.
- [[hazard.stillbloom|Stillbloom]] — the plant version: wide root mats classify vibration, and violence fires a volley of poisoned spines. Slow movement is safe; running to help the wounded is exactly what it waits for.
- [[creature.unsaid-macaw|Unsaid Macaw]] — the harmless end: a parrot that repeats what you chose not to say, in your voice. Non-hostile; the exposure is the hazard.
- [[creature.blight|Druid Lich]] — the garden's owner, bound at [[region.aruhe-the-grove|The Grove]].

Giant spiders in the terrace brush and blood hawks over [[region.aruhe-the-beach|The Beach]] are the only SRD fauna left recognizable.

## Forbidden fruit

Aruhe grows **genuinely excellent consumable magic items**. No curses, poison chances, addiction, exhaustion, or hidden drawbacks on the fruit itself. The fruit works exactly as advertised.

**Mechanical standard.** Normal 5e magic-item logic. Most miracle fruits are **Rare consumables**; deep-interior specimens are occasional **Very Rare**. No attunement. Consuming takes an **Action** (2014-style, matching potions). Effects mirror established potions/spells where possible; their strength is intentional — the environmental/metaphysical consequence balances availability, not a mechanical downside. Ordinary 1d4-healing terrace produce stays the baseline so the miracle fruit reads as escalation.

**The catalogue:**

**Abundance.** Redheart, [[item.lion-citrus|Lion Citrus]], and [[item.breathmelon|Breathmelon]] (near wetlands) are abundant; [[item.stonepear|Stonepear]] and [[item.giants-guava|Giant's Guava]] are common; [[item.ghost-plum|Ghost Plum]] and [[item.whisper-fig|Whisper Fig]] are uncommon; Quickeye and [[item.skybladder|Skybladder]] are rare; [[item.rotheart|Rotheart]] is very rare. Scarcity is not the primary limiter — visible abundance is the temptation engine.

**Identification.** No blind-consumption gameplay; the interesting decision needs informed temptation. DC 13 Nature or Medicine (1 minute) identifies the broad physiological function; DC 15 identifies the exact mechanical effect; *Identify* gives the exact effect automatically. Environmental observation also identifies: a wounded animal eating a Redheart and visibly recovering, an animal consuming a [[item.stonepear|Stonepear]] before fighting, a creature eating a Quickeye and bolting at impossible speed, a floating [[item.breathmelon|Breathmelon]] releasing trapped gas underwater.

**Placement rule.** Place fruit immediately before or beside the problem it solves — converts loot into temptation rather than generic treasure. [[item.breathmelon|Breathmelon]] before dangerous river travel, [[item.stonepear|Stonepear]] before [[creature.terror-bird|Terror-Bird]] territory, [[item.giants-guava|Giant's Guava]] near a [[creature.bear-elk|Bear-Elk]] crossing, [[item.ghost-plum|Ghost Plum]] before an exposed stealth crossing, Quickeye before a pursuit or patrol-timing challenge, [[item.rotheart|Rotheart]] only after [[region.aruhe-the-rot|The Rot]] has begun inflicting exhaustion, Redheart beside badly injured characters.

**Campaign function.** The fruit is temptation, emergency resource, and export bait. It is valuable enough that taking one is rational.

## Landmarks

- **Reef gap** — only approach for anything larger than a canoe.
- **Landing beach** — sole sand; wrecked [[vehicle.vethka|Vethka]] hull above the tideline.
- **Terrace stair of broken risers** — only climbable way inland from [[region.aruhe-the-beach|The Beach]]. First place the garden's size is obvious.
- **Mute canopy** — no birdsong from the water. The jungle ate the small noise first.
- **Death Bloom** — central memorial tree on [[region.aruhe-the-grove|The Grove]]; who lies under it is owned by [[creature.blight|Druid Lich]].
- **[[location.aruhe-the-clear-lake|The Clear Lake]]** — inner-Rot highland lake that feeds the rivers; watering hole; [[region.aruhe-the-grove|The Grove]] sits on a shelf above one shore.

> [!secret]- Secrets
> Nothing here is a new species from scratch. The [[creature.blight|Druid Lich]]'s garden held nature at the extreme and never let it back. [[creature.grung|Grung]] believe the island still stands exactly as it did the morning their last landing died. A [[region.midchain|Midchain]] rumor (pilot left a dinghy after finding a goat-sized scorpion under it) travels as a joke. The wreck on [[region.aruhe-the-beach|The Beach]] is likely the "dark hull" [[npc.nona-black-jaw|Nona]] has hunted since Session 04 — unconfirmed.
