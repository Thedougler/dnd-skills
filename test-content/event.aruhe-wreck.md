# The Aruhe Wreck

A storm splits the [[creature.grung|Grung]] fleet carrying [[settlement.calveno|Calveno]]'s stolen hundreds south to [[region.karath|Karath]]. Part of it wrecks on [[region.aruhe|Aruhe]] instead, and the island keeps almost everyone who washes up.

## Trigger

In the days after the Session 07 raid, [[faction.fisks-fleet|the Fleet]] carrying all four hundred eighty-seven [[settlement.calveno|Calveno]] captives ran the [[region.central-strait|Central Strait]] south toward [[region.karath|Karath]]. A tropical storm out of the east caught the crossing.

## If Uninterrupted

Already run its course in the notes. Survivors remain in the hull until they starve, break the taboo, or a sail that is not [[creature.grung|Grung]] threads the gap — see [[situation.the-aruhe-rescue|The Aruhe Rescue]].

## What Could Prevent It

- The storm not catching the crossing (past).
- Hulls staying on the charted line to [[region.karath|Karath]] (two hundred ninety captives did).

## What Happened

- **Summary:** Storm drove part of [[faction.fisks-fleet|the Fleet]] west onto [[region.aruhe|Aruhe]]'s reefs; two captives still live in a broken [[vehicle.vethka|Vethka]] hull on [[region.aruhe-the-beach|The Beach]].
- **Outcome:** 290 captives reached [[region.karath|Karath]]. 48 went down with two hulls lost in the crossing. 149 broke apart on [[region.aruhe|Aruhe]] with their guards. Most drowned. Three [[creature.grung|Grung]] who made the sand died within hours. Three [[settlement.calveno|Calveno]] captives rode a broken outrigger strut ashore: [[npc.sandro|Sandro]], [[npc.nino|Nino]], and Tomo. Tomo ate from a shore bush the first night and was dragged into the treeline by both ankles before dawn.

> [!narration] As It Was Witnessed
> The sea laid a [[vehicle.vethka|Vethka]] over until the outrigger cleared water, then put the following boat thirty feet in the air. A wall of green water took the mast. Three people rode a plank in through mist that did not move with the wind. Two [[creature.grung|Grung]] were already dead on [[region.aruhe-the-beach|The Beach]] — one at the tideline, one with ribs held open and pale shoots growing from cavity and mouth.

The storm was weather. A captive remembered the sea as gleeful. That is a survivor's memory, not a willing hand behind the storm.

## Participants

- [[npc.sandro|Sandro]], [[npc.nino|Nino]], Tomo — [[settlement.calveno|Calveno]] captives (NPC pages not minted).
- Unnamed green-caste [[creature.grung|Grung]] who held [[npc.sandro|Sandro]]'s rope for one second as the mast went.
- [[creature.grung|Grung]] guardsmen lost with the hulls and on the sand.

## Location

- **Primary:** [[region.aruhe-the-beach|The Beach]] (reef, beach, wrecked hull).
- Crossing also the [[region.central-strait|Central Strait]] and the charted line to [[region.karath|Karath]].

## Consequences

- **Immediate:** [[npc.sandro|Sandro]] and [[npc.nino|Nino]] shelter in the hull, eating only open-sky fish, never crossing tideline moss, watching the gap.
- **Ripple effects:** Likely [[npc.nona-black-jaw|Nona]]'s "dark hull" from Session 04 — unconfirmed against the 487 count. Sits across [[pc.perrin-black-jaw|Perrin]]'s [[region.aruhe|Aruhe]] survey path. Feeds [[situation.the-aruhe-rescue|The Aruhe Rescue]].

## Aftermath

Smoke/signal fires from [[region.aruhe-the-beach|The Beach]] are the live tell in the rescue brief (two weeks in that draft).

> [!secret]- What Most People Do Not Know
> Tomo's death taught the remaining two not to take fruit. [[npc.sandro|Sandro]] has not explained that death to [[npc.nino|Nino]]. Fiction texture from the archived castaway draft.
