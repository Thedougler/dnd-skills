# Sandro

## First Impression

> [!narration] Narration
> Salt-cracked and sun-browned, [[npc.sandro|Sandro]] crouches in the shade of the broken [[vehicle.vethka|Vethka]]. The open side of the hull faces the surf. The shade behind him points inland, toward the terraces.
>
> He works a bent hook through a shoelace. [[npc.nino|Nino]] stays behind him, inside the broken ribs. He sees the boat coming through the reef and stands so fast the hook drops. He waits for someone to cross the beach before he speaks.

## Wants

- **Right now:** Keep himself and [[npc.nino|Nino]] alive until the sail in the reef gap is rescue, not another [[vehicle.vethka|Vethka]].
- **Long term:** Get off [[region.aruhe|Aruhe]] alive.

> [!tip] Playing Tips
> Fish-market economy of attention. He notices and stores. Cuts the treeline subject short. Voice is short sentences, no speeches.

## What They Know

- How Tomo died (fruit, then drag marks) — will not tell [[npc.nino|Nino]].
- Two [[creature.grung|Grung]] dead on the beach after the wreck; he never told [[npc.nino|Nino]] what killed them.
- Tomo ate fruit, then something dragged him into the treeline.
- They eat only open-sky shallows fish. They do not cross the tideline moss.

## Relationships

- [[npc.nino|Nino]] — fellow [[settlement.calveno|Calveno]] captive; he has not explained Tomo.
- Tomo — dead the first night.
- [[pc.perrin-black-jaw|Perrin Black-Jaw]] / [[npc.nona-black-jaw|Nona Black-Jaw]] — survey and hunt that can land on this beach.
- [[creature.grung|Grung]] — captors; he is not sorry about the dead on the sand.

## Levers

- A calm approach across the beach.
- Names from [[settlement.calveno|Calveno]].
- Food or water from the boat.
- Asking about Tomo or the treeline shuts him down until [[npc.nino|Nino]] is safe.

> [!secret]- Secrets
> He sat down in the sand over a [[creature.grung|Grung]] with ribs held open and pale shoots growing from cavity and mouth. He did not tell the others.

> [!danger] If Violence Starts
> He does not fight the island. He goes back into the hull. Against people he bargains first.

## If Left Alone

He and [[npc.nino|Nino]] keep signal fires until they cannot. Then they join the island's count. See [[situation.the-aruhe-rescue|The Aruhe Rescue]], [[situation.aruhes-hungry-isle|Aruhe's Hungry Isle]], and [[event.aruhe-wreck|The Aruhe Wreck]].

## Mechanics

No statblock. Exhausted commoner with a bent hook.

## Continuity

Watching the gap as of the wreck's aftermath.
