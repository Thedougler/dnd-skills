# The Rot

Inner jungle of [[region.aruhe|Aruhe]] where the island's hyper-nature turns sick in the open: blackened leaf-edges, luminous sap, split bark. [[creature.grung|Grung]] named it from one generation-old look and never came back to revise the name.

[[region.aruhe-the-quiet|The Quiet]] was mute size. This band is the same garden rotting while it still grows. Leaves too large and curling black. Fungi in colors that belong to nothing else on the island. Roots running above ground like veins, bright with sap, all of them pointing the same way. The Rot's identity is growth with its regulation gone: living tissue keeps making more of itself, even when the new tissue is already failing.

![[assets/places/aruhe-the-rot.jpeg]]

## Overview

> [!narration] Overview
> Daylight barely reaches the floor. Every leaf is black at the edge. Exposed roots thread luminous sap upslope toward a higher grove. The air tastes of spore and unfinished rot. Stop when they choose to step over a root, follow it, or turn back to [[region.aruhe-the-quiet|The Quiet]].

The deep interior inside [[region.aruhe-the-quiet|The Quiet]]: highland jungle around the lake and [[region.aruhe-the-grove|The Grove]]. Crossing toward that clearing is days of climb, not hours.

## Geography

Canopy sealed. The ground climbs the whole way. Footing is root, stone, and wet leaf-fall. Sight is worse than in [[region.aruhe-the-quiet|The Quiet]] because the remaining light is wrong — sap-glow from below, almost no sky.

The [[creature.blight|Druid Lich]]'s root network runs visibly above ground here. Exposed roots, wrist-thick to thigh-thick, thread luminous sap toward the central grove. They are direct and fast, but observable: light pulses upslope when a creature crosses, and the network records who follows it. The full island-scale Expedition Turn and route-state procedure remain deferred to [[dungeon.aruhe-wilderness-megadungeon|Aruhe Wilderness Mega-Dungeon]]; this region owns the Rot's local route choices and their physical consequences.

The land is still working, and the work is excess gone to decay. The living growth has lost its brakes: bark splits and does not heal clean, new tissue emerges necrotic, injured trunks answer with excessive scar growth, fungi consume fallen tissue faster than it can settle, and roots crowd roots until viable paths braid into chokepoints. Walking trees — [[creature.corpsewood|Corpsewood]] — drag dead roots through the deep interior. [[creature.terror-bird|Terror-Birds]] hold solitary quarter-mile territories, moss growing out of the feathers, still as canopy until something enters the claim.

A few river valleys still open here. The grass yellows. The rim sap-glows. Those cuts are still [[region.aruhe-the-grasslands|The Grasslands]], sicker than the ones in [[region.aruhe-the-quiet|The Quiet]]. Follow a cut uphill and the canopy breaks on [[location.aruhe-the-clear-lake|The Clear Lake]]: the source basin, a watering hole, not the last room.

Outward is [[region.aruhe-the-quiet|The Quiet]], across a local [[creature.bear-elk|Bear-Elk]] circuit. Inward along the sap-lines is [[region.aruhe-the-grove|The Grove]]. A brighter still sky through the trees is the lake. Off the roots is slower and does not ping [[region.aruhe-the-grove|The Grove]] — until a root is still underfoot.

## Climate

Spore-heavy air. Heat sits under the sealed canopy and does not move. After an hour the lungs know it. Dusk and dawn, [[creature.silence-moths|Silence Moths]] load the canopy corridors; the first tell is a deeper hole in air that was already quiet, then pale wings blotting what little light remains.

## Peoples

None. Cultivation never reached this band. [[creature.grung|Grung]] have one old look at it from [[region.aruhe-the-quiet|The Quiet]] edge and treat that as enough.

## Threats

> [!danger] Active Threats
> The air itself exhausts. Roots alert the memorial. [[creature.terror-bird|Terror-Birds]] hold solitary territories — the ground shakes before the charge. [[creature.corpsewood|Corpsewood]] walks the deep interior, never faster than a walk. [[creature.silence-moths|Silence Moths]] at dusk and dawn. [[creature.deer-stalker|Deer-Stalkers]] still hunt the darker tree line after a lost meal. [[creature.strangler-fig|Strangler Figs]] persist where old trails pinch. Each known approach from [[region.aruhe-the-quiet|The Quiet]] has a local [[creature.bear-elk|Bear-Elk]] circuit; the lake-facing loops include the shore of [[location.aruhe-the-clear-lake|The Clear Lake]].

**Corrupted Air.** At the end of each 4-hour Expedition Turn spent unprotected in the Rot, make a DC 14 Constitution save. On a failure, gain 1 level of exhaustion. Make the same check immediately after particularly intense spore exposure. Proper filtration, protective magic, a discovered safe grove, or a cave route confirmed safe for its air negates the routine check while that protection or route lasts. A creature carrying [[hazard.razer-grass|Razer-Grass]]'s Glass-Choked condition makes the save with disadvantage — exposure inland compounds exposure at the coast. ^[inferred]

**Root-road choices.** The roots always point toward [[region.aruhe-the-grove|The Grove]], so following a known road does not lose the party.

| Choice | Travel | Cost |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Avoid the roots | Slower | Quieter. |
| Cross a root | Normal | DC 14 Wisdom (Perception) spots a safe crossing. On failure, light pulses toward [[region.aruhe-the-grove\|The Grove]]; the nearest [[creature.corpsewood\|Corpsewood]] or [[creature.terror-bird\|Terror-Bird]] arrives in 1d4 rounds. |
| Follow a root-road | Half travel time; direct to [[region.aruhe-the-grove\|The Grove]] | [[region.aruhe-the-grove|The Grove]] knows someone is coming. |
| Damage a living root | Route is harmed or cut | Nearby animals notice the damage. |

The road's speed is therefore also its exposure: a party can choose concealment, ordinary crossing, or a direct approach that tells [[region.aruhe-the-grove|The Grove]] it is coming. A DC 14 Wisdom (Survival) check can identify a route; once found, the road remains legible and cannot lose a party following it toward [[region.aruhe-the-grove|The Grove]].

**Silence-moth overlap.** Inside the 30-foot silence field: no verbal spell components, no Help on another creature's save, no shouted warning — including during a Corrupted Air save.

**[[creature.bear-elk|Bear-Elk]] circuit.** Same 4-hour local loop as on [[region.aruhe-the-quiet|The Quiet]]: one scored corridor, not the whole island. A known approach from that band crosses its own circuit.

**Canopy apex.** The largest and oldest [[creature.aruhe-crown-squid|Crown Squids]] live here; they are more sessile because prey is abundant.

**[[creature.snakewood|Snakewood]].** [[creature.snakewood|Snakewoods]] grow larger but less healthy-looking here — the spore-heavy sealed canopy suits a sessile ambusher whose prey still goes upward.

**[[hazard.stillbloom|Stillbloom]].** Transitional patches where Quiet green goes black-edged — game trails and sunbreaks, not the deep interior. The Rot's roots already detect creatures and pass information along the sap lines; Stillbloom root mats push the same mechanism into vibration classification: slow movement is safe, violence fires a volley of poisoned spines (DC 14, 7 (2d6) piercing, escalating venom saves).

**Forbidden fruit.** The Rot's deep interior grows the island's Very Rare specimens: [[item.skybladder|Skybladder]] in the sealed canopy, and [[item.rotheart|Rotheart]] — the cure for the Rot's own exhaustion — only after the corrupted air has begun to bite. [[item.whisper-fig|Whisper Fig]] grows at [[region.aruhe-the-quiet|The Quiet]]/Rot edge. All work exactly as advertised.

## Landmarks

- **Black-edge line** — where Quiet green first shows black leaf-tips. The name of the band becomes obvious in a few paces.
- **Luminous root-roads** — exposed sap-lines pointing at [[region.aruhe-the-grove|The Grove]]. Fastest climb. They report who uses them.
- **Canopy corridors** — moth routes at the edges of day. Vibration hunting. Fire still drives a swarm off.
- **Split-bark galleries** — trunks opened and glowing, fungal blooms in colors that do not belong. Cover that is also the network.
- **Quiet/Rot boundary** — chest-high claw scores and the [[creature.bear-elk|Bear-Elk]]'s circuit. Last place the air is only silent, not spore-thick.
- **Cave mouths** — the deepest root systems have penetrated the basalt and formed entire subterranean ecosystems beneath the Rot. Entrances to [[region.aruhe-the-cave|The Cave]].
- **[[location.aruhe-the-clear-lake|The Clear Lake]]** — pale-stone highland lake that feeds the rivers. Grove shelf above one shore. Skyline watering hole.

> [!secret]- Secrets
> Nothing here hides what is growing the jungle. The sap lines point at [[region.aruhe-the-grove|The Grove]]. The Rot is the [[creature.blight|Druid Lich]]'s body made visible — hyper-nature held past health, not a disease arriving from outside.
