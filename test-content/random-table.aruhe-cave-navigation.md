# Cave Navigation Table

## Table

**Roll:** `d8`

| Roll | Result |
|------|--------|
| 1 | **Sound chooses the wrong wall.** Speak: "A rattle seems ahead. The echo comes from a parallel tube." DC 14 Perception finds the true direction. Failure: 30 minutes off route. |
| 2 | **Dead Air pocket.** Speak: "Flame gutters. Breath turns deliberate. Dead insects lie under a shelf." Resolve [[hazard.aruhe-dead-air\|Dead Air]] unless they withdraw or find higher flow. |
| 3 | **Drumstone roof.** Speak: "Footsteps sound hollow overhead." DC 13 Investigation or Survival spots the thin crust. Failure triggers [[hazard.aruhe-drumstone\|Drumstone]] and may split them vertically. |
| 4 | **Squeeze refuge.** Speak: "The tube pinches below three feet. The big thing behind cannot follow." Entering costs time and uses squeezing rules. Refusing leaves them in the large tube. |
| 5 | **Root well feeding ground.** Speak: "Pale roots hang through the roof with water and insects." DC 13 Survival tells insect feeding from [[creature.blackrail\|Blackrail]] rubbing. Water is safe. Living roots are not. |
| 6 | **Flood pulse warning.** Speak: "Cold air pushes upstream. Grit lifts off the floor." DC 14 Survival names [[hazard.aruhe-flood-pulse\|Flood Pulse]] before the low passage fills. Failure starts the flood at close range. |
| 7 | **[[creature.blackrail\|Blackrail]] certainty.** Speak: "Dust falls. The leg-rattle grows. About thirty seconds." Light, squeeze, climb, silence, or a stand. |
| 8 | **Flow principle clicks.** Speak: "The frozen lava shapes start to read: down-tube toward the coast, up-tube inland." Mark two Navigation successes or improve this route to Observed. |

> [!tip] Running This Table
> Use one 30-minute Exploration Turn for each unknown fork, marked return, root well, squeeze, or tube descent in [[region.aruhe-the-cave|The Cave]]. Roll after a failed navigation check.

## Usage Notes

- **Navigation DC:** DC 14 Wisdom (Survival) or Intelligence (Nature). Use DC 15 without light, marks, or a map.
- **Progress:** Three successes before two failures finds an exit, stable route, or known branch.
- **Mapping:** Five total successes maps a connection well enough to retreat or reroute without the first-search risk.
- **Failure:** On each failed 30-minute turn, roll this table. The result should threaten air, stone, water, separation, or pursuit.
- **Taking:** Basalt, loose stone, shed shell, guano, and dripping groundwater are safe. Living roots, cave plants, eggs, bats, or insects are takes.

## Variants

- In a large main tube, replace result 4 with a side crawl the party passed 60 feet ago.
