# The Quiet

[[region.aruhe|Aruhe]]'s outer jungle above the terraces. [[creature.grung|Grung]] named it from the edge — no birdsong, no insect noise — and turned back.

This is the island's hyper-nature without the garden's fruit to explain it. Leaves hang too large. Roots break the path. The animals that use this band are oversized, aggressive, and slightly wrong. The uncanny part is the mute: a rainforest that should roar, and does not.

![[assets/places/aruhe-the-quiet.jpeg]]

## Overview

> [!narration] Overview
> Canopy closes sight to a few paces. Leaves hang bigger than they should. A game trail cuts inland, packed wide where something shoved the undergrowth aside. No birdsong. No insects. A boot comes down and the jungle does not answer. Stop when they choose a trail, wait, or go back down the terraces.

Wraps [[region.aruhe-the-old-gardens|The Old Gardens]] on three sides and then keeps going — tens of miles of mute canopy. Crossing toward [[region.aruhe-the-rot|The Rot]] is days on a trail, longer off it.

## Geography

Dense jungle climbing from the terrace tree line toward the interior. Footing is root, leaf-litter, and undergrowth the whole way. Sight dies at a few paces almost everywhere. The only reliable inland lines are game trails, and those trails exist because predators shoved them open.

[[creature.thornback|Thornbacks]] root the floor and leave corridors of broken brush and spent quills, each spine long enough to use as a stake. [[creature.strangler-fig|Strangler figs]] sit where those trails pinch between stone outcrops. [[creature.vine-lash|Vine-lash]] thins compared to the terraces but still hangs in the denser brush. [[creature.wolfrabbit|Wolfrabbits]] range up from the terrace warrens along the lower edge.

The land is still working. Growth here is excess, not recovery: trunks too thick, leaves the size of shields, aerial roots in curtains. What the terraces do with fruit, this band does with silence and size. Deeper in, bark at chest height is claw-scored along local circuits — a [[creature.bear-elk|Bear-Elk]]'s route on each known Quiet/Rot approach.

Rivers from [[location.aruhe-the-clear-lake|The Clear Lake]] tear valleys in the hundred-foot canopy. Those openings are [[region.aruhe-the-grasslands|The Grasslands]]: miles of eight-foot grass and sudden sky along the water. The mute dark breaks. The hope does not last. Follow a cut inland and the source is still days upslope, through [[region.aruhe-the-rot|The Rot]].

Down is [[region.aruhe-the-old-gardens|The Old Gardens]]. Inland along a trail is [[region.aruhe-the-rot|The Rot]]. A brighter seam through the trees is a river valley. Lateral travel can take a week. Off-trail is shorter on a map and longer in the living wood.

**Maze role.** The Quiet is [[region.aruhe|Aruhe]]'s first true wilderness level: a maze without dependable visual landmarks. Similar trunks, curtains of root, and sight lines that end a few paces away erase a bearing as soon as the party leaves a known line. Navigation is learned from what the living wood leaves behind, not from a distant peak. The local route choices belong here; the island-wide expedition procedure and full route mastery remain deferred to [[dungeon.aruhe-wilderness-megadungeon|Aruhe Wilderness Mega-Dungeon]].

The maze still gives readable signals. A party can compare several rather than rely on one check:

- **Game trails** are packed, direct, and fastest, but they are predator highways. Freshly crushed brush, spent [[creature.thornback|Thornback]] quills, and the direction of recent tracks tell the party which way the corridor is being used.
- **Claw marks** on chest-high bark mark territorial circuits and sometimes the crossing into [[region.aruhe-the-rot|The Rot]]. Repeated scores are a route record, not a promise that the territory is empty.
- **Scat** identifies which animal is using a corridor and how recently it passed. Mixed spoor warns that a fast line is contested rather than safe.
- **Disturbed undergrowth** shows where something large pushed through, where a hidden side path branches, or where a watcher left the trail without breaking it.
- **Bird gaze** replaces birdsong: silent birds turn their heads toward an intruder, then toward one another. The direction of their attention can reveal a watched approach, but never explains who is watching.
- **Water sound** is a difficult-to-place orientation line. A party that follows it through several cross-checks may find a valley seam and [[region.aruhe-the-grasslands|The Grasslands]], then the [[region.aruhe-the-river|River]]; a single echo can send them toward a dead cut.
- **Canopy breaks** mark sky, wind, and brighter growth over a valley. They are the best visual destination signal in the Quiet, but the break may still be days away through the folds of the land.

These signals support three materially different approaches. A **game-trail route** is quick and legible once found, but gives predators a prepared highway to the party. An **off-trail route** avoids predictable corridors and can conceal intent, but is slow through roots and brush and exposes the party to more hazards, dead ends, and lost time. A **river-seeking route** is the hardest to locate because water sound and canopy breaks do not line up cleanly; finding it opens the island's express network, while missing the seam can spend an expedition turn without moving the destination closer. The party can change routes when new evidence warrants it; none is a prescribed solution.

The Quiet's defining identity is **silence as surveillance**. There is no alarm chorus to tell the party that something has noticed them. The same silent birds, watching predators, and disturbed leaves that teach a route also report movement through the living system. Hearing-based detection remains unreliable here, but silence is not cover: the party may be observed before it knows which signal to trust.

## Climate

Still air under closed canopy. The silence is the weather. Heat sits. Sound does not carry. Dusk and dawn, [[creature.silence-moths|Silence Moths]] work the outer edge — the first tell is a deeper hole in the already-quiet air, then pale wings blotting the last light. Fire drives them off. They do not chase anyone who leaves the trees.

## Peoples

None. Cultivation never reached this canopy. [[creature.grung|Grung]] named the band from the terrace edge and would not walk it.

## Threats

> [!danger] Active Threats
> Predators close without sound. The few paces of sight line are the warning. [[creature.deer-stalker|Deer-Stalkers]] crash off a carcass, then watch from the tree line and close after dark on whoever is alone. [[creature.thornback|Thornbacks]] charge what startles them; two in one stretch fight each other first. [[creature.strangler-fig|Strangler Figs]] hang where trails funnel. [[creature.silence-moths|Silence Moths]] work the outer edge at dusk and dawn. [[creature.vine-lash|Vine Lash]] remains in the brush. A [[creature.bear-elk|Bear-Elk]] patrols the Quiet/Rot boundary.

**[[creature.bear-elk|Bear-Elk]] circuit.** Each known Quiet/Rot approach has its own scored loop. One circuit every 4 hours on that corridor — not the whole island. DC 15 Wisdom (Survival) to time it from tracks, broken undergrowth, and the chest-high claw scores. A waiting party has a 30-minute gap; crossing needs a DC 14 Dexterity (Stealth) group check. Failure: the [[creature.bear-elk|Bear-Elk]] returns to investigate. This is a learnable schedule and soft gate, not a level lock: a party can watch, wait, take another approach, or decide that a fight is worth the attention it will draw.

**Strangler-fig funnel.** DC 15 Wisdom (Perception) to spot before walking under. Failure: the fig drops on the lead creature and attempts to grapple and engulf.

**Mute close.** In this band, Perception checks that rely on hearing to notice a creature are made at disadvantage. The jungle does not give a predator away.

**[[creature.unsaid-macaw|Unsaid Macaws]] — rare.** Not resident: the band's no-birdsong silence is why the species stays out. A lone bird may briefly follow travellers in from the rim edge before turning back; if it echoes someone's unsaid thought, that is the whole encounter. Non-hostile.

**[[creature.grubnade|Grubnade]] traces.** The few insects the mute jungle still holds are [[creature.grubnade|Grubnades]] — neat caterpillar bites in giant leaves, little blackened circles on flowers, occasional scorched patches five feet across, and predators with old burn scars around the muzzle. They persist because nothing can safely eat them.

**Canopy drop.** The Quiet is the [[creature.aruhe-crown-squid|Crown Squid]]'s prime habitat — where the species becomes a major threat. DC 14 Wisdom (Survival) reads that something extremely large uses the upper canopy; DC 17 identifies the unmistakable hunting signs; DC 20 Wisdom (Perception) or passive Perception 20 finds a motionless adult before it attacks.

**[[creature.snakewood|Snakewood]] strike.** The sealed, silent canopy is also prime habitat for the [[creature.snakewood|Snakewood]] — an overhead predator that waits for one body to pass beneath its strike zone and reels it upward. Its territory reads in absence: no carcasses, only things that fell off carcasses — a snapped antler, a single boot — because everything edible went upward. DC 13 Nature (different-looking vines are shoots of the same plant), DC 14 Survival (trails avoid certain branches), DC 15 Perception (vines under tension), DC 16 Nature (parallel compression scars) read its tells.

**[[hazard.stillbloom|Stillbloom]] patch.** On game trails and sunbreaks at the Quiet → Rot transition, wide root mats classify vibration instead of touch: rain, a falling leaf, a mouse, a careful footstep mean nothing; a Small or larger creature bolting, dashing, jumping, or falling means fire. DC 14 Wisdom (Perception) spots the launch-spines, punctured carcasses, and strange scavenger paths. The tell: small scavengers walk between the stems with exaggerated slowness and freeze when anything larger approaches — slow movement is the safe rule.

**Forbidden fruit.** The Quiet grows [[item.stonepear|Stonepear]] (resistance to all damage — the fruit animals eat before fighting) and [[item.ghost-plum|Ghost Plum]] (invisibility) as common and uncommon finds, with [[item.giants-guava|Giant's Guava]] ranging in from the terrace edge. All of them work exactly as advertised.

## Landmarks

- **Terrace tree line** — where cut stone ends and true jungle starts. Last place a voice from the beach might still carry.
- **Game trails** — only reliable inland lines; also hunting corridors. Packed wide by [[creature.thornback|thornbacks]]; spent quills mark recent rooting.
- **Strangler-fig gaps** — where a trail pinches between stone. One tree per pinch. Bones in the bark.
- **Quill fields** — floor littered with javelin-length spines around a [[creature.thornback|thornback]] territory.
- **Quiet/Rot boundary** — claw-scored trunks at chest height along the [[creature.bear-elk|Bear-Elk]]'s circuit. Sap on the bark glows faintly after dark.
- **Cave mouths** — root wells and old skylights swallowed by vegetation lead down to [[region.aruhe-the-cave|The Cave]]. [[location.aruhe-the-daylight-hole|The Daylight Hole]] is a collapsed skylight here: a seventy-foot circle of canopy, an emergency exit from below.
- **Valley seams** — a brighter cut through the mute canopy. River noise after silence. Those openings are [[region.aruhe-the-grasslands|The Grasslands]].

> [!secret]- Secrets
> [[creature.grung|Grung]] never surveyed this band. The name is what reached them before they left. The silence is not empty jungle — birds and insects are gone because the island's hyper-fauna ate the small noise first, and the [[creature.blight|Druid Lich]]'s garden has no use for a warning chorus.
