# The River

The rivers that drain [[location.aruhe-the-clear-lake|The Clear Lake]] cut [[region.aruhe-the-grasslands|The Grasslands]] open. They are the only clear headings in [[region.aruhe|Aruhe]] — loud, fast, and honest. Follow any stream uphill and it ends at the lake. The water is not a trap. The water is the best road through the island.

Something else already owns it.

![[assets/places/aruhe-the-river.jpeg]]

## Overview

> [!narration] Overview
> The jungle breaks on water. Green-blue current cuts through eight-foot grass on one bank and hundred-foot roots on the other. The water is cool enough that after days in [[region.aruhe|Aruhe]] it feels almost obscenely good. Pale limestone shows beneath ten feet of green-blue water. Long ribbons of submerged grass stream downstream. Fallen trees lie visible on the bottom. Sunlight flashes off schools of little silver fish. There is no visual sign that anything is wrong — no bones, no blood, no evil mist. The river is the prettiest place on the island. Stop when they step in, watch the bank, or keep walking the grass.

## Geography

**Extent.** Multiple rivers drain [[location.aruhe-the-clear-lake|The Clear Lake]] eastward through [[region.aruhe-the-rot|The Rot]] and [[region.aruhe-the-quiet|The Quiet]], opening [[region.aruhe-the-grasslands|The Grasslands]] wherever the current prevents canopy closure. The rivers are not a single waterway — they braid, split, and rejoin. But every channel leads back to the lake.

**Lateral artery.** The River is not another concentric band. Its channels cut laterally across the bands: a party can leave [[region.aruhe-the-quiet|The Quiet]] or [[region.aruhe-the-grasslands|The Grasslands]], cross cuts in [[region.aruhe-the-rot|The Rot]], and follow water uphill toward [[location.aruhe-the-clear-lake|The Clear Lake]]. It is the island's express route. A comparable crossing through jungle takes days; a navigable reach by water compresses that distance to hours.

**Fast or exhausting.** Water travel is faster, cooler, and easier to navigate than forcing through jungle or grass, but the express route is dangerous: current, cataracts, deep pools, sudden rapids, and [[creature.aruhe-river-otter|River Otter]] territory all belong to it. Walking the banks is slower — often days or several exhausting Expedition Turns for the same cross-band movement — but lets a party avoid open water and choose between grassland visibility and jungle cover. The grassland bank exposes travelers to [[region.aruhe-the-grasslands|The Grasslands]] predators; the jungle bank trades cover for difficult footing and vertical threats. This is a meaningful fast-dangerous versus slow-exhausting choice, not a safe route beside an unsafe one; portage and bank changes remain available when the river's evidence warrants them.

And every fifty yards, another place where the water could take you.

**Hydrology.** [[location.aruhe-the-long-reach|The Long Reach]] is the signature stretch: two to three miles where the river broadens to 150–400 feet, slows, and clears to fifteen feet of visibility over white limestone. Upstream and downstream, the character changes — cataracts where stone shoulders force enormous current, deep pools that swallow sound, rapids that appear without warning. The river does not stay gentle.

**Seasonal.** Flood stage widens [[region.aruhe-the-grasslands|The Grasslands]], drowns the jungle margin, and makes the current lethal. Dry stage exposes limestone shelves, concentrates the otters, and makes the water *too* clear — you see them watching from farther away.

**Durable access.** Seeing a channel gives a party a heading, not ownership of a route. Recording its turns, identifying safe landings and portages, learning current and seasonal signs, and finding a defensible camp or refuge turns a first crossing into durable River access. On a later return, a mastered reach preserves the hours-scale crossing and lets the party choose when to accept otter exposure instead of re-solving the approach; it does not make rapids gentle or [[creature.aruhe-river-otter|The Family]] safe. The island-wide Expedition Turn and route-state procedure remain on [[region.aruhe|Aruhe]] and the deferred [[dungeon.aruhe-wilderness-megadungeon|Aruhe Wilderness Mega-Dungeon]]; this page owns the River-specific discoveries and access consequences.

## Climate

Sudden glare after closed canopy (same as [[region.aruhe-the-grasslands|The Grasslands]]). The water is loud enough to cover a step, a breath, a charge until it is close. Mist hangs at dawn and dusk. Night does not empty the river. It only takes the sky back.

## Peoples

None live here. [[faction.grung-clans|Grung Clans]] name the rivers from the terrace edge and turn back. The nearest settlement is [[region.karath|Karath]], a country away.

## Threats

> [!danger] Active Threats
> The water is not a trap. The water is the best road. **[[creature.aruhe-river-otter|River Otters]] own it.** They are magnificent — twelve feet nose to [[region.the-tail|Tail]], glossy coats, clear eyes, fat pups. They hold hands while drifting. They groom. They whistle. They roll in the shallows. Their affection ends at [[creature.aruhe-river-otter|The Family]] boundary. Anything entering the water is participating. Anything taken from the river belongs to [[creature.aruhe-river-otter|The Family]]. Anything that responds to play teaches them a new game.
>
> [[creature.deer-stalker|Deer-Stalkers]] and [[creature.terror-bird|Terror-Birds]] work the grassland bank. [[creature.wolfrabbit|Wolfrabbits]] hunt the jungle margin at dawn and dusk. The jungle rim drops things that wait in roots.
>
> **The switch.** Harm a family member and the play stops. Whistles cease. Splashing ceases. Every visible otter disappears. Ten seconds of silence. Then one person is hit from below. The rest never see more than wakes. Five minutes later the pups are wrestling again.

**Grassland bank.** Eight-foot blades. Difficult terrain. Heavily obscured beyond 10 feet. [[creature.terror-bird|Terror-Birds]] wait in the rim shade and charge anything in the light. [[creature.deer-stalker|Deer-Stalkers]] stand in the grass and do not lean with the wind.

**Jungle bank.** Root curtains, difficult terrain, vertical drops. Things hang in the overhang and drop on what passes below. A [[creature.aruhe-crown-squid|Crown Squid]] hunts from the jungle bank but rarely extends a grabber over deep water — open water is otter territory. The canopy rim is a hunting post.

**Water.** Fastest travel. Cool. Clear lines of sight. **[[creature.aruhe-river-otter|River Otters]] treat anything in the water as enrichment.** The full social puzzle and escalation belong to [[location.aruhe-the-long-reach|The Long Reach]]; this region owns the broad choice to enter their express route and the durable consequences of learning or avoiding it.

**[[creature.unsaid-macaw|Unsaid Macaws]].** River-valley margins — a secondary habitat. A macaw works the bank and occasionally follows travelers; it repeats the unsaid surface thoughts of whoever is within 30 ft., in their own voice, socially salient ones only. Never tactical information, never a lie detector. Non-hostile.

**Forbidden fruit.** [[item.breathmelon|Breathmelon fruit]] grows abundant along the river margins — the fruit that lets a party breathe water and swim as fast as they walk, placed exactly where the otters drag prey underwater. It works exactly as advertised.

## Landmarks

- **[[location.aruhe-the-long-reach|The Long Reach]]** — two to three miles of broad, slow, gin-clear water over white limestone. The signature stretch. Where the otter family plays.
- **The cataracts** — where the gorgeous river suddenly pours between stone shoulders into something enormously stronger than it looked ten minutes earlier. Portage required.
- **The deep pools** — stretches of Lower Congo hydrology: exceptionally deep, powerful, complex currents. Sound vanishes.
- **The outlet fans** — where rivers leave the lake and cut downhill. Obvious approach, not a gate.
- **The jungle rim** — hundred-foot roots hanging over the water. Vertical threat.
- **The lateral seams** — places where a channel leaves one band and reappears in another, making the River a learned cross-band connector rather than a destination inside one band.

> [!secret]- Secrets
> The river's clarity is not natural. The [[creature.aruhe-river-otter|River Otters]] engineer it: they cull the grazers that would muddy it, fell trees that would shade it, patrol the margins. The beauty *is* their garden. A druid or [[character-option.ranger|Ranger]] reading the water finds: **unnatural clarity — water too clear, biomass too concentrated, silence where insects should be. This river is tended.**
>
> Their gardening leaves physical evidence even before anyone sees an otter: shade trees cut or dragged clear of the banks, grazers absent where their spoor should churn the shallows, concentrated fish and submerged growth, and unnaturally quiet insect life. Following those signs can reveal a tended reach, a likely landing, or a boundary to avoid; it does not reveal [[creature.aruhe-river-otter|The Family]]'s response to being approached.
>
> The Death Bloom perfected the otter according to a worldview in which nothing outside [[creature.aruhe-river-otter|The Family]] deserves consideration. Otter curiosity became sadism without the concept of sadism. Otter family love became: **Everything that is not us is either food, threat, or enrichment.**
>
> The otters are what the river monsters are afraid of. They play tug-of-war with [[creature.deer-stalker|Deer-Stalker]] heads. The [[creature.deer-stalker|Deer-Stalker]] canonically avoids larger fauna rather than contesting them.
