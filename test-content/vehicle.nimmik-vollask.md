> [!narration] Overview
> The *Nimmik Vollask* rides bright orange against grey water, boxy from bow to stern, two-foot Gnomish letters down both sides. A halfling woman waits at the starboard rail behind a folding table, ledger open, coffee pot steaming. Below the main hatch, gnomish voices count a rhythm. A cargo crane swings empty over the gap. Tar and frying meatballs from the hold. She closes the ledger. Chalk already in hand.

Cargo brig, tier 2, unmoored. Sixty gnomes live aboard; the hull was built for cargo, then fitted to gnome scale. It rocks in any wind. Shipbuilders call the centre of gravity "theoretical." Nobody aboard who speaks Gnomish will say what *Nimmik Vollask* means.

**Service.** Flag Cotter Foss at the rail. Name cargo. Pay the fee. Coffee and gnomish meatballs come with it. Medium-or-larger customers never board. Orders pack below; a bundle shoots the hatch; voices call the manifest across the gap; Cotter confirms by ear; the crane throws the bundle into your hold. Misses get logged with an apology.

**Stock.** Rope, canvas, food, water, tar, pitch, oakum, block and tackle, tools, lanterns, oil, candles, healer's kits, antitoxin. Alchemist's fire by request. Workshop goods: Depot Signal Whistle (5 gp), Gnomish Splicing Fid (8 gp), Self-Heating Tar Pot (12 gp), Vollask Meatball Tins (1 gp), Gnomish Rigging Blocks (15 gp). A [[item.stormwork-quiver|Stormwork Quiver]] (150 gp, attunement) sits behind the normal goods. Those item pages are unminted.

**Layout.** Weather deck gnome-scaled. Between-decks 4.5 ft. Hatches and corridors bar Medium creatures at any posture. Lower hold reached only through those passages. No Medium creature has ever gone below.

Four years on the [[region.central-strait|Central Strait]]. Never sunk. Gnomes say skill. Cotter says luck.
