# The Great Bore

The signature location of [[region.aruhe-the-cave|The Cave]]: a nearly straight lava tube perhaps **three miles long, 25–40 feet across, gently sloping inland**. No junctions. No real cover. Easy walking. A fantastic shortcut — the party can cover in a few hours what would take a day or more through the jungle above.

It is [[region.aruhe-the-cave|The Cave]] equivalent of [[region.aruhe-the-river|The River]]: **the best road.** And something else already owns it.

> [!narration] Arrival
> The tube ahead does not bend. It runs on in a straight line, high enough that the torchlight gives out before the far wall does. The floor is polished almost smooth by repeated passage — not by water. Paired scratches run along both walls at regular height. Easy walking. No junctions. No cover. A shortcut that feels like a gift.

## Senses

- **Signature:** Polished smoothness underfoot where the rest of [[region.aruhe-the-cave|The Cave]] is rubble and scrambling. The floor has been worn flat by something passing — many times.
- **Footing:** Even, easy walking over the whole length.

## Features

- **The polished floor:**
  - **Obvious:** Almost smooth, worn by repeated passage.
  - **Reveals:** Something uses this tube as its road. What wears stone flat travels here often, and has for a long time.
  - **Logic:** The Bore is the straightest, easiest route in [[region.aruhe-the-cave|The Cave]] — the best road draws the thing that dominates the environment.
  - **Consequence:** The shortcut is fast; the price is exposure.

- **Paired scratches along the walls:**
  - **Obvious:** Regular-height paired gouges along both walls.
  - **Reveals:** Midway through the encounter the marks suddenly make sense — they're not scratches from something passing beside you. **They're where the legs touch when it runs.**
  - **Logic:** The [[creature.blackrail|Blackrail]] runs this tube, legs catching both walls.
  - **Consequence:** Reading the marks late is the point. They are the tell for what owns the Bore.

- **The collapsed side blister:**
  - **Obvious:** An old collapsed side blister **120 feet away** when the [[creature.blackrail|Blackrail]] comes.
  - **Reveals:** Shelter — if reachable.
  - **Logic:** The [[creature.blackrail|Blackrail]] cannot turn sharply at full speed and cannot fit everywhere.
  - **Consequence:** The encounter question becomes very simple: **can they reach it before the [[creature.blackrail|Blackrail]] reaches them?**

## Hazards

> [!danger] Active Hazards
> **The [[creature.blackrail|Blackrail]]'s road.** Midway through the Bore: *Tick. Five seconds. Tick. Four. Tick. Three.* Dust sifts from the ceiling. The marks along the walls make sense. Then the [[creature.blackrail|Blackrail]] comes down the tube — a much better introduction than rolling initiative immediately. ^[inferred — the source describes the encounter as designed to precede initiative]

## Inhabitants

- [[creature.blackrail|The Blackrail]] — the only real resident. The floor polish and wall scratches are its spoor.

## Secrets

> [!secret]- Hidden Details
> The Great Bore is [[region.aruhe-the-cave|The Cave]]'s version of the [[region.aruhe-the-river|River]]'s structural trick: the safest, easiest route is also the territory of the thing that dominates that environment.

## If Left Alone

The tube stays open and easy; the [[creature.blackrail|Blackrail]] keeps using it, and the floor keeps polishing. No named imminent change.
