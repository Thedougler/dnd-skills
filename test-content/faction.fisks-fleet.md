# Fisk's Fleet

> [!narration] Overview
> Five ships, five captains, one commission — all lost to [[deity.umberlee|Umberlee]]'s wrath in under a minute.

## Overview

A five-ship privateer fleet sponsored by the [[faction.chain-council|Chain Council]], with [[pc.delmar-fisk|Delmar Fisk]] commanding as Rear Admiral. The fleet existed because six crewmates refused to transport enslaved people; the mutiny left them with no ship, no patron, and no prospects — they stayed together anyway, working through every hard vessel and every failure until Fisk had ships of his own to give them.

[[faction.chain-council|the Council]] commission was a mask. The [[faction.tessarine-concordat|Tessarine Concordat]] wanted the [[item.pearl-of-souls|Pearl of Souls]] kept away from [[npc.aldric-drave|Aldric Drave]], who they believe could use it to expand his abilities beyond his own bloodline. [[faction.tessarine-concordat|the Concordat]] used the [[faction.chain-council|Chain Council]] to have Fisk's Fleet steal it before [[npc.aldric-drave|Drave]] could. [[faction.chain-council|the Council]] itself believed it was protecting [[region.midchain|Midchain]] waters from [[deity.umberlee|Umberlee]]'s wrath, and never knew the real reason it was paying for the theft.

The fleet's one commission is already complete and the fleet itself destroyed — no ongoing Front lives on this page. The live consequence threads run on [[faction.chain-council|Chain Council]] ([[situation.denial|Denial]], [[situation.tessarine-containment|Tessarine Containment]], [[situation.midchain-anger|Midchain Anger]]) and [[faction.waveservants|Waveservants]] ([[situation.the-red-wake|The Red Wake]]), under each page's own Goals & Fronts.

## Goals

- **Stated goal:** Patrol [[region.midchain|Midchain]] waters as a [[faction.chain-council|Chain Council]] privateer fleet; suppress piracy and protect commerce.
- **True goal:** Steal the [[item.pearl-of-souls|Pearl of Souls]] from a [[faction.waveservants|Waveservant]] shrine on behalf of the [[faction.tessarine-concordat|Tessarine Concordat]] (via the [[faction.chain-council|Chain Council]] as a cut-out).
- **Immediate objective:** None — the fleet was destroyed hours after completing the theft.

## Methods

The fleet operated as a disciplined privateer unit: coordinated five-ship formations, dedicated roles per vessel (flagship, scout, heavy, survey, combat), and a shared history that made communication nearly tacit. The theft operation used a distraction team at the shrine's main entrance (sustained bombardment, spell suppression, trap triggering) while a scout team mapped the reef and a second team entered the shrine's sub-surface passage.

> [!tip] Running This Faction
> The fleet is gone. What remains are five souls inside one man, five wrecks on [[region.shelfworks|The Shelfworks]], and a political wound the [[faction.chain-council|Chain Council]] cannot close. When the party encounters echoes of the fleet — [[pc.delmar-fisk|Delmar]]'s Mortis voices, [[vehicle.red-lady|The Red Lady]]'s wreck, the Pearl's signal — play the discipline and the shared history. These five chose each other before they had ships.

### Captain Voice Triggers (Mortis Manifestation)

Each captain's voice surfaces in [[pc.delmar-fisk|Delmar]] under specific conditions:

- **[[npc.rael-corven|Rael Corven]]** — Commanding, protective. Surfaces when an ally drops to 0 HP within 30 ft, or at the sight of a woman in a dark red dress (searches her face for someone lost).
- **[[npc.maren|Maren]]** — Sassy, contemptuous of authority. Surfaces when [[pc.delmar-fisk|Delmar]] is denied entry/passage blocked, or at the sight of a Navy vessel/insignia.
- **[[npc.detto-kalash|Detto Kalash]]** — Blunt, physical. Surfaces when [[pc.delmar-fisk|Delmar]] fails a Con save, is knocked prone/grappled, or at the sight of a white horse (goes still, says nothing).
- **[[npc.sienne-orre|Sienne Orre]]** — Methodical, careful. Surfaces when [[pc.delmar-fisk|Delmar]] is targeted by a 2nd-level+ spell or fails an Int save, or at the sight of a green mermaid sign/figurehead (recognizes it, asks about the place).
- **[[npc.bastian-ookage|Bastian Ookage]]** — Theatrical, warm, confrontational. Surfaces when an enemy's Deception/Persuasion succeeds against [[pc.delmar-fisk|Delmar]], when [[pc.delmar-fisk|Delmar]] witnesses coercion of the powerless, or at the sight of a black jackdaw (surfaces warm, may reach for the bird).

**Soul-Anchor:** [[pc.delmar-fisk|Delmar]]'s soul-anchor is a brass flagon — the sight or touch of one, while any captain is surfaced, brings him back. Tied to the night after the mutiny when the five stranded crewmates passed one flagon around: the moment before the fleet existed.

Succession: None. The fleet died with the ships.

## What Happened in the Water

Fog closed in thick enough that the sea itself went invisible, surrounding all five ships at once. A [[character-option.rogue|Rogue]] trench (the [[region.drowned-maw|Drowned Maw]]) opened beneath them — all five plunged 80 feet down before the walls closed over them. [[pc.crissdalynn-khinriss|Crissdalynn Khinriss]] dove from the *[[vehicle.red-lady|Red Lady]]*'s crow's nest, grabbed Fisk, and flew straight back up; she had risen only 50 feet when the water closed, and momentum and buoyancy carried them both the rest of the way to the surface.

The [[item.pearl-of-souls|Pearl of Souls]], still aboard the *[[vehicle.red-lady|Red Lady]]*, activated at the moment of sinking and collected the souls of all five ships' crews. [[deity.umberlee|Umberlee]] pressed the five captains' souls into Fisk separately, as punishment and leverage — Fisk carries the five captains, the Pearl holds the crews.

[[deity.umberlee|Umberlee]]'s own words to [[pc.delmar-fisk|Delmar]] stayed to "what is Mine" and "the souls you stole." The party was told only that he "carries souls [[deity.umberlee|Umberlee]] claims" and that a Pearl must be retrieved — never the five-captains story.
