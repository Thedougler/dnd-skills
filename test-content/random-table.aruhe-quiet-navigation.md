# Quiet Navigation Table

## Table

**Roll:** `d8`

| Roll | Result |
|------|--------|
| 1 | **Bird gaze without birdsong.** Speak: "Silent birds turn toward the party, then toward a side trail." Follow the gaze: advantage on the next Survival check. Ignore it: next predator check gains surprise risk. |
| 2 | **[[creature.thornback\|Thornback]] corridor.** Speak: "Spent quills and plowed brush mark a corridor something uses." DC 13 Survival tells if it is fresh. Failure puts the party in the corridor when the owner returns. |
| 3 | **Strangler-fig pinch.** Speak: "The trail pinches between stone and bone-barked roots." DC 15 Perception spots the [[creature.strangler-fig\|Strangler Fig]] before it drops. Failure: lead creature grappled. |
| 4 | **Water lies from two directions.** Speak: "A brighter seam and a water sound do not agree." The seam leads toward [[region.aruhe-the-grasslands\|The Grasslands]]. The sound leads to a dead cut unless DC 15 Survival. |
| 5 | **[[creature.deer-stalker\|Deer-Stalker]] leaves dinner.** Speak: "Something crashes off a carcass and watches from the green." If they camp or separate before the next success, it returns after dark. |
| 6 | **[[creature.snakewood\|Snakewood]] absence.** Speak: "The path is too clean. A snapped antler and one boot lie under hanging vines." DC 15 Perception or DC 16 Nature reads the strike zone. |
| 7 | **Stillbloom lesson.** Speak: "Small scavengers freeze inside a root mat and do not finish the freeze." DC 14 Perception spots [[hazard.stillbloom\|Stillbloom]] launch-spines. Slow crossing is safe. Running, jumping, or falling fires the patch. |
| 8 | **Cave breath.** Speak: "Cool air rises from a root well." Possible [[region.aruhe-the-cave\|Cave]] mouth. Mark one route success, or descend and switch to [[random-table.aruhe-cave-navigation\|The Cave Navigation Table]]. |

> [!tip] Running This Table
> Use one 30-minute Exploration Turn for game trails, off-trail travel, river-seeking, or return-route marks in [[region.aruhe-the-quiet|The Quiet]]. Roll after a failed navigation check.

## Usage Notes

- **Navigation DC:** DC 14 Wisdom (Survival). Use DC 15 for river-seeking or off-trail travel in rain or dusk.
- **Progress:** Three successes before two failures finds the chosen line: deeper trail, valley seam, return mark, or safe withdrawal.
- **Mapping:** Five total successes maps a route between the tree line and one known destination.
- **Failure:** On each failed 30-minute turn, roll this table. Failure should change position or threat, not say only that the party is lost.
- **Sound:** Hearing-based Perception remains unreliable. Use sight, tracks, bird gaze, scat, and disturbed growth.

## Variants

- At dawn or dusk, replace result 1 with [[creature.silence-moths|Silence Moths]] crossing the outer canopy.
