# Whisper Fig

> [!narration] Appearance
> A small green fig, unremarkable until it is split — the flesh inside is threaded with pale veins like the inside of a nerve, and the air around the cut goes briefly quiet.

## Properties

- **Type:** Fruit (consumable)
- **Rarity:** Rare
- **Attunement:** No
- Consuming takes an Action. Gain the effects of *detect thoughts* for 10 minutes (DC 13), with no concentration required.

## Biology

Neurologically active plant metabolites. The [[creature.blight|Blight]] exaggerated the fig's own signalling chemistry — compounds plants already use to speak to each other — until they speak to animal minds instead.

## Identification

DC 13 Nature or Medicine (1 minute): broad physiological function — a fruit that reads or alters thought. DC 15: exact effect. *Identify* spell: exact effect automatically.

## Placement

The general rule applies: place the fruit immediately before or beside the problem it solves — a mystery to read, a mind to pry open. Grows at the edge of [[region.aruhe-the-quiet|The Quiet]] where it meets [[region.aruhe-the-rot|The Rot]].

## Abundance

Uncommon.
