# Flood Pulse

Surface rain can enter [[region.aruhe-the-cave|The Cave]] through skylights and cracks far uphill. The party doesn't necessarily know it is raining. Not every flood fills the entire cave — most are **violent pulses traveling through the lowest channels**.

## Description

> [!narration] Player-facing description — read or adapt at the table.
> The trickle beside your boots suddenly reverses direction around a stone. Then another thin stream appears from a crack that was dry before. The air coming downhill gets colder. One minute later, the pulse arrives.

## Trigger

Surface rain entering through skylights and cracks far uphill. The pulse arrives **one minute** after the tell.

## Effect

> [!danger] What happens when the hazard triggers.

In a [[vehicle.the-narrow|Narrow]] active drainage tube, a creature makes a **DC 15 Strength saving throw**. On failure: **10 (3d6) bludgeoning damage**, knocked prone, and **swept 40 feet downstream**. On success: half damage and hold position.

A creature swept into a wall pocket or grate of roots may become **restrained**, with an escape DC of **14 Athletics or Acrobatics**.

## Detection

The tell precedes the pulse by a minute: the trickle beside the boots reverses direction around a stone; another thin stream appears from a crack that was dry before; the air coming downhill gets colder. No check is stated — the tell is observable.

## Counterplay

> [!tip] Running this hazard — how the party can mitigate, avoid, or neutralize it.

The minute between tell and pulse is the window: get out of the lowest channels, brace, or find high ground. The strongest characters hold position on a success; the swept are carried downstream until they can grab hold.

## Escalation

> [!secret]- What happens if the hazard is ignored or interacted with repeatedly — collapsed by default.

Source is silent. Most floods are violent pulses through the lowest channels; not every flood fills the entire cave.
