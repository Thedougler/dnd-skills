# Cave Bat

Huge by ordinary standards but not giant-monster huge: **3–4 foot wingspans**. The base of [[region.aruhe-the-cave|The Cave]]'s food web — bats → guano → decomposers → cave fauna — and a primary food for the [[creature.blackrail|Blackrail]].

## Overview

> [!narration] Player-facing description — read or adapt at the table.
Source is silent.

## Ecology

They leave at dusk through skylights and feed over [[region.aruhe|Aruhe]]'s canopy. Bats are [[region.aruhe-the-cave|The Cave]]'s chief imported energy: guano feeds the decomposers and cave fauna that the rest of the ecosystem — up to the [[creature.blackrail|Blackrail]] — depends on.

## Behavior

A colony suddenly taking flight is an excellent signal: **something disturbed them farther inside.** The party can read bat behavior as an early-warning system.

## Habitat

Roosting in [[region.aruhe-the-cave|The Cave]], leaving at dusk through skylights; feeding over the surface canopy of [[region.aruhe|Aruhe]] at night.
